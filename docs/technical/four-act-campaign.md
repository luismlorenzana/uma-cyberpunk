Use stable private catalog IDs for an Uma, General, each lieutenant, duo, home, capital, ship, expedition, encounter and Seraine. Keep the same actor across portrait roles, locations, aliases, outfits and both opposing and allied unit slots. Verify uniqueness of actor IDs, idempotency of gains, role-qualified portraits and expected battle profiles. Resolve wardrobe by approved outfit and combat sprites by identity, role, form and any reviewed enemy encounter override. The frozen fitting governs tactics. Keep the original outfit ID and deterministic mapping for guest crew records. Avoid a second copy of each actor in a global roster when they join an expedition.

Map the private authoring content into typed campaign data. A named personal chain selects one required Uma and optional supporting participants. A fixed duo links two distinct Umas in the same system, their two distinct Generals, both homes and exactly six unique lieutenants. Validate the home, actor and lieutenant membership at build time. Every capital contributes exactly one result to the global gate. A route's named Seraine target must be a Herald or Echo. Queen missions are admitted by the final act only. Scope each encounter by act and side, including mission spawning, supports and reinforcements. Build a dependency report with each prerequisite, grant, presentation slot and the owner's approval state.

Use the existing quest primitives and story player where they express the rule. Add typed blocks or a successor book format for the fixed duo, capital, expedition and finale. The compiler derives all gate rows, references and state projections from the same source. Simulate legal reachability from an empty save in each act. Find dependency cycles, required actors who become permanently unavailable, resource deadlocks, incomplete forks and impossible unlocks. Verify the entire content graph in one run. The four-act catalog supports 4,096 quests, 256 command encounters, 512 ground encounters and 2,048 documents. M4 fits 2,521 quests, 183 command encounters, 356 ground encounters and 1,234 documents. The publisher keeps each document’s hash and assembles separately hashed transport pages. The browser loads 31 pages and the independent campaign document. ## State and save

| Record | Stable ownership and transitions |
| --- | --- |
| Arc progress | One record per Uma. Recruitment request, sign-on and actual quarters assignment, personal quests, custody result and individual rehabilitation steps. |
| Actor status | One identity per actor. General progresses through hostile, captured, in rehabilitation, allied resident and cleared for Act III. A lieutenant can be hostile, scattered, contacted, returned and assigned. Use explicit content decisions where an actor can skip or revisit a stage. |
| Residence and access | Derive reachable cabin, base or expedition locations from residence. Check rehab, custody, leave, fatigue, away missions and facing ownership independently when applying action eligibility. A move out of quarters preserves relationship progress. |
| Capital and home | Each home has investigation and service results. Each base records establishment, projects and effective benefits. Each capital stores the committed liberation result for its system. |

| Expedition | Launch receipt, duo and ship IDs, roster, status, departure allocation, cargo and consumables, unlocked route, current stage and outcome. Keep one active player mission in the initial implementation. Define return, recovery and resumption at chapter and battle boundaries. |

## Catalog and review receipts

The optional `fourAct` campaign field is a versioned registry parsed by the same book loader as quests and scenes. Version 1 binds `systems[].capital` to a playable operation and its civic settlement. `arcs[]` match one recruit to an authored home, a General, three distinct lieutenants and, for complete routes, the nonlethal operation and custody quest. `duos[]` bind two same-system leads and separately identified returns. Production books require all 50 arcs, 25 fixed duos and five capital outcomes. An older local opening draft may omit the registry.
A capital route can bind five eligible bases and proof flags to the existing capital quest chain. Once the proof
scene is seen, that same branch supplies the ready and naming scenes and the capital squad. The route uses the
existing scene history and flags. Books with the original capital fields still load.

A personal route starts at the shared premise and may depend on earlier steps in its own chain. Each battle chapter can field the named lead alone. The four-act runtime uses the quest-to-lead index for availability and deployment. Existing save records keep the completed encounter and played custody scene, which together make custody and scattering visible on the roster. The opening review restores either seen variant of a settled dialogue after reload.

Use `pnpm campaign inspect --four-act --out <release-root>` to record the selected manifest hash, byte counts, routes, bound scenes, environment, portraits, sprites and rehabilitation, lieutenant and capital coverage. An optional release slug reads an immutable staged candidate. Inspect and select share the same compiled route and payload checks. Selection and rollback copy the exact manifest bytes into the local pointer. Keep output private with the Story and Art receipts.

## Eligibility and assignment

Centralize policy for ordinary duty, mining/operations, engineering, crafting, bridge stations, encounter support, tactics, private visits, shared residence and expeditions. Both UI and action handlers must enforce it. Return a reason and a next action for contingent eligibility. Allow an authored required Uma to return from a conflicting assignment or receive a viable starter route. Restore drafted units after cancellation or withdrawal according to the shared sortie contract. Track one exclusive operational assignment per person at a time. Keep optional civilians and Boss Ally advisers as story cast with narrow access. In Act II exclude Boss Allies from engineering, resource operations, bridge combat and tactics, including via bench, station auto-fill, or saved presets. A returned lieutenant eligible to move into Act III still requires her own unit role, canonical identity and legal equipment. Only the ten declared members enter an Act III battle. General and lieutenant role checks use the actor's current clearance, never an outfit name or presence in the story.

## Solo recovery, supply and base operations

Make every personal route winnable with its named Uma as the only required recruit. Cover deterministic loadout choice, issued gear, minimum support when a map needs it, starter navigation, accessible resupply and appropriate battle objectives. Use genuine bridge and ground encounters. Support recall and relief in the campaign itself with visible costs; protect progress when a depot cannot replenish immediately. Keep optional interactions, duties, mining, workshop and planet management connected to the same state. Model mining and salvage as authored jobs or operations that feed existing resource handling. Evaluate a larger mining system only if an approved mission requires its own mechanics.

Support base discovery, establishment, development, civilian resupply and capital planning. Separate port stock, crew cargo and wallet gains. Record committed receipts, funding, time, yield and unlocks. Keep contribution caps and upkeep coherent if several homes share a port. At capital settlement grant civic change and reliable route availability once, including if the player reloads the debrief. Never let repeated retries mint supplies or grant new personnel twice. Use the existing distinction between authored actions and successful battle outcomes.

## Expedition ships and command

Use one ship registry and one rules path with ship-scoped loadout, roster, resources and presentation. Every expedition gets a persistent ship identity and active-crew context. Support Home Ship, Prepare, Launch, Expedition operations, Return and Debrief. Specify offline behavior, resumption, interruption at a released port, repeat visits, crew return and cargo rounding. Initial reference policy allows one active expedition and lets subsequent ready duos use a shared hull template. Preserve route progress if a player returns at an authored checkpoint. No second full ship construction system is required to deliver the smaller ships.

Freeze ship stats, crew roles, loadouts, objective, costs, enemy orders, cinematic context and declared support at launch. Battle retries reuse the frozen command input. Four stations can remain while other crew provide named dialogue or mission benefits. Missing art must still show which ship or crew is acting and convey the actual result. Distinguish departure, captured target, retreat, failure, return and settlement. Deployed crew stay available for required story beats only through the operation's own context.

The four-act catalog accepts an expedition ship ID and six lieutenant role entries on each fixed duo. It derives the two leads and their two Generals from the same identity register and validates exactly two lieutenants in each role family. Launch readiness counts both saved General clearance scenes separately from allied residence, five settled capitals and six returned lieutenants. The other duos’ optional lieutenant returns stay independent. The launch also checks both leads’ presence, both Generals’ clearance, the staging berth and available cargo.

An authored expedition route declares a launch scene, ordered quest stops, return scene and upfront fuel and provisions. Shared transit runs once for the first active expedition and ends with the saved Gate flag. The local supply ledger regenerates at least two fuel and six provisions above local upkeep per berth and night in M4. The first voyage starts with five fuel and eight provisions on the expedition hull. A version 20 save freezes the crew, ship IDs and remaining supplies, while completed quests determine the current chapter. The ship command snapshot freezes the operation, presentation, crew and as-launched stats. Its encounter charge comes from the expedition hull. A retry replays that snapshot without another charge. The final return transfers unused cargo back once within the Homestretch’s stock limit. Older save versions start with no active expedition.

A four-act quest can declare `requiresAny` to accept one completed prerequisite in addition to its ordinary `after` and `requires` gates. Use this for the distinct local proofs that can open one capital. The verifier checks all branches for missing quests and cycles. It treats only shared ancestors as guaranteed downstream.

The first Arclight candidate imports the private authored bindings. Meeting-order choices are committed when the earlier scene finishes because the save keeps seen scene IDs sorted. The receiver, clock relay and gantry use exact objectives. Completion requires reaching the marked tile or using the terminal. The ship assaults that call for an emitter to be disabled also require that result before victory. Civic alloy production opens at the saved handover.

## Ten-unit ground battles

Ordinary saved squads have four members. Existing map schema 1 reserves five friendly tiles for ordinary squads and guests, and ground rules 1–6 preserve their original inputs. The expedition reducer pairs map schema 2 with rules 7. It requires ten occupied friendly spawns and uses the existing enemy cap of eight. The save parser checks that an expedition battle has ten distinct squad entries and spawn slots, and resume replays the versioned geometry, rigs and actions. Forecast and board playback consume the frozen battle. The campaign Hangar still uses its ordinary slots. The Act III deploy flow uses the frozen expedition formation. Umas carry mid-range rifles and decks, Generals carry great blades, and each lieutenant pair uses its assigned shield, medic or mounted long-range loadout. The first route offers hack, timed hold and reach objectives. The named target must remain active until the reach objective resolves before the liberation scene can commit the release. Expedition maps fit their playable area at the current viewport and use staggered starting slots. Panning and touch picking use the same projection scale.

Check scenario and map sizes, spawn lanes, objective density, movement search, path congestion, turn scheduling, AI candidate count, reaction chains, threat markers, control overlays, unit and target selection, ally pathing, support decisions and effect cleanup. Choose map and enemy caps from the real campaign encounters and profile on the target iPhone. Keep existing four-way facings, role readability, reduced motion and touch-safe controls. Reserve board space for friendly turns and avoid long compulsory cut-ins for routine actions. Tune block, healing, cover, long-range positioning, branch consequences and nonlethal capture together. Automated regression cases can support the work. The acceptance checkpoint is a real chapter with all ten visible and acting on the same board.

Track textures, decoding, WebGL disposal, CPU, startup JS budget, turn latency, total mission length and tap accuracy on the landscape Home Screen app. Use measured budgets and early reusable clip coverage. Verify normal, fast and reduced-motion playback from the same actions. Demonstrate 10 legal units, a real loss, free retry, reload mid-encounter, continued control of all ten, victory and exactly one committed liberated target. Consider the additional actors in the largest Act IV battle separately after its story design.
