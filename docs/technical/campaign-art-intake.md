## Strike effect

`commandCinematics.effects` binds `kind: "fighter-strike"`, slot `cy55-effect-fighter-strike`, to
`command-fighter-strike`. Its approved 1774 × 887 transparent native image is unchanged. The existing
reader places one contained layer at x240/y65, width360/height205, on an 844 × 390 canvas with portraits
shown. The wing silhouettes symbolize the resolved action; their count does not depict an allocation.

The [cinematic truth conditions](command-cinematics.md#effects-and-truth-conditions) remain authoritative:
only a committed strike with positive ship or emitter damage selects the effect. It appears during
confirmed-action/result beats. Captain-order/acknowledgement beats and a valid no-damage Evade do not
show it. Partial emitter damage is distinct from disablement; neither image claims approach victory,
boarding success or destruction. An unpowered strike remains an invalid order under the existing rules.

Profiles, operating poses, the command station, numeric marks and controls remain separate. Missing
art keeps the factual result and Continue control. Normal playback, fast/skip, reload and content
replacement never resolve another order, spend cargo or settle another night.
