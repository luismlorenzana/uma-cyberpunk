| D8 | 2026-11-12 | **CY4 is done: `@cyber/battle`, the core rules, rules version 1.** (1) **The package** depends on nothing, takes plain numbers, and builds CJS for Node like `@cyber/domain` while Vite and Vitest read its source. (2) **The map format, schema 1,** as the packet set it: 6–14 a side, digit height rows 0–3 (uma-stage's), `.`/`#`/`=`/`c` tiles (grating and cover play as floor until CY5), exactly five squad spawns and one to eight enemy spawns; `parseMap` names the path of any error. (3) **The seeded roll** is keyed by the battle's **turn number**, not its round (`seed:turn:unit:purpose:n`), since a fast unit can act twice in a round; FNV-1a with murmur3's finaliser so neighbouring keys spread evenly. (4) **The clock** fills to 1,000 at 8–12 a tick by Speed band, so band 3 takes 100 ticks (a round) and band 5 takes 84; ticks 1–100 are round 1. (5) **Decided where the packet left it open:** range counts steps with no diagonals; line of fire runs from 0.8 of a level above the shooter's tile to 0.5 above the target's, blocked by solid tiles and higher ground, never by units, and Long needs it too; where an attack lands uses quarters, so diagonals are the side; the attacker turns to her target as she fires (so counters usually land on her front); the exchange runs main strike, then Crossfire, then the counter; **Cover** steps in only when the target's own shield doesn't guard the attack, needs her link on like Crossfire, takes the main strike from the front, and a covered attack draws no counter; each hit rolls its own hit, crit and part; overflow past a part's HP is lost; a kneeling unit isn't a target and extracts when her next turn comes; **beating every enemy always wins**, whatever the objective; reaching the tile ends the battle before her action. Aiming needs a Visor with `aim`. (6) **Damage is exact integer arithmetic** (whole-number percentages multiplied, one floor), so a replay can't drift between machines. (7) **Tests:** 60 in the package, one file per rule area; the forecast's hit % is within two points of the resolved rate over 2,000 seeds for Melee, Short and Long, and every resolved hit's damage is the forecast's number. **Seven golden battles** on two test maps (`test-yard`, `test-steps`) cover counters, Crossfire both ways, Cover, Guard, parts breaking, Rig Break, Hold on, extraction and all four objectives; their actions come from a plain greedy player (CY6 brings the real AI), and a one-number change to `tuning.ts` fails them by name.
