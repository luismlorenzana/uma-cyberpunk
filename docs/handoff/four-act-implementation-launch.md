# Four-act implementation launch

This is the execution schedule for the [production roadmap](four-act-roadmap.md). Use the [campaign design](../design/campaign-rework.md) for game rules and the [authoring contract](../design/campaign-art-delivery.md) for private content delivery. Progress advances through the actual campaign. Each checkpoint has a playable entry, a saved result and the inputs required to reproduce it.

## Engineering contract order

| Deliverable | Where it connects | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |

Extend the campaign document with a versioned registry when its first producer and consumer land. | Does every independent arc, fixed duo, capital and expedition resolve to the intended IDs? Does a missing or cyclic prerequisite fail before selection? |
| Quest readiness and completion | Existing quest selection and finish handlers, recruitment, mission launch and settlement. Store typed results for actor, home, capital and operation when the successful action commits. | Does a displayed available action remain legal when committed? Do stale, denied, repeated and out-of-order actions preserve the prior state? |
| Roster, residence and access | Roster, quarters, relationship, resource duty, workshop, bridge and ground sortie handlers. Drive availability from one role/clearance policy and return a player-facing blocked reason. | Can a recovered General move in and date while unable to deploy? Can a named Uma return to her personal route when assigned elsewhere? |
| Supplies and capital settlement | Supply network, civilian port stock, base project/receipt, debrief and capped persistence. Track committed consequences separately from scene viewing. | Does a retry remain possible for a sole recruit? Can the player clear all five capitals while unrelated duos remain unfinished? |
| Mission and operation receipts | Ship and ground start, resolution and replay. Carry release ID, mission definition, inputs, costs, rules version, roster and relevant branch state to both renderers. | After a reload or content update, does the same frozen action produce the same result and visual cues? |
| Ten-member formation | Domain validates roles and fitting families. Formation preparation feeds real scenarios and the existing battle reducer. Campaign, board, replay and reduced-motion playback consume the same snapshots. | Are all ten a legal controllable formation with viable paths? Do early fights still use their original caps, replay rules and visuals? |
| Combined publishing | Current Story packer, Platform approvals, game asset publisher and release selection. Extend art publication with four-act scope before moving any real approved art to that pointer. | Can a failed pack be retried and can an approved selection be rolled back without changing unrelated pointers, art or saves? |

Measure existing caps before changing them. The current pack allows 1,024 quests and 1,024 story files, 64 encounter definitions in each combat lane and 100 combat-sprite entries. The save accepts 300 crew records and the API body limit is 1 MiB. The existing late-save test covers 60 crew. Size a complete catalog with actual chapter and encounter counts before committing to the current eager load. Start with quests, command and ground encounters, scene and image references, sprite manifest entries, crew, saved flags and seen scenes. Record source files, compressed download, first-load timings, save bytes, turn timings and texture memory on the first expedition and the full campaign candidate. Keep mission and visual loads lazy when measurements warrant it.

## One record across Story, Art and Engineering

CY96 keeps the filled records private. Use one row per playable beat, with references for shared assets and alternate outcomes. Each row has a stable beat ID, act and scope, Story revision and scene IDs, prerequisite and committed result, required actor roles, optional cast, location, mission and retry path. The same row carries environment states, portraits with outfit/expression cues, splash and framing, sprite fitting and action cues, audio where required, exact source pins, candidate, evidence and open decisions.

| Lane | First delivery | Handoff condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story · CY92 | Fixed pairing and capital register, exact opening, two independent Act I routes and capture/recovery joins. Reconcile prior tier scripts with the new rules. | ID, role, trigger, outcome and variant recorded per beat. The first expedition and Act IV requests only commit to actual approved targets and outcomes. |
| General Art · CY93 | Entrance, bridge, sign-on and berth, quarters, service and supply points, first ship/ground location, custody staging and the reusable civic/base states. | Candidate shows the right location before and after progression; asset and owner-approved reuse pins resolve. |
| Character Art · CY94 | Lead and General looks and facing states for first two routes, accurate conversation crops, first sign-on and relationship splashes, the capture/recovery role change. | Author expression tags and splash variants only where the scene calls for them. Bind the exact role and outfit in the real sequence. |
| Sprite Art · CY95 | Published crew reuse, General enemy/ally form reference, lieutenant families and validated equipment fittings for the first campaign fights. | Bring release/fitting/role data into the same manifest. Extend to ten-slot act-specific action coverage and Seraine silhouette families once the first expedition is pinned. |
| Integration · CY96 | Create the first private candidate from compatible components, including correctly labelled temporary assets. | Record each lane's submitted revision, approved bytes, selection and repeatable real-campaign evidence. Review families in batches. |

If a scene changes its location, cast, objective or outcome, reopen the relevant binding rows with the same beat ID. Dialogue-only revisions keep the game and art rows if their actual contracts still hold. Keep earlier authored beats as reuse candidates where they fit. Prior tier recruitment gates, compulsory relationship branches, Act II operation access, NPC-only lieutenants and prior finale timing cannot define the new runtime or its assets.

## Starting intake

The November 24 private Story register contains 50 complete named J1–J9 sets, 25 fixed duos and 150 lieutenant roles. The first revised duo, capital and expedition chapters have private binding files. The chapter inventory measures source availability. CY92 still verifies whether each chapter fits a specific act, result and role. The earlier Story game plan and paired-case pages supply executable scene and encounter references. They require reconciliation for open recruitment and lieutenant deployment. The Seraine bible supplies court and rank identities, the five capital connections and visual states. Its previous reveal schedule and final liberation sequence go through the new act and finale decisions.

The first implementation uses `/campaign/four-act` as a direct review entry. The Home Screen default changes at CY91. The campaign assembler and local review still use the existing Campaign screen, private release loader and ordinary save lifecycle. The first private draft uses existing authored material while Story supplies the fixed initial duo and coordinated capital routes. Treat one active expedition and one expedition per fixed duo as implementation planning assumptions. Act IV access, exact Queen encounters and post-ending choices remain workshop decisions.
