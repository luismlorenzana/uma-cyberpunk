**D73 update (2026-11-20).** Use CY59's helmeted-first bodies, shared family poses and CY58 energy
equipment. Match each actual opponent's frozen hand/mount profile: the current mid-range Laser
rifle is two-handed and Long weapons stay Back-mounted. Reuse identity assets across enemy/ally
appearances when appropriate. Helmet-off variants follow actual staging needs. Missing approved

Inventory actual ground-encounter unit references by private identity, tier, commander/sub-boss/support
role and armor requirement. The current authoring cast includes fifty bosses and 150 supporting
guests; reuse a single identity's set across encounters where appropriate. A pilot-suit approval does
not approve a new armor design. Pin missing armor baselines with the existing authoring workflow.
Use Story's updated lieutenant lineage in G15. Where the approved likeness reveals it, preserve
the elven ear silhouette across the portrait and the compatible helmet design.
Do not turn a humanoid boss into a literal dragon or introduce a creature transformation without
its approved story brief. Record visual scale and combat footprint separately.

Use four authored directions, consistent ground anchors and reproducible frame placement. Reuse
approved armor construction, compatible poses and CY58 equipment while preserving the actual
opponent's identity and frozen profile. Share the same approved body across its Ally and Enemy
roles. Bind each role to its actual weapons, Back unit and outcome clips.

## Authoring work

1. Start with the first campaign boarding opponent and her actual supporting cast. Select their
   approved identity, faction/role cues, palette and armor or request the missing armor approval.
   Use common CY58 weapon designs unless a named story weapon needs a separate approved design.
2. Follow the compatible CY57/CY59 frame and attachment contract: four facings, foot anchor, grip/mount
   anchors, depth order, stand/walk/action/reaction coverage and helmet policy. Preserve identity
   between dialogue portrait, sprite and eventual release/recovery scenes.
3. Present the commander's silhouette alongside ordinary squad figures at phone size. Larger art can
   project above a single-tile anchor, but keep cover, adjacent units, target rings and hit selection
   legible. Record the extent/bounds in metadata. A multi-tile footprint remains separate rules work.
4. Author warned attack and recovery/wind-up poses only against CY61's supported signature/intent
   contract. Coordinate the corresponding splash with CY62 so the same weapon and silhouette carry
   into the attack. A visual wind-up cannot imply an extra turn or an unimplemented move.
5. Review capture/Rig Break and nonlethal outcomes against CY63. Body poses and portraits remain
   recognizable; destroyed equipment follows actual disabled-part state. Expand by tier and actual
   encounter coverage after the first boarding group passes review.

## Handoff

Return identity-to-encounter/unit mappings privately, approved armor/weapon references, editable
projects/rigs, reproducible exporter settings, clean frames/atlases and all numerical frame,
attachment/bounds metadata, alpha, hashes, scoped approvals and intended visual scale. Record
reused sets explicitly. Source identities and franchise art never go in git. Use the existing enemy
authoring collections and a documented private staging path if their albums do not yet support the
sprite subtype; development records any required shared publication change.

## Done when

Every required combat identity in the encounter registry has an approved set and correct binding,
with supported attack/reaction states, clear silhouettes and checked equipment. The first boarding
group, a later sub-boss and a larger boss have in-game phone-size evidence. Unapproved armor and
unsupported creature/footprint requests remain explicitly open. Delete this packet with its Done row.
