# CY77 Development · Base looks, expressions and boss presentation

> **Archived under D86.** See the [disposition matrix](four-act-disposition.md) for the active successor. This packet preserves earlier requirements and delivery evidence.

## Outcome

Reconcile the hosted roster with approved Crew, Boss Ally, Boss Enemy and sub-boss portrait and
armor sources. Resolve the intended appearance for each wardrobe, dialogue, ship and ground
screen. Use CY65–CY70 for neutral looks, crops and sparse face recipes. Covers G14 presentation
with CY73's committed Story phase data.

The hosted baseline already contains 300 crew bindings and 50 Boss Ally wardrobes. Work from
exact asset and recipe differences. Review the role of the armor entries already visible in
the hosted inventory before using those bindings as precedent.

## Implementation

1. Extend the private register by stable outfit or heroine board identity and presentation role.
   Record the native look hash, approved expression recipe and crop bounds, published source
   record, current campaign binding, and Story/runtime consumer. Preserve identical pilot assets
   across both collections where their source and hash agree. Bind new armor and expressions
   through exact indices and runtime roles.
2. Use the existing wardrobe/crop publisher for approved base art and sparse expression states.
   Preserve native artwork and approved profile/framing. Route explicit scene outfits first,
   then the approved context default. Use the exact speaker's neutral if an expression is
   absent. Wardrobe-only outfits do not enter scenes unless requested by their authored route.
3. Route Boss Enemy armor and controlled faces for the actual flagship, approach or ground
   encounter that uses them. Route post-capture recovery, release and optional wardrobe looks by
   committed phase and authored scene intent. Retain the wardrobe access gate and independent
   battle identity. Treat a sub-boss's portrait as its own stable board identity even if her
   capture shares a mission outcome with her boss. Keep appearance mappings configurable across
   the existing campaign and the new pilot.
4. Verify exact owner approvals and source versions before a scoped publish. Use a private
   exception list for a blocked or semantically unmatched image. Align the role/outfit/emote
   row with Story and Art before moving it into a live scene. Share the role and silhouette
   reference with CY78/CY79.

## Acceptance

- Check one crew member in wardrobe, recruit dialogue and ship comms, one boss before capture,
  in recovery and after joining, and one sub-boss on the correct encounter. Verify stable names,
  outfit labels, registered emotion variants, neutral fallback and exact profile crops.
- Confirm existing wardrobe bindings and original Story/rule/save fingerprints are preserved
  during replacements, with scoped behavior for the new pilot. Test repeat, incompatible face
  base, wrong actor/role, missing source and rollback before expanding by cohort.
