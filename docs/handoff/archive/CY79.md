# CY79 Development · Boss, Ally and sub-boss figures on the board

> **Archived under D86.** See the [disposition matrix](four-act-disposition.md) for the active successor. This packet preserves earlier requirements and delivery evidence.

## Outcome

Render structured Boss Enemy, Boss Ally and sub-boss sprites from their actual unit identities
and battle state. Use CY60's figure/scale brief, CY76's verified bundle pipeline, CY77's role
bindings and CY74's Collar/Talk results. Start with one actual named boss and one of her sub-bosses
before expanding. Use the same boss identity for her later legal Ally deployment.

## Implementation

1. Extend the game bundle index, publisher and board loader to accept canonical heroine board
   IDs and scoped presentation roles. Preserve numeric crew outfit parsing and replay
   compatibility. Resolve the actor and enemy/ally role from frozen unit data and the committed
   result. Keep versioned assets and fit/scale references per actor. Validate that the board
   lookup resolves the exact encounter actor, including alternate or transformed forms where
   declared.
2. Select stances, equipment, guard and hit cues from the actual chosen encounter. Keep readable
   silhouette, feet, effects, shadow, cover layering and tap area in the existing board camera.
   Separate figure height and projected art bounds from logical tile occupancy, reach and line of
   sight. Use approved pilot/armor looks as source references and the existing portrait fallback
   when a structured bundle is pending.
3. Wire controlled, freed, down and withdrawal states to the same cues that settle the fight.
   Apply the actual allied fitting and role on subsequent crew deployment. Keep ship combat
   communication art and authoring-only armor views scoped to their matching uses.
4. Stage the first boss/sub-boss package through the same guarded release pipeline. Record
   browser memory, texture disposal and side-by-side readability with a crew recruit on phone.
   Map later units from the executable encounter/cast register after the cohort is verified.

## Acceptance

- On an actual campaign board, verify actor identity, orientation, scale, selection, ordinary
  cues, shield or armor integrity, Collar break, authored sub-boss Talk/removal and enemy
  fallback. Resume during the saved outcome path and replay the frozen input.
- Deploy the released Boss Ally with a legal squad if her Story route permits it. Check that the
  Ally uses the correct role, equipment and crew UI. Test that missing assets do not block
  settlement, objective targeting or retry.
- Return private approvals, hashes, figures and outcome evidence. Report named encounters whose
  full motion or physical-device check remains open under CY60/CY37.

shares a model and identity across the controlled and playable roles, with the correct equipment
for each. The sub-boss uses her authored opponent fitting. The Stage and select commands reuse
the guarded Platform publisher, with a separate pilot channel and role validation against
the campaign's current Story configuration.

The [private Art handoff](../../../content/cy79-review/art-handoff.md) includes the editable
blockout, exact approved armor references and required contact and exit clips. The final Art

remains on her saved fitting. Both board exits released their WebGL context.
