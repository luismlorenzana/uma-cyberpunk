# CY62 Art authoring · Armored character and boss signature splashes

> **Archived under D86.** See the [disposition matrix](four-act-disposition.md) for the active successor. This packet preserves earlier requirements and delivery evidence.

## Outcome and dependencies

Author armored special-attack splashes for the playable cast and bosses, bound to the actions CY61
actually implements. Use the [delivery contract](../../design/archive/tier-campaign-art-delivery.md),
[CY61 Signatures](CY61.md), [CY63 Story](CY63.md), [CY58 Equipment](CY58.md) and
[CY59](CY59.md)/[CY60](CY60.md) figure references. References and thumbnails may begin early;
final action compositions follow the confirmed rules/trigger and phone layout fixture.

Start with the two [White Spire draft actions](CY61.md#white-spire-draft). Review the named
crew strike and the boss's tile warning against actual board timing before expanding.

## Scope and authoring

1. Create the action-art registry from actual eligible actors. Target one primary signature splash
   for each playable character and boss with a supported signature; track the fifty crew and fifty
   bosses individually. Featured sub-boss splashes require an explicit authored action row. Ordinary
   support enemies do not automatically receive a unique cinematic for every hit.
2. Pin approved armor, helmet policy, character likeness, palette and equipped canonical weapon.
   The splash and sprite must show the same actor and equipment identity. If a signature is tied to
   a weapon family, state its compatible loadouts and fallback presentation rather than showing an
   unequipped unique weapon.
3. Establish the first campaign recruit and first boss's compositions together. Frame the main
   action, weapon and silhouette for landscape; leave readable areas for the action label, optional
   profile and short spoken line. The Captain remains off camera.
4. Use still splash layers plus reusable motion/effects where effective. Return an explicit impact,
   shield, repair, hack or area-attack subject matching the actual effect. A single-target skill must
   not look like a map-wide blast. A boss warning and its final strike are distinct cues; only author
   extra wind-up/strike variants when the confirmed action needs them.
5. Make large boss compositions consistent with CY60's approved silhouette/scale. Maintain armor,
   weapon and faction continuity through dialogue, warning, board action and capture. Do not create
   new transformations, weapons or attack phases through artwork alone.
6. Reuse the existing armor portrait and expression crops for speech overlays. Splash art is an
   action composition, not another expression set. Expand by tier after the first real battle passes
   layout and action review; retain a per-actor coverage list and approved reuse notes.

## Return and review

Return stable actor/action/presentation keys, supported loadouts/look, native masters and exports,
alpha/layer arrangement, anchors, subject/effect bounds, dimensions, SHA-256 and exact approval scope.
State whether the shot is a full CG or transparent pose/effects over the shared treatment. Provide
the matching neutral profile/crop references, text-safe view and in-game capture from CY61.

Review with normal, quick and skipped playback, opponent framing, dialogue and action name at actual
phone size. Keep the decisive pose legible in the quick view. Missing final art must be identifiable
as a draft in the shared record, even when the generic cut-in works.

## Done when

Every eligible actor/action row in the locked registry has approved compatible exports and correct
in-game triggering. The first hero/boss battle, a later weapon family and a larger boss show consistent
equipment and outcomes. Physical iPhone observations remain in CY37. Delete with a Done row.
