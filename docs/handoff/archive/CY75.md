## Implementation

1. Register each new cohort against the shared capture ledger. Five distinct captures unlock
   the tier's Signal and ten unlock its Core. Preserve independent recruit order within an open
   tier. Make sign-ons depend on the settled preceding Core.
2. Author each new Core sequence against the real ship and ground inputs. Preserve
   investigation, muster, retry and resume where the route uses them.
   Validate every settlement/reward and next-tier transition. Define the Core III
   re-capture and return as authored state transitions on the same crew identity and ensure
   equipment, quarters, bonds and dispatch records reconcile after it.
3. Integrate front locations, civilian supply and home liberation at the authored order.
   Preserve the five-system travel model. Track two new homes per system per tier and the
   capitals/final operation in the last tier. Use approved setting equivalents with named
   Story routes while new locations land. Reuse ship upkeep and project consumers, testing
   benefits, provisioning and launch gates on the revised route.
4. Reuse the mission-scoped call-up for resettled pairs listed by later operations.
   Review call-ups alongside any new custody, temporary removal and return rules.
5. Finish Core V and its post-operation landing/epilogue. Review every tier as a vertical slice
   covering Story, character art, battle and ship/planet management. Record binder changes and
   the owner's canonical campaign decision through CY36/CY37.

  and genuine next-tier gates all behave correctly. Test save and reload mid-core.
- Verify resettled, active, away and temporarily called-up units across deploy, cancel, resume
  and settlement. Confirm no lost home support or duplicate duty assignment. Exercise the Core III
  re-capture and restoration when authored.
- Produce a per-tier release receipt covering the ten journeys, five-signal/ten-core gates,
