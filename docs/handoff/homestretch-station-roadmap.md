## Combined action economy

The military clock runs at 0600 Duty, 1400 Free and 2200 Night. Travel and completed operations move the same clock. Reserve a Night watch for an eligible conversation or a Free watch for rehabilitation. Night supports two short visits or one overnight that uses the remaining time. Morning scenes resolve in order after dawn. The directory groups Umas, released Generals, resident Lieutenants and eligible shared visits. Established bonds keep their progress during a long rotation.

The station and the cutter pay their own provisions. Homestretch pays two plus one per 20 residents and can draw from its reserve or local surplus. Staffed Life Support scales with population. Liberated bases can dispatch surpluses and the cutter can take a bulk load. A shortfall pauses optional output while core work and campaign recovery remain available. Visits give next-day support through strain recovery, staffed duty output, or a resident Lieutenant's ration contribution.

The exact live Story book supported a 250-resident rotation over 125 nights. It covered 100 personal routes and 150 Lieutenant check-ins, two queued morning scenes, a consented overnight, next-day duty output and a complete ten-person expedition afterwards. At full occupancy the station paid 14 provisions per dawn. With low reserves and a staffed Life Support facility the rotation remained funded. Removing that duty assignment in the low-stock check produced a shortfall of eight provisions. Physical iPhone play and feedback on long-session pacing remain part of CY125.
