## First playable checkpoint

From a fresh game, finish First Watch and zoom from station to deck to room. Queue Habitat A or a production room through the real build flow with its time and power delta visible. Recruit a first Uma from a different home system with the starter cutter. Verify first docking, meeting, invitation, berth assignment and Act I entry. On return, assign the new Uma to a crew watch and see the recorded job and build completion. Follow the chosen lead's authored availability, then book and play an eligible evening. Enter an existing Act I cutter battle and commit its result. Demonstrate this on the same campaign profile, using short baseline Story and reviewed shared art where final assets are pending. Play one path end to end before rolling the patterns across the full catalog.
