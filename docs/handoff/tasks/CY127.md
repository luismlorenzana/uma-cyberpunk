## Integration contract

Use the game’s room keys and four deck groups. The model fits the five core facilities and ten additions using the
same socket coordinates throughout growth. Bring proposed color, silhouette or geometry changes back as markup against
the existing [visual kit](../../../apps/web/src/campaign/VesselVisuals.tsx). Hull condition, livery and loadout marks
follow the actual cutter identities in Fleet, launch, expedition and the battle snapshot.

Approval can be a short decision plus the screen and state reviewed at 844×390 and 667×375. A replacement setting needs
its exact live destination, the one approved image with its source/version/SHA-256, and a readable dialogue crop. Replace
the existing setting slot through the normal asset publisher. Keep the shared library within ten and verify its exact
bindings. Workshop and historical source files remain in the authoring archive.
