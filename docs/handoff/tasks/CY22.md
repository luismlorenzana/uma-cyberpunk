# CY22 Authored recruitment Training

## Why

Training makes a recruit's style and the tactics rules legible in the real campaign after sign-on.

## Context

Use the [campaign release path](../../technical/campaign-content.md) and the authored opening in [CY38](CY38.md). Read the [mission flow](../../game/missions.md), [battle manual](../../game/battle.md), `packages/battle/src/rig.ts` and the mission input adapter. The private opening and recruitment briefs identify eligible trainees and their first assignments.

The private `baseline-4` pack replaces the draft sign-on with Training and makes the debrief the gate for quarters. The draft was exercised in Chrome through briefing, Hangar, a Bulwark placement, reload, an aimed Forecast, victory, the recruitment debrief and morning. Each of the four starter kits has an automated winning replay. The simulator's default kit won 55 of 60 seeds, averaging 6.9 player turns. The location backdrop and battle figures are placeholders. The reviewed content swap and physical iPhone playthrough remain open.

## Decisions made

Use the campaign's briefing, Hangar, deploy, battle, results and debrief. Offer Training as optional familiarization after sign-on. A failed exercise can retry without consuming campaign supplies. Use targetable rig damage, one main weapon style and limited armor expansions. Preserve prior replays across rules changes.

## Done when

A fresh Captain can sign on a recruit, reach the optional exercise, deploy the issued gear, lose and retry, close and resume mid-battle, complete the exercise and see its debrief. The first evening works on either opening route. Completion applies once. The integrated map, dialogue, portraits and weapon cues are readable on the phone.
