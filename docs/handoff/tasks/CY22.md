# CY22 Authored recruitment Training

## Why

Training makes the opening recruit's style and the tactics rules legible in the real campaign.

## Context

Use the [campaign release path](../../technical/campaign-content.md) from CY20 and integrate with CY21's opening and evening flow. Read the [mission flow](../../game/missions.md), [battle manual](../../game/battle.md), `packages/battle/src/rig.ts` and the mission input adapter. The first-pair candidate and opening brief are in the ignored `content/campaign-notes/` directory in the active worktree.

The private `baseline-3` pack rehearses sign-on, the night and the morning. Replace its draft sign-on quest with Training. Its `evening.after` gate should then depend on the authored recruitment debrief.

## Decisions made

Use the campaign's briefing, Hangar, deploy, battle, results and debrief. A failed Training exercise can retry without consuming campaign supplies. Start with targetable rig damage, one main weapon style and limited armor expansions. Preserve legacy replays across rules changes.

## Do

- Implement the starter loadouts the script needs, with a melee and a ranged option and the mounted actions the exercise teaches. Carry them through gear validation, legal actions, forecasts, AI and display. Verify broken arms with a two-handed main and define which expansions still work.
- During implementation, run a playable baseline briefing, coach line and debrief with a provisional map and art through the same campaign and pack wiring. Replace them with the reviewed quest content using the agreed references.
- Integrate an authored HD-2D Training map and a real quest, including the recruit's dialogue, movement and cover, forecast reading and a useful mounted action. The encounter uses campaign state and yields recruitment, a debrief and access to her quarters on completion.
- Run automated replays and simulator passes on the authored encounter definition, then play the full sequence on the iPhone. Tune where the recruit speaks and where the player sees enough information to act.

## Done when

A fresh Captain can reach the exercise through the opening, deploy the new gear, lose and retry, close and resume mid-battle, complete the exercise, see the debrief, and visit the recruit that evening. Completion applies once. The integrated map, dialogue, portraits and weapon cues are readable on the phone.

## Deliver

Record damage and loadout calls from the playtest. Follow the repo PR workflow, delete this packet when complete and add its Done row.
