## Production

1. Inventory approved assets for 50 Umas and 50 Generals, plus 150 lieutenants and the Seraine cast used by Story. Include recurring civilians and fallback speaking roles. Register exact actors, role state, outfit, expression and source hash. Keep previous endorsed master and crop approvals with their scope. A source approval and a game scene's destination approval are distinct.
2. For Umas, verify existing civilian, ship and operational outfits and their dialogue/bridge and combat framing for the scripts. For the Generals, bind controlled Enemy, captured/rehabilitation, released Ally, cohabitation and cleared expedition variants where used. For elf lieutenants, preserve their identities across hostile, missing, returned and expedition roles. Add only the outfits required by the route and artist-reviewed dressing.
3. Author the minimum expression matrix by actor/outfit based on actual scenes. Reuse approved neutral portraits and sparse face crops, keep native and mechanical profile/dialogue crops, use exact sky, helm or expression contexts, and preserve approved facial features. Keep condition branches and live wardrobe admission in sync with story and operational clearance.
4. Cover supported meeting, confession, seated cabin chat and bed positions, stay/leave and morning. Agree camera, eyes, feet/hands, layering, room reuse and cast for shared visits, rehabilitation, investigations, duo bases, ensemble briefings and epilogues. Author private poses against terrain and room references supplied by General Art. Use countable required comps and repeatable layers where stage geometry matches.
5. Supply ship-command character art keyed to actual station, actor, outfit and order result. Use pilot poses and talking profiles as supported by existing layering. Author named enemy communication, assault and retreat cues only where Story intends them. Match tactical signature CGs, weapons and warning poses to confirmed rules and bind distinct action and actor IDs.
6. Cover Heralds, Echoes and Queens with approved Joined designs. Where Story shows a Seraine end, use an ending composition in which her markings go dark and she comes apart into light. Use the exact CY110 art brief and approved identity for each destination.
7. Canonical identity, outfit, expressions, splashes and exact release joins must pass approved scopes, hash checks and in-game preview. Keep Art on Platform and game release paths using the current Cyberpunk publisher. Link each result to [CY96](CY96.md).
