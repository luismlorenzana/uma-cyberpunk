## Implementation

1. Resolve a saved rig to a visual manifest keyed by stable part IDs. Cover core, arms, legs, visor,
   both hands, Back unit and coating. Reuse canonical shapes with masks/palette/material treatments.
   Missing variants fall back visibly and preserve the equipped rig; art never changes stats.
2. Implement per-frame attachment and facing/depth order, including behind-body equipment, two-handed
   grips, shields and Back mounts. Support weapon-family-compatible arm poses. Keep asymmetric
   accessories on their original sides. Validate anchors, bounds, palette masks and missing layers.
3. Drive visuals from battle cues and frozen inputs: stand, walk, family attack/support, hurt,
   guard and kneel/reboot. Victory/signature motion follows supported needs. Start production with
   helmeted armor; helmet-off combat sets follow an authored requirement. Broken arm/legs/Core
   states agree with actual rules, including degraded two-handed use.
4. Load approved crew armor sets and explicit guest/enemy visual IDs. Map the actual cast boss and
   subordinates from the encounter, rather than substituting arbitrary identities. Keep old sprite
   and standee fallback paths usable. Art mappings are versioned outside the deterministic reducer.
5. Support reviewed larger boss silhouettes with correct foot anchor, selection, depth and camera
   clearance. Retain current one-tile occupancy. Record any actual multi-tile/creature requirement
   as a separate rules proposal with its scene need before promising that asset family.
6. Extend checking, private export/packing/loading and compatible release selection. Preserve old
   input versions, action replays, saves and legal gear. Use synthetic fixtures in git and private
   masters/cast only in ignored content. Integrate an early real recruit and her actual opponent
   with placeholders before approved sheets land, then roll out by tier.

## Verification and completion

Prefer composing/caching equipped frames at battle/loadout setup while measuring against live
layers. Reuse body, arm/hand occlusion, equipment and palette sources. Avoid generating every
combination or rebuilding full textures every render frame. Cache keys include source hashes,
fixture version, look, equipment, palette and represented damage/helmet variants. Bound the
active-encounter cache and dispose owned resources on eviction. Freeze visual loadout metadata
at deploy separately from deterministic rules. Cover stat parts through shared details/materials
or reviewed readable representations, rather than repainting a body for each part.

Check all catalog mappings, legal one/two-handed rigs, empty slots, four facings, broken parts,
helmet policy and missing-art fallback. Test old input/replay compatibility and release replacement.
Play a planet-funded fitting in the Hangar and use it in the next real boarding battle. Review cover,
front/back, hit selection and larger boss occlusion at 844 × 390. Measure a representative full battle
for texture memory, frame time, loadout cache growth and saver behavior on the available device.
Run lint, tests, build and affected actual-encounter replays; leave physical iPhone findings in CY37.

Done when the supported composition contract, all equipment mappings and crew/guest loading work in
campaign, representative action families are readable and performance is recorded. List pending
