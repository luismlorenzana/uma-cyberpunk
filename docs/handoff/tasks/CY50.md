# CY50 Story authoring · Four relationship scenes and the evening flow

## Outcome and linked work

Stage the fifty existing Captain arcs for meeting, confession, seated cabin chat and staying overnight.
Start with the first real recruit and a second recruit who proves independent relationships. Use the
[delivery contract](../../design/campaign-art-delivery.md), [implemented CY48 contract](../../technical/campaign-content.md#reserved-cabin-evenings-cy48) and
[CY49 Art](CY49.md). Work in the Story Authoring book's private Cyberpunk files and verdicts.
Existing service plots, tier gates, custody and appointments remain the campaign spine.

## Scene requirements

| Family | Required writing and choices |
|---|---|
| Meeting | Short first introduction at the shared ship welcome/briefing area, character voice and one concrete service concern; retain the existing join and independently launchable story |
| Confession | Captain can directly express romantic interest; the character answers mutually; retain a warm, complete Trust option and the existing relationship milestone ID |
| Seated chat | Repeatable conversation while sitting in her cabin; character-specific daily concerns and a natural lead into the stay/leave choice |
| Overnight | An explicitly welcomed stay, a lying-in-bed conversation beat and a coherent morning; leave also ends the same reserved evening |

## Authoring and state contract

1. Reconcile `content/campaign-notes/pillar-one-splash-scenes.json` with current script exports. Supply
   a row for every recruit with existing sign-on, confession, repeatable visit, eligibility and morning,
   and name new overnight IDs explicitly. Record current physical staging and required changes. Never
   rename a completed quest or silently relocate a simulated port to fit the shared meeting picture.
2. Write the first complete sequence with short executable dialogue, precise speaker/expression cues
   and illustration/portrait intent. Reconcile sign-on props with the ship location and the service
   story. Add a small connecting line when the old scene assumed a shoreside meeting.
3. Keep characters distinct in how they receive a confession. A mutual Heart choice begins/continues
   the appropriate relationship, while Trust preserves friendship and campaign access. Current
   milestones and their service prerequisites are retained unless the shared record explicitly
   requests a mechanic change. Do not rewrite every prior line just to add an image.
4. For regular chats, author a bounded set of repeatable lines with the selection rule and any once-only
   milestone clearly stated. Keep ordinary concerns, earlier service outcomes and later handovers
   consistent. Define stay eligibility, mutual invitation, unavailable lines, leaving and the next
   morning. Ask CY48 to implement unsupported selection/state behavior before final export.
5. Staying follows chat within one evening. Specify rapport/effects and their exact completion point;
   neither re-entering a scene nor resuming a pack may award them twice. Art controls do not change
   story state. Current consent/relationship is required before its overnight branch is offered.
6. Inventory existing shared visits separately. Write paired seated and bed variants only where the
   participants' release, living arrangement and individual choices permit them. Provide separate
   invitation/decline lines and mornings where required. Preserve the independent appointment and
   resettlement outcomes. Add no new solo boss romance system through dialogue alone.
7. Expand through all five tiers using the same contract. Keep earlier crew visits valid after each
   intake and after later civilian crossings. Return a concise revision list so development can
   combine the export without overwriting newer approved story from another cohort.

## Done when

export. Development can bind the approved four-scene flow without guessing costs, consent, flags or
art intent. Record integration findings and delete this packet with its Done row.
