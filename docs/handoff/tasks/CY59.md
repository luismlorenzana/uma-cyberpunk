# CY59 Art authoring · Helmeted armored character sprites

## Outcome and linked work

Create recognizable helmeted armored figures for the real campaign, using reusable body/pose assets
and separate [CY58 equipment](CY58.md). Follow [CY57 Development](CY57.md), [CY60 Enemies](CY60.md),
[CY63 Story](CY63.md), the [delivery contract](../../design/campaign-art-delivery.md) and
[approved art direction](../../design/art-direction.md). D73 establishes **helmeted armor first,
shared family poses and attachments**. The older [image request](../../design/combat-sprites-request.md)
remains reference history for proportions/anatomy; its helmet-off-first order and sheet quotas no
longer set production scope. This packet covers the fifty playable identities; CY60 uses the same
method for actual bosses/sub-bosses. Reuse an identity's assets across enemy/ally appearances where
look and equipment match. A role change alone does not require a new complete set.

## Source intake and likeness

Inventory Combat Sprites collections and exact approved armor/helmet references. Keep scored outfit
identities and sister variants distinct. Original-outfit traveler sprites are fallbacks, not approved
Cyberpunk armor. Record usable masters, approval hashes, pending studies and missing baselines.

Keep the established chibi proportions, armor silhouette, ears/housings, tail, emblem, palette and
anatomical asymmetry. Draw front, back, left profile and right profile; never manufacture a facing
by mirroring asymmetric details. Front/back must read at phone size because facing changes damage.
A helmet hides the face but preserves identity through armor, ears, tail, silhouette and colors.

Use the established eye-level upright-sprite convention for the board's 35-degree camera. Preserve
the 512-pixel source-cell convention unless CY57 records a reviewed alternative. The executable
fixture fixes layout, scale and foot position before bulk exports; it also fixes the required frame
counts/timings. Do not duplicate the older complete sheet count for every weapon and outfit.

## Required body and pose families

| Family | Contacts and behavior |
|---|---|
| Helmeted base, idle and walk | Four facings; stable feet/scale; armored weight; attached carried equipment |
| One-hand melee/compact emitter | Anatomical grip; compatible off-hand sidearm or shield and independent layers |
| Heavy two-hand melee | Primary/support grips; supported slash, impact or thrust and recovery |
| Two-hand energy rifle/carbine | Right primary and left support grip through ready, fire and recovery; distinguish burst rifle from single-shot carbine |
| Mounted offense | Back/shoulder sockets, bracing, actual firing direction; one installation |
| Support/technical action | Repair, signal, barrier or boost use matching actual cues |
| Hurt and guard | Brief clear reaction; shield/arm contact matches usable equipped parts |
| Rig Break and reboot | Nonlethal armored exhaustion/kneeling and supported recovery |
| Degraded two-hand use | One-arm-disabled pose/fallback matching reduced accuracy; both arms broken cannot attack |

Reuse compatible torso/leg movement and arm poses across weapon families. Heavy motions may require
specific torso frames; record actual pose compatibility rather than a new complete outfit. Empty
hands alone do not establish a two-hand action. Supply separable hand/forearm occlusion pieces when
needed, with matching frame IDs and exact placement. Game FX provide beams/pulses and impacts;
ordinary attacks do not require unique illustrated moves per item and identity.

Victory and elaborate choreography follow implemented needs. CY61/CY62 define signature rules and
warnings before dedicated wind-ups/splashes. Helmet-off combat variants follow an authored scene or
mechanic; do not automatically duplicate this first batch. Dialogue/profile expression work remains
under its separate shared outfit standard.

## Authoring work and deliverables

1. Begin with the first real recruit's helmeted approved armor. Return a clean body, reusable pose
   and occlusion layers as needed, plus equipped fitting previews using CY58's canonical sources.
2. Supply native alpha masters and exports: body/look ID, fixture version, animation, facing, frame
   index/timing, foot anchor, hand contacts, mount sockets, depth order and subject bounds. Record
   palette masks, compatible equipment/pack families, SHA-256 and exact approval scope.
3. Keep the body independent of weapon geometry. A fitted preview proves the composite but does
   not replace reusable layers/metadata. Cropping a neutral full-body image is not an animated set.
4. Review the rifle, heavy melee, mixed-hand and shield/support combinations with CY57/CY58. Check
   foot drift, arm loss, grips, Back gear, cover, front/back and all four facings through motion.
5. Preview in authored Training and actual boarding. Correct the fixture, canonical equipment or
   character pose at source and regenerate affected exports. Record source approval and integrated
   game evidence separately. Expand by tier after the first cohort works in campaign.
6. Cover all fifty playable identities with per-body/pose compatibility and missing-row status.
   Preserve each identity's specific head/ear housings, tail, proportions, armor motif and palette.

Review near 70-pixel figure height and 844 × 390 landscape, on light/dark terrain, behind cover and
beside actual opponents. Keep rings, floor shadows, FX, numbers and UI out of raw art. Existing
Combat Sprites collections retain source/review history and feed the versioned private publication
contract. Native master, runtime export, Platform publication and campaign integration are distinct.

## Done when

Required identities have approved helmeted bodies, needed pose compatibility and usable CY57
attachment exports. Planet-funded equipment is recognizable in the next real battle, including the
two-handed Laser rifle, damaged parts, reload and art-release replacement. Record missing art and
physical iPhone findings through CY57/CY37. Delete with a Done row on completion.
