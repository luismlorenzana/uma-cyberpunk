# CY59 Art authoring · 3D-to-pixel armored character sprites

## Outcome and linked work

Create reusable rigged 3D authoring sources and render pixel sprite frames for the real campaign.
The owner adopted this method in D76. Use the [first-character brief](../../design/combat-sprites-request.md),
[CY57 integration](CY57.md), [CY58 equipment](CY58.md), [CY60 opponents](CY60.md),
[CY63 Story](CY63.md) and [approved art direction](../../design/art-direction.md).

The first delivery is one equipped real recruit. The complete scope remains the fifty playable
identities, reusing approved identity assets across matching ally/enemy appearances. Expand after
the source/export method works in campaign.

## Source and first loadout

Pin the recruit from the private first-cohort handoff, her approved helmeted armor and identity
references. Preserve the actual armor design, proportions, silhouette, palette, emblem, tail,
helmet/ear housings and anatomical asymmetry. Build an editable low-detail model and reusable rig.
Document any missing construction reference. A new mesh/export has its own review status.

Start with the existing issued Training loadout: two-handed Laser rifle in the right equipment
slot, left equipment slot empty, Bulwark on the Back. Both anatomical hands contact the rifle.
Use the canonical equipment sources with their exact draft/approved status. CY58's approved shield
and compact normal-shoulder pod remain references for subsequent loadouts; no new armor is implied.

## Reproducible source and pixel profile

1. Return a native editable project, preferably Blender, with linked/packed resources and a batch
   render/export script. Record tool/version, model/rig IDs, object/collection names, material zones,
   coordinate system and named hand/mount sockets. Keep equipment independently replaceable.
2. Render front, back, left and right from the same model and pose, preserving asymmetry. Use one
   fixed orthographic camera and light setup at 35 degrees downward. Check the direction-to-board
   mapping and ground root with CY57.
3. Begin with the brief's 128 × 128 native frames, ground anchor `(64, 120)` and roughly 96-pixel
   standing figure. Produce a 2× nearest-neighbor candidate compatible with the shared 256-pixel
   cell/foot convention. Use clear pixel clusters, broad cel shadows and approved palette shapes.
4. Return four equipped standing renders and a phone-size/enlarged contact sheet first. Generate
   body-only or object/depth passes from the same project when needed for QA/composition. Record
   cleanup steps and hashes so model revisions can regenerate exports.

The executable atlas/action format and final profile remain CY57's checked contract. A 3D source,
illustration or contact sheet alone does not establish a runtime-ready sprite set.

## Motion and expansion

After standing works on the campaign board, export a short walk loop, rifle ready/fire/recovery,
hit/guard reaction and nonlethal Rig Break/reboot from the same rig. Agree frame IDs, durations,
holds, foot alignment and effect events with CY57. The game supplies actual pulses, impacts,
numbers and results. Preserve contacts and occlusion through motion and degraded arm states.

Next prove another actual loadout with the same source. Share body movement and compatible family
poses across one-hand gear, shield, heavy melee, rifle/carbine, mounted offense and support.
Model equipment separately and use shared palette/material zones. Render supported cohort variants
or reusable passes under CY57's measured strategy rather than commissioning a body per combination.
Helmet-off variants and signature choreography follow their authored needs in CY61/CY62.

## Campaign handoff and acceptance

Return project, exporter, textures/materials, source hashes, exact rig and body/look IDs, camera
profile, frame sequences, timings, anchors/bounds, proposed atlas and requested unsupported fields.
Keep source-design approval, model review, pixel export, runtime import, Platform publication and
campaign acceptance separate. Existing approved drawings retain their exact original scope.

Development starts with a fully equipped atlas for the first exact rig, with accurate fallbacks
for other loadouts. Review in the real recruit's Training and actual boarding mission at 844 × 390:
four directions, cover, targeting, foot drift, contacts, saved facing, actual hits, disabled parts,
Rig Break/reboot, interruption and art replacement. Physical iPhone findings remain CY37.
