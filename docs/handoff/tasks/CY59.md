# CY59 Art authoring · Full-proportion cel-shaded character sprites

## Outcome and linked work

Create polished full-proportion rigged 3D sources and render clean cel-shaded 2D sprite frames
for the real campaign. The owner selected this finish/proportion direction in D77, retaining D76's
reusable source method. Use the [first-character brief](../../design/combat-sprites-request.md),
[CY57 integration](CY57.md), [CY58 equipment](CY58.md), [CY60 opponents](CY60.md),
[CY63 Story](CY63.md) and [approved art direction](../../design/art-direction.md).

The first delivery is one equipped real recruit. The complete scope remains the fifty playable
identities, reusing approved identity assets across matching ally/enemy appearances. Expand after
the source/export method works in campaign.

## Source and first loadout

Pin the recruit from the private first-cohort handoff, her approved helmeted armor and identity
references. Preserve the actual armor design, proportions, silhouette, palette, emblem, tail,
helmet/ear housings and anatomical asymmetry. Match the full adult proportions of her approved
full-body reference. Build a polished editable model and reusable rig; review helmet/visor shape,
chest/abdomen, hips, limbs, joints and boots against that design. Use appropriate curve normals
and plate edges. Document missing construction references. A new mesh/export has its own review.

Start with the existing issued Training loadout: two-handed Laser rifle in the right equipment
slot, left equipment slot empty, Bulwark on the Back. Both anatomical hands contact the rifle.
Use the canonical equipment sources with their exact draft/approved status. CY58's approved shield
and compact normal-shoulder pod remain references for subsequent loadouts; no new armor is implied.

## Source-quality checkpoint and clean render profile

1. Return a native editable project, preferably Blender, with linked/packed resources and a batch
   render/export script. Record tool/version, model/rig IDs, object/collection names, material zones,
   coordinate system and named hand/mount sockets. Keep equipment independently replaceable.
2. Render front, back, left and right from the same model and pose, preserving asymmetry. Use one
   fixed orthographic camera and light setup at 35 degrees downward. Check the direction-to-board
   mapping and ground root with CY57.
3. Follow the brief's 512 × 512 antialiased master, ground anchor `(256, 480)` and approximately
   416-pixel figure height. Return master-size and clean filtered runtime candidates with normalized
   anchors and scale metadata. Use broad controlled cel shadows, smooth alpha coverage and
   art-directed contour weight. The prior palette snapping, binary-alpha and pixel-outline cleanup
   are removed from this export path.
4. Return source-quality body/three-quarter views beside the approved reference, then four equipped
   standing renders at inspection size and proposed 80/100 CSS-pixel display sizes. Generate
   body-only or object/depth passes from the same project when needed for QA/composition. Record
   cleanup steps and hashes so model revisions can regenerate exports.

Reuse the earlier prototype's rig/exporter work where useful, adapting mesh construction, bone
lengths and contacts to the full-proportion source. Keep that prototype and its hashes as history.
The executable atlas/action format, sampling and display scale remain CY57's checked contract. A 3D source,
illustration or contact sheet alone does not establish a runtime-ready sprite set.
