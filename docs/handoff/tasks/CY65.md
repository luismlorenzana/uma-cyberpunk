## Routing contract

| Context | Default role / authoring rule |
|---|---|
| Shore/world conversation | `street` |
| Public ship spaces and ordinary off-duty conversation | `ship-casual` |
| Private cabin, confession, seated evening, bed and morning | `quarters`; retain the scene's authored pose |
| Staffed operational bridge or station | `pilot` |
| Ordinary welcome/ship briefing | `ship-casual`; an explicitly staged operational or equipped briefing keeps its matching role |
| Equipped ground mission | `armor` |
| Explicit sealed/helmeted mission | `armor-helmet` |

A scene's explicit supported look takes precedence. Being docked at a planet does not make a ship
interior a shore scene. A `briefing` trigger alone does not determine whether the crew is off duty,
operating a station or already equipped. Story reviews the actual place, activity and suit continuity.
Missing Ship Casual art retains a readable neutral/previous-role fallback and never changes a save.
