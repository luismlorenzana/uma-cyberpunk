## Workflow

1. Maintain one private production ledger for all 50 individual stories, 25 fixed duos, 150 recoveries, 5 capitals, all expedition routes and Act IV. Include owner decisions, actor IDs, phases, canonical references, art needs, role/gear/cue, choice or condition, source lock, milestone release, evidence and outstanding work. Use the current checked-out revision as intake, and keep earlier snapshots as evidence.
2. Accept versioned Story exports and three Art lane manifests. Verify referenced content, coverage, readability choices, battle inputs, scripted result, asset kind, scope and checksum. Track neutral and background placeholders and retire them through the same paths. Distinguish draft, original approval, destination approval, staged and selected status.
3. Build candidates with stable artifact IDs, validated scene graph, generated start map, specific actual profiles and scope. Use an explicit fallback for missing art in local review. Keep private data and originals out of Git and other games' scope. Carry previously approved outfits, scenes, silhouettes and assets with verified destination pins where they still match.
4. Stage, play and accept one integrated release per milestone from its real campaign save flow. Inspect deterministic test reports, source/art data in-game and environment state actually dictated by a saved result. Exercise staging in install-like conditions, missing/slow assets, resume, retries, accessibility, fast and reduced-motion settings. Batch the checks for commonly reused character variants, generic rooms and motion families.
5. Publish approved art through the existing Cyberpunk Platform and game lanes. Verify exact bytes, immutable release, simultaneous manifest approval and pointer preconditions, scope-specific promotion and rollback. Use a fresh save and explicit campaign content selection during release. Verify failure, safe retry, corrupt candidate and pointer races.
6. Report readiness across Story, General Art, Character Art, Sprite Art and Engineering for each chapter and milestone. Surface measurable blockers early and update the linked design, production packet, story slot and runtime consumer together. Preserve a durable handover of outstanding work and the deployed versions for future updates.
