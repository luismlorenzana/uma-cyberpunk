# CY24 Ship command and defense

## Why

Ship battles put the Captain in command of officers whose training matters when a threat appears.

## Context

Use [CY23](CY23.md) for ship capabilities, the first relief escort in [CY40](CY40.md) for the authored sortie and [CY34](CY34.md) for bridge presentation. Read the [rework brief](../../design/campaign-rework.md), existing ship posts, reactor and sortie logic.

The private `baseline-6` relay defense plays from Selden's port and uses the [ship command contract](../../technical/campaign-content.md#ship-command). Chrome exercised a gunner drill, a paid launch, a loss, a free retry, a committed order after reload and the authored victory debrief. The bridge reuses the dock backdrop as a placeholder. Dedicated officer and ship art, encounter cinematics and the physical iPhone playthrough remain open.

## Decisions made

Use a ship command encounter with visible threats, limited power and short crew responses. Use the same encounter rules for later planetary offense and ship-to-ship engagements. Reactor allocation during an encounter and strategic resource costs are distinct.

## Do

- Implement assignments and training for pilot, shields, gunnery and engineering. Give each a distinct command and visible outcome. Use a known fallback for missing or changed assignments.
- Show enemy intent, shield sections, fighter interceptions or strikes, weapon fire, and power redirection. Resolve each order into state changes and cues so callouts and portraits reflect what occurred. Persist the battle and its outcome for resume.
- With story, author a defense objective that matters to the landing planet, with lines for the officers and relevant results. With art, integrate the bridge, officers and silhouettes, and test the threat and damage states at phone size.

## Done when

A real campaign defense mission can be played, lost or retried and resumed. Power allocation, officer skill and fighter orders change meaningful outcomes. The player's orders, the crew's lines and the objective status agree.

## Deliver

Record the encounter and cue contract for the later approaches and the final operation, including the physical iPhone review. Follow the repo PR workflow, delete this packet when complete and add its Done row.
