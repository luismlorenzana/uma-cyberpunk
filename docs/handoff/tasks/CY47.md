# CY47 Civilian handovers and officer retirements

## Why

Pay off each officer's first request by showing who now keeps the recovered service running.

## Context

Use [CY46](CY46.md), the private five-system retirement chapters, and the sector assignments from [CY39](CY39.md). These are individual goals and handovers following fleet victory.

## Game implementation

- Add one accessible handover entry per officer after fleet victory and the related sector's prerequisites. Keep scenes discoverable from the roster and relevant port. Apply handover and retirement once, update availability and succession, and retain the person's story record and relationships.
- Support continued visits and the authored arrangements for the former commander. Keep local projects, duties and reserves consistent with the civilian successor. Let the Captain complete handovers in any supported order and see which have happened.
- Wire the last watch to the authored handover milestones. Keep relevant aftermath scenes available from completed saves and validate a release replacement across a pending handover.

## Story and art

Integrate the ten scripted handovers and their choices. Show the civilian service in use under its successor, with each opening keepsake or local duty receiving the agreed payoff. Review the final fleet and personal beats together and reflect continued bonds in their own scenes.

## Done when

A saved victory can complete all ten handovers in at least two orders. Check one mixed relationship run, custody outcomes, retained bonds, succession and unavailable duty slots. The last watch reflects the saved handovers, survives reload and leaves the campaign in its agreed postwar state. Pass the private release and device checklist to [CY36](CY36.md).
