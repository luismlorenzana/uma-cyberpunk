# CY46 Joint fleet approach and authority breach

## Why

Give all ten commands a visible job in the final operation and let the Captain finish it with a practical squad.

## Context

Use [CY45](CY45.md), the private joint operation script, the approach and ground primitives from [CY28](CY28.md), and the command and squad work in [CY33](CY33.md) through [CY35](CY35.md).

## Game implementation

- Bind the authored ten command contributions to calls or resolved actions. Show what they protect and how they affect the approach. Keep the boarding squad within the supported size and eligibility rules. An authored deputy can cover a command for a temporary return aboard.
- Carry the normal entry and the emitter-opened service entry through the ground breach. Protect the civilian route and make navigation and authority controls distinct and readable. Freeze contributions, crew availability, gear, entry and paid prep in the launch or battle state. Restore both entries on reload and retry.
- Resolve the armistice and independent civilian crossing as a saved campaign outcome. Hand the victory flag to [CY47](CY47.md) with per-command outcomes available for aftermath.

## Story and art

Use the fleet script for the ten commands, rescue contact, opposing council and independent passage. Review friendly and civilian silhouettes, receiver controls, objective feedback and the first verified crossing against the resolved play state. Reuse the prototype layouts as staging where they match the final objectives.

## Done when

Launch from a mobilized campaign, win both entries and finish the agreed authority objective while preserving civilian passage. Verify loss and free retry after launch, save and resume through the breach, and visible contributions from officers both ashore and temporarily aboard. Victory opens the handovers once.
