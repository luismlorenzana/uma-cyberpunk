# CY30 Planetary supplies for squad preparation

## Why

Make a planet's exports change what the squad can take into a ground battle.

## Context

Use the [port ledger](../../technical/campaign-content.md#ports-and-supply), [Hangar rig rules](../../game/hangar.md) and [Selden boarding flow](../../technical/campaign-content.md#occupied-routes-and-ground-missions). Integrate after CY29 if both change the save schema.

## Game implementation

- Add a campaign Hangar entry before the first Selden boarding. Reuse the existing rig part, capacity and slot validation. Show recipes and available materials, the equipped rig, and the fielded result. Start with Alloy exported from a port and two distinct craftable options, such as armor protection and a Back unit.
- Debit the displayed materials and grant the part in one saved transaction. Equip and swap the resulting gear on the campaign crew record. Begin with the crew's existing starter or issued equipment. Weigh draft recipe costs against exports, reserves and travel so the opening stays playable when funds are spent elsewhere.
- Freeze the fitted rig into the normal sortie input. Preserve the paid inventory and frozen equipment across reload, loss and retry. Carry preexisting campaign saves into the Hangar with their current crew and balances. Keep Training's issued equipment available as its teaching loadout.

## Story and art

Write a dock or workshop handoff that identifies who supplied the Alloy and what it changes on the boarding. Brief the part icons, fitted rig silhouette and deployment cue together. Use provisional images under the intended asset IDs.

## Done when

Play from shipment to purchase, fitting, boarding and debrief in the campaign. Compare two preparations on the same encounter and trace the difference in Forecast or legal actions. Verify insufficient stock, a repeated purchase action, reload before deployment and loss followed by free retry.
