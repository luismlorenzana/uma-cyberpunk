# CY55 Art authoring · Pilot-suit ship combat splashes

## Outcome and linked work

Create the character and boss pilot-suit splash art and reusable ship-combat kit. Use the
[delivery contract](../../design/campaign-art-delivery.md), [CY54 Development](CY54.md) and
[CY56 Story](CY56.md). Use the current approved Cyberpunk suit masters and likeness by exact identity;
the boss's neutral likeness is shared across enemy and ally roles. References/drafts can begin while
CY54 establishes the first playable layout.

Match the [approved art and visual world direction](../../design/art-direction.md) (D66) across
character layers, bridge plates, protected craft and reusable combat effects.

## Asset families

| Family | Scope and composition |
|---|---|
| Playable operating pose | One primary transparent operating/action composition for each of the fifty crew, in her approved pilot suit; fit the actual duty and shared station plate |
| Boss command pose | One primary command/communications composition for each of the fifty bosses, in her approved suit; consistent identity when her role changes |
| Talking profiles | Crop the matching approved neutral suit portrait and use the existing character/boss expression packets; keep it a distinct overlay |
| General combat kit | Bridge/station plates, ship and fighter silhouettes, shields, firing/impact effects, target indicators and reusable communications treatment |
| Protected objectives | Preserve the Rainward transport; author the works ferry and hired tender against their actual integrity-panel framing |

Role-specific alternate poses are recorded where a scene needs them. Do not multiply every character
by every possible station before checking whether her primary pose and a different station plate
communicate the order. A new action splash does not require a separate face-expression library.

## Authoring sequence

1. Pin the current pilot-suit masters, likeness references, pose/look IDs and hashes. Check the live
   authoring collections; approved suit updates may already be waiting to be integrated. Keep native
   transparent pose art separate from profile crops and generic plates.
2. With CY56, establish the first real escort's actor, station, order, response and result. Produce one
   crew operating shot, one boss communication shot and the required shared effects. Review them in
   CY54 with real dialogue, choices, profile and integrity displays before expanding the cast.
3. Make shield, gun, fighter and power actions visually distinct. Effects represent only outcomes the
   game emits; their intensity or silhouette must not imply extra damage, a new weapon or a destroyed
   ship. Retain civilian craft readability and room for the actual target/emitter states.
4. Expand crew and boss compositions by the encounter registry and recruitment tiers. Preserve each
   participant's palette, outfit details and physical posture. Provide pose-specific profiles only
   when a reviewed shot truly requires one; reuse approved neutral/profile crops by default.
5. Review local defenses, fleet shots, occupied approaches and later civilian craft with the same
   kit. Keep the narrative's strike/withdrawal/arrival outcomes consistent with the final image.

## Return and completion

Return asset IDs, source versions, native size/alpha, hashes, approvals, pose anchors, actor bounds,
portrait base/crops, layer order, effect origin and text-safe preview. State the exact cue and outcome
represented by every effect/variant. Keep native unmodified sources and approval history privately.
Development records the album source and the campaign manifest where it is actually integrated.

Done when the required cast compositions and locked general kit have approved exports, correct
identity/outfit/profile matches and in-game evidence at landscape phone size, including the escort,
approach, boss call and later protected craft. Record CY37 findings and delete with a Done row.
