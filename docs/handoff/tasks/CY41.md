# CY41 Nacre relief and reserve operations

## Why

Test the two-operation contract at a port that must protect food, clean water and rescue tenders.

## Context

Use the private Nacre, civilian-route and case-ledger briefs. Build on [CY40](CY40.md), [CY32](CY32.md), [CY33](CY33.md), [CY34](CY34.md) and [CY35](CY35.md).

## Game implementation

- Play the ration tender escort, the independent reserve rescue, their local ground objectives and both appointments. Show which convoy or reserve is being protected. Match seals, escort or extraction constraints to authored objectives and the selected crew kit.
- Surface the port's reserves before export. Demonstrate the effect of the restored relief route and rescue command on the local ledger. Preserve separate sector outcomes at the shared berth.
- File the required ration record and the separate reserve testimony. Unlock the relief watch after the two appointments. Attach an optional two-officer scene to its own prerequisites.

## Story and art

Integrate the reef chart, tender, public reserve scale and civilian dive or sealed-work look. Pair bridge calls and silhouettes with the actual escort result. Align the recovered records and joint relief report with the case ledger.

## Done when

Win both operations in both orders, take a paid escort loss and retry without paying again, and verify that each service changes on its own resolution. Restore a pending save and confirm the required case can be read before the optional testimony and joint watch.
