# CY56 Story authoring · Ship command dialogue and cinematic cues

## Outcome and dependencies

Turn resolved ship orders into short, coherent crew and boss sequences in the real campaign. Use the
[delivery contract](../../design/campaign-art-delivery.md), [CY54 implemented contract](../../technical/command-cinematics.md) and
[CY55 Art](CY55.md). Preserve all current mission objectives, costs, outcomes and service prerequisites.

## Authoring work

1. Inventory the existing ship encounters by campaign tier, mission purpose, acting station,
   opponent/commander, protected objective, target and outcome. Identify current order-line templates
   and any unsupported shot or mechanic request. Bind by stable encounter and action/result keys.
2. Write the First Watch escort sequence first: Captain order, actual officer acknowledgement,
   action cue and concise factual result. Cover shields, gunnery, interception/strike and engineering
   responses supported by the encounter. Let the numeric result fill exact damage/shield amounts.
3. Add the first occupied approach and boss communications. Distinguish a normal win, optional emitter
   result, failed approach and retry. A ship that withdraws or strikes must not be described as
   destroyed. Keep custody/boarding outcome separate from winning the ship encounter.
4. For each cinematic cue, return speaker, expression, pose/plate, intended effect, occurrence and
   skip behavior. Use the existing exact character, boss-enemy and boss-ally expression tags. Record
   expressions absent from the art pack as pending authoring rather than new arbitrary synonyms.
5. Keep missing-station fallback lines, reassigned officers and ashore contributors coherent. Personal
   voice can vary while the same order still communicates the same mechanical result. Avoid dialogue
   that claims a station specialization or unlocked weapon the frozen input does not provide.
6. Expand to local defense, fleet participation and later-tier civilian escorts. The works ferry and
   hired tender keep their actual names, stakes, routes and arrivals. Record repeated, once-only and
   branch-specific cues explicitly; fast orders retain a short understandable result.

## Handoff

Deliver checked private script/cue tables keyed to the implemented CY54 fixture, a conditional-outcome
matrix, exact speaker/expression/asset references and a scoped revision list. Provide baseline lines
early so the first mission is playable. A cue table is a proposal until its fields are supported by
development; retain a list of requests requiring parser or mechanic work.

Every approved sequence names the matching CY55 source revision and the campaign release used to
review it. Keep spoilers, dialogue and cast identities in the private book. Preserve existing quest,
scene and encounter IDs when replacing narration.

## Verification and completion

Play the actual escort, approach and one later civilian escort with normal and fast playback, loss,
free retry and reload after a committed order. Compare the line and picture against the result log.
Review actor changes, absent crew, both approach outcomes and a compatible content release swap.

Done when every registered command outcome has a coherent checked sequence or explicit approved
generic line, the named campaign checkpoints agree with their art, and no scene implies an unsupported
rule. Record integration/device findings and delete with a Done row.
