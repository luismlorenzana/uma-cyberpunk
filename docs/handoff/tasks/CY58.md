## Visual direction and source intake

Keep Hardline Anime contours, angular cel shading and readable silhouettes. Reconcile the latest
approved sources first. The equipment session's private index records selected muted industrial
materials: graphite/gunmetal, restrained silver and warm gray, black grips and reusable character
palette zones. Original emitters, cells and field elements express the energy technology. The design
itself should be fictional; changing the caption on a real firearm replica does not establish it.

Retain approved designs and fitting studies as references. Record exactly which source is reused,
adapted or replaced and its approval scope. Pending drawings and derivatives retain their own review
status. Read the current equipment index and generation-gap report. Track source approval, usable
export, Platform publication and game integration separately.

## Authoring work and return contract

The first sprite fitting follows the [current baseline image brief](../../design/combat-sprites-request.md)
(D75): one helmeted character in four directions, drawn from a consistent slightly elevated
viewpoint, with matched clean-body and two-handed Laser rifle previews. Match that painted elevation
in the equipment. Record the exact approved or draft rifle source. Numerical attachment exports
follow the reviewed baseline and CY57 fixture; a fitting preview has its own review status.

1. Establish canonical family design/fitting references: silhouette, scale against approved armor,
   contacts/mounts, palette zones and movement clearance. Start with the two-hand rifle, heavy melee,
   mixed one-hand rig, shield and one role pack. Use modules for actual mechanical distinctions.
2. Provide inventory/workshop icons and Hangar/deploy exports for all catalog rows. Check the seven
   current project-recipe icons. Shared sources are allowed; item text/stats are generated by UI.
3. With CY57/CY59, supply transparent equipment and required front/behind splits for four drawn
   facings. Match the executable fixture: primary/support grips, Back/shoulder sockets, frame IDs,
   placements, bounds and depth order. Concept fittings are references until numerical metadata
   and usable exports are returned. Preserve asymmetry rather than mirroring equipment.
4. Return reusable masks for structural/base, accent, trim and emblem/material zones as needed.
   Preserve native masters and hashes. Character adaptation should reuse the design and masks
   instead of repainting the full character sheet for every equipment choice.
5. Check carry, movement, firing/strike, guard, disabled-arm and Rig Break poses with Sprite Art.
   Fingers occlude grips; both rifle contacts remain attached. Back gear clears hair/tail, cover
   and adjacent figures. Beams, impacts, warnings, rings and floor shadows belong to game FX.
6. Return a row per part: family ID, mode/module, source path/SHA-256, dimensions/alpha, masks,
   files, supported poses/facings, anchors, bounds, approval and integration status. Record shared
   source relationships so a canonical revision can regenerate all affected exports.
