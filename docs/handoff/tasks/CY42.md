# CY42 Cindervale yard and clinic operations

## Why

Make the two local services and their roles in the investigation legible through the ship and ground missions.

## Context

Use the private Cindervale and case-ledger briefs, [CY41](CY41.md) and the production, duty and repair deliveries in [CY32](CY32.md) and [CY33](CY33.md).

## Game implementation

- Play the yard bypass and defensive approach, and the clinic's separate heat and damage-control operation. Use the authored target, protected worker or craft, and repair or ground objective. Check the distinct issue kits and roster roles on the two ground entries.
- Apply yard and clinic consequences independently. Show how the civilian reserve for heat and repairs interacts with export, projects and the two sector appointments.
- File the conductor case and the optional cutoff record through their own outcomes. Open the local handover scene when both appointments are recorded.

## Story and art

Use the wage tally, clinic heat log and signed conductor drawing in their scenes. Make the protected craft and repaired circuit visible in the bridge and ground views. Show who is on duty in the final handover.

## Done when

Finish both operations in either order, with a useful officer or rig choice in each. The bypass can be restored while the clinic still needs help, and vice versa. Verify the supply ledger and investigation after reload and exercise one shared port project with both commands active.
