# CY36 Integrated campaign tuning and content

## Why

Check that the four pillars support each other from recruitment to the finale.

## Context

Use the integrated releases from [CY38](../../technical/campaign-content.md#recruitment-and-personal-stories) through [CY47](../../technical/campaign-content.md#tier-1-sectors-projects-and-fleet) and the four-pillar work in [CY32](../../technical/campaign-content.md#tier-1-sectors-projects-and-fleet) through [CY35](../../technical/campaign-content.md#tier-1-sectors-projects-and-fleet). Bring the approved story and art for [CY22](CY22.md), [CY23](CY23.md), [CY24](CY24.md) and [CY28](CY28.md) into the same playable release. Start with First Watch through both Arclight appointments and the joint watch.

The two integrated campaign replays reach Last Watch with real ship and ground results. The selected private draft and legacy pack both pass content validation. Use those checkpoints for the approved art and script review, balance tuning across the authored encounter set, and a full landscape playthrough.

## Game implementation

- Play a fresh opening and tune the timing of visits, relationship choices, shipments and gear preparation. Track when a player first understands each system and can use it in a consequential mission.
- Run the actual ship and ground encounters with different officer, gear and squad preparations. Record costs, encounter length, common losses and viable choices. Exercise friendship and romance, and compare the independent custody and appointment outcomes.
- Verify two operation orders per system, the five-case reveal, ten-command launch and both gate entrances. Run an early reveal with unfinished sectors and finish ten handovers. Check upgraded saves within the authored release, rollbacks for earlier draft profiles, a pending night, insufficient resources and transitions between missions.

## Story and art

Review the approved opening dialogue, crew looks, port and bridge presentation, and Training cues in context. Then review the later arcs and finale together. Check that each clue can be understood at the point it appears and pays off in the playable reveal. Use the [finale art handoff](../../design/art-requests/campaign-finale.md) for the gate's readable state changes and aftermath.

## Done when

The approved private preview plays from First Watch through the fleet action and personal handovers. Scripted checks cover supported branches and release replacement with a pending scene or encounter. Record balance and content findings for CY37's physical-device pass.
