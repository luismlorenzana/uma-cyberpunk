# CY36 Integrated campaign tuning and content

## Why

Check that the four pillars support each other from recruitment to the finale.

## Context

Use the deliverables from [CY31](CY31.md) through [CY35](CY35.md). Bring the approved story and art work from [CY22](CY22.md), [CY23](CY23.md), [CY24](CY24.md) and [CY28](CY28.md) into the same playable release. Start with the opening through Selden's home defense and use that arc as the first review checkpoint.

## Game implementation

- Play a fresh opening and tune the timing of visits, relationship choices, shipments and gear preparation. Track when a player first understands each system and can use it in a consequential mission.
- Run the actual ship and ground encounters with different officer, gear and squad preparations. Record costs, encounter length, common losses and viable choices. Keep friendship and staying aboard as ways to reach the finale and show the consequences of other choices.
- Verify faction ordering, evidence and ship and ground retry rules together. Confirm that the two gate entrances and the ending remain reachable on upgraded saves. Check skipped or replayed scenes, a pending night, insufficient resources, and transitions between missions.

## Story and art

Review the approved opening dialogue, crew looks, port and bridge presentation, and Training cues in context. Then review the later arcs and finale together. Check that each clue can be understood at the point it appears and pays off in the playable reveal. Use the [finale art handoff](../../design/art-requests/campaign-finale.md) for the gate's readable state changes and aftermath.

## Done when

The approved private preview plays a fresh Selden arc and reaches the approved finale. Scripted checks cover the supported branches and a release replacement with a pending scene or encounter. Record balance and content findings for CY37's physical-device pass.
