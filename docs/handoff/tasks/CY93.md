## Inventory and authoring

Use [CY111](CY111.md) for general scenes, locations and ship-space art. It supersedes the earlier location-by-location background list. Start from the approved shared library of 5–10 setting images and document setting reuse and readable dialogue crops.

Own the general fixtures that carry gameplay information, including encounter terrain, cover, room markers, map icons, route geometry and ship or ground impact effects. Pin approved sources and document the exact encounter, system, state or interface that uses them. Verify targeting, occlusion, safe areas and reduced motion in the campaign.
