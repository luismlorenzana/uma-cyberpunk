Cover origin places, civilian starting berths, each ship approach, protected craft, Palinode ships, boarding and surface map kits, retreat, capture and status effects. Add missing reusable quarry, repair, engineering and crafting props.
- **Act II.** Deliver rehabilitation, shared quarters, investigation and civic props, base exterior and rooms, meaningful interactive site states for all duos, obsolete/working route markers, supplies and construction states. Cover all five capitals, unique approach landmarks, bridge/shore and tactics maps and effects for clearance, defeat, release and revived civilian service. Provide shared environmental language for the first Seraine evidence.
- **Act III.** Deliver Homestretch dock/launch, shared expedition hull and its bridge, engineering, cargo, quarters and return, plus outfit-neutral room framing. Produce acted-on sky and route plates, opponent ship forms, common Seraine world/court arena kits, per-route landmarks and key props. Account for 10 friendly positions, camera and sightline needs, elevation, cover, markers, targeting, battle readability and linked clue/target states.
- **Act IV.** After CY90, add final approach, arena and outcome states, shared story backgrounds and soft-ending locations. Coordinate multiple Queen silhouettes and attack warnings with Character and Sprite Art.
- **Shared FX and UI.** Verify damage, missile or fictional laser traces, emitter, hull and shields, status and readiness markers, warning/action/result, nonlethal release, fade, approach and retry reuse. Assign each shared icon/prop/FX to a real existing need. Account for reduced motion and target size for frequently used controls.
- Export layers, map components, metadata and optimized assets in the current formats. Pin native sources and approved derivations. Review gameplay and actor posed areas, depth, alpha, movement clearance, occlusion and landscape crop in the real scene. Attach the route and state and report missing slots by family.
