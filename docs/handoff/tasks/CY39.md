# CY39 Sector appointments and crew availability

## Why

An officer's commission changes where she can serve. Her personal choices determine which relationships continue and where people live.

## Context

Build on [CY38](CY38.md), the private military campaign and academy bibles, and the existing [shared evening and home contracts](../../technical/campaign-content.md#shared-evenings-and-home-readiness). Preserve the legacy pair-home rules for packs that use them.

## Game implementation

- Give each authored sector a stable ID, its officer, its liberation and appointment milestones, and its local post. Derive or persist the officer's assignment consistently so a saved crew member can serve aboard, command ashore, or return for a specific operation with a deputy in place. Use that assignment for launch eligibility, station and duty rosters, quarters and visits.
- Apply the appointment after the authored liberation and civilian accountability milestones. Free temporary ship posts and make the local contribution available in the same saved change. Retain the officer's rig, experience, training and bonds. Interpret completed legacy home choices with their release's existing contract.
- Give the former commander a separate custody and release outcome. Keep legal custody, employment, romance, shared evenings and shared or separate quarters independently resolvable through the story. Provide an authored ashore visit where appropriate. Keep all required progression available with friends-only enabled and after a pending scene resumes.

## Story and art

Use one paired Arclight appointment as the first handoff. Script the civilian appointment, cleared watch slot, staffing at the new post and the former commander's distinct disposition. Use existing bridge, office and cabin views for staging. Agree on the status and art IDs for the roster, local command and room views.

## Done when

Play that appointment with two different relationship choices and at least one local custody outcome. Reload on both sides of it. Verify assignment, posts, allowed deployments and visit choices, including a bounded return aboard and departure in a campaign fixture. The other sector at the same port can keep different status. A legacy preview still reads its own completed home outcome.
