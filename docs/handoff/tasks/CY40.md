# CY40 Arclight paired operations

## Why

Prove that two authored operations can share one civilian port and each produce an independent local resolution.

## Context

Use [CY38](CY38.md) and [CY39](CY39.md), the private Arclight scripts and case ledger, and [the ground mission contract](../../technical/campaign-content.md#occupied-routes-and-ground-missions). The review pack already stages both operations. Its story tests supplied ground victories and its joint watch is a standalone chapter.

## Game implementation

- Play the relief escort, both relay entries, the separate rescue approach and the two ground objectives through real launches, encounters, debriefs and appointments. Bind any borrowed map to a kit, objective and success or retry rule that matches the script. Exercise both tactical entries and a failed attempt with saved resources.
- Add campaign prerequisites for the joint watch and an optional cross-crew scene. Use stable quest or outcome IDs and evaluate each prerequisite at launch and commit. The two operations may progress in either order. Completing one leaves the other's local mission available and preserves its own release, assignment and local support.
- File the required case from the designated liberation debrief. Record the second testimony in its own file. Show both in the investigation and verify that the testimony adds context while the required record is complete on every custody and relationship branch.

## Story and art

Review the relief manifest, service record, escort silhouette, relay route and tram or archive objective against their actual outcomes. Stage the required props and expression cues. Brief the joint-watch art with both officers in the same civilian location and review the line that changes with the order of play.

## Done when

From First Watch, finish both operations in each order and see both appointments and the joint watch. Use both relay entries, play actual ground wins, take a loss and free retry, and reload an encounter. Compare the case and testimony after alternative choices. Another officer elsewhere on the roster remains available to start her operation. Record encounter, save and story fixtures for the following systems.
