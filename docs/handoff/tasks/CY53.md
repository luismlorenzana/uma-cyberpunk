3. Bind the first real shipment to the Hangar and its funded fitting/refit. Specify the cargo, who
   receives it, any single-use acknowledgement and the destination of the material. Give CY52 the
   exact facility, cargo and handover states that must be visually distinct.
4. Cover shortage relief, unfinished work and an unavailable officer with brief coherent responses.
   State whether a beat occurs once, repeats, or changes after a project. Keep the game's nightly
   order and actual costs unchanged unless a mechanic request is agreed with CY51.
5. Preserve civilian control, local work and resettled officers' contributions after liberation.
   Review later-tier ferry, school, receiver and arrival services against the shared room/prop kit.
   Distinct local services can share a location plate while retaining their own words and props.
6. Keep dating, custody and command appointments independent. A commander in local custody is not
   casually present in a ship lounge. Reconcile travel and physical presence with P1 visits and P3
   station calls. An image replacement must not introduce a new plot gate.
