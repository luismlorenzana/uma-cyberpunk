# CY52 Art authoring · Ship locations, civilian facilities and management props

## Outcome and linked work

Author reusable location plates and state/prop art for the management pillar. Use the
[delivery contract](../../design/campaign-art-delivery.md), [CY51 Development](CY51.md) and
[CY53 Story](CY53.md). Reuse CY49's ship welcome area and cabin geometry. Inventory the existing
First Watch environment masters before requesting replacement artwork.

Use the [approved art and visual world direction](../../design/art-direction.md) (D66) and the
hash-approved private cel-style source set for these room, facility and prop families.

## Location families

| Family | Required subjects and variants |
|---|---|
| Command | Bridge and welcome/briefing area; preserve the ship's established layout |
| Living | Private/shared cabins, corridor and entrances; compatible seated, bed and morning framing |
| Social | Galley, lounge and observation deck; space for multiple speakers |
| Care/custody | Medbay, brig/interview area; distinct privacy and release/residence cues |
| Operations | Hangar/workshop, engineering/reactor, cargo hold and airlock |
| Planet management | Civilian berth, base office and the production facilities actually bound by CY51 |

This is the family inventory. The shared record locks exact reachable views and states before
production; a room with no interactive feature can still be a scene backdrop when CY53 binds it.
Do not author a separate room for every character or every routine transaction.

## Authoring work

1. Review the current ship bible and approved masters with CY53. Establish a location sheet showing
   spatial relationships, recurring doors/windows, lighting and materials. Return layout conflicts
   before drawing. Use the existing Cyberpunk style and the same ship as P1.
2. Author common plates with quiet dialogue and management-panel areas. Cabin variants keep the same
   furniture anchors as CY49. Preserve the existing background when it already satisfies the shot;
   record any approved reuse by hash. Day/evening/morning variants share geometry.
3. From CY51's actual state list, produce construction/completion and repair variants only where the
   game stores that distinction. Keep local civilian ownership visible after liberation. A building
   state can use a prop/layer swap rather than another full painting.
4. Create readable resources, cargo, refit and workshop props matched to the real recipe/ledger IDs.
   Coordinate weapons and equipment with CY58; those canonical designs supply their icons. Do not
   invent a visually distinct resource that has no game or story meaning.
5. Complete the first port shipment, fitting and refit sequence as a set. Review its images in the
   actual panels and scene transitions before expanding across the five systems and later services.

## Export and integration handoff

Return logical asset/state IDs, native files and dimensions, alpha, source versions, SHA-256 and
recorded approval scope. Supply subject bounds, layer placements, the saved predicate the image
represents, supported text/controls framing and phone-size review images. Use the CY48/CY51 fixture
for metadata. Keep campaign-local plates and prompts in ignored content; cast art stays in its
authoring collection. Identify authoritative sources and every reused file.

Review at 844 × 390 with actual management controls, both light/dark UI regions, text, portraits and
safe areas. Small props must read at their displayed size. Development records the private pack in
which each export is integrated, independently of album publication.

## Done when

The locked reachable location/state inventory has approved exports and coherent scene continuity,
including one complete funded-prep sequence. Required replacements and reuse are accounted for,
with no placeholder disguised as final art. Record CY51 integration evidence and delete with a Done row.
