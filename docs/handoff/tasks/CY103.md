# CY103 · Resource balance across ship and planets
Measure practical flow of fuel, provisions, alloy, income and spend through normal campaign play.

## Four-act balance results

Six scripted campaigns started at First Watch, liberated five capitals, returned five ten-person expeditions with one witness per court, then finished Act IV and returned to play. Each of the three profiles ran in two system orders with different authored duos. Story-only ran on issued gear, the required yard, and ordinary crew duties. The upgrade run used two salvage wins, fitted the resulting Runner legs, built and staffed Life Support, funded one planetary project and reinforced a cutter. Recovery started from actual campaign saves and deliberately depleted cargo at four checkpoints, including the Gate expedition.

| Profile | Main route | Alternate route | Extra supply and berth waits | Emergency relief |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Story only | 214 watches | 343 watches | 0 in both runs | 0 |
| Salvage and upgrades | 217 watches | 296 watches | 0 in both runs | 0 |
| Low-stock recovery | 230 watches | 354 watches | 15 on the main route, 10 on the alternate | 2 per run |

With the same conservative command choices on the main story-only route, local repairs cut travel from 76 to 30 legs. The route spent 24 Ship Alloy at remote docks. Homestretch repairs remain free. Emergency cutter relief shortened the drained expedition’s resupply wait from six watches to two on the main route. The starter and expedition cutters share the same daily relief receipt. Advancing and fighting still use the normal clock.

New recruits start with zero XP. First clears now pay 75/60/85/100 XP across Acts I–IV. In the six runs the selected leads finished their own Act I at Lv 3, entered Act III at Lv 7–10, entered the finale at Lv 11–14, and reached Laser Rank 10. Recommendations reflect the regular bridge, the capital battles and the ten-person finale ground formation separately. The six scripted runs met the displayed lower recommendations. Physical iPhone pacing stays in CY125.
