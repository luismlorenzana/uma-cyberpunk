# CY103 · Resource balance across ship and planets
Measure practical flow of fuel, provisions, alloy, income and spend through normal campaign play.

- Record jumps, upkeep, needed watches, relief, crafting and refit choices, production, and launch and recovery costs. Compare low- and high-recruitment routes, two system orders, optional investment and a failed voyage.
- Tune main-ship consumption, local stocks and shipments, decisions around the 25 base projects, and expedition budgets together. Give the player a useful forecast and a recovery route when stock is tight. Coordinate output with CY101 equipment and CY105 expedition detours.
- Update project reports and port states when prices, upgrade times or benefits change. Verify the visible amounts and real save debits agree.

The continuous verification route recorded 63 fuel shipments, 104 provision shipments, 18 extra supply watches and eight relief grants while clearing the campaign. The test topped up to each required cost using port shipments and supply rules. Treat the result as a reachability and logistics baseline. Compare it with normal travel, short campaigns and optional spending during the economy pass.

**Exit.** Report the resource curves, waiting and backtracking on tested routes. Demonstrate progress without repeated rescue requests on normal plans and a clear recovery path after avoidable overspending.
