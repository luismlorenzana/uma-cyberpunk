# CY32 Planet projects and ship refits

## Why

Give the Captain choices about developing a liberated base and building a ship that can reach farther and sustain its crew.

## Context

Use [planet-funded squad preparation](../../technical/campaign-content.md#planet-funded-squad-preparation), the [nightly ledger](../../technical/campaign-content.md#ports-and-supply), [homeworld return](../../technical/campaign-content.md#homeworld-return-and-defense), [ship build rules](../../game/ship-build.md) and [duties](../../game/duties.md). Start at liberated Selden and a connected port. Use resident jobs from the shared-evenings draft for base staffing, with the existing civilian crews available on the stay-aboard path.

## Game implementation

- Let the player fund local projects that trade construction cost and local upkeep for distinct outputs. Start with two choices, such as a supply depot and a fabrication shop. Show the local reserve, production and export consequences before commitment. Have resident crew contribute at the base and let civilian staffing support the stay-aboard path.
- Bring the drive and selected ship modules into the campaign build view. Author a further route upgrade that opens an optional deeper approach or a useful corridor. Keep early landing access across the five existing systems. Assign ship staff to relevant posts and show construction progress.
- Tie the ship's nightly bill to occupied quarters, brig and installed operating systems. Keep the shortfall recovery path. Resolve production, upkeep, projects and exports against one ledger. Preserve old cargo, infrastructure, and route access through migration.

## Story and art

Name who benefits from each project and write its completion scene. Brief the existing base state, both improvements, the ship modules and the lane states in one delivery. If a project adds an export, ship that export with a recipe that can spend it.

## Done when

Two base development choices produce visibly different exports and upkeep. A shipment can fund a refit and unlock its route. Test shortage, reserve protection, completion across reload and the saved effects of both residence paths.
