# CY35 Campaign squads and weapon roles

## Why

Make ground sorties depend on the team the Captain prepared and on how its weapons and armor expansions work together.

## Context

Use [CY30](CY30.md) for acquisition, [the battle rules](../../game/battle.md) and [the Hangar](../../game/hangar.md). Start with a later faction station fight and apply the working flow to boarding and the finale.

## Game implementation

- Give the campaign a squad picker with available crew, role, equipment and reasons someone is ashore, resting or otherwise unavailable. Retain each crew member's rig between missions and show any objective-required role or expansion. Author a bounded team size for each objective and reserve allied slots where that makes sense.
- Complete the melee and ranged lineup with distinct jobs for great blades, axes, lances, short guns, long precision and area weapons. Add the intended limited armor-mounted backup blade and cannon alongside shielding and repairs. Define capacity, charge or ammo, area targeting and broken-arm behavior. Version any changed battle behavior so saved input stays replayable.
- Use equipment, crew roles and prep resources to give the player more than one sound plan on the same map. Carry scenario qualifiers, forecast and legal actions through the normal battle entry point.

## Story and art

Write mission briefings that teach the mechanic they actually require. Pair each new weapon or mount with a silhouette, targeting cue, hit effect and damaged state. Add authored cover and objective placements to support the squad choices.

## Done when

Use planetary exports to equip a multi-member squad, then run a station objective with two viable compositions. Compare replayable outcomes for both, including parts breaking and a mounted backup action. Confirm the new squads can reach the gate on both residence paths.
