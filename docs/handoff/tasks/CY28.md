# CY28 Five faction campaigns and the final operation

## Why

The local liberations should lead to a shared goal against the alien power behind them.

## Context

The first complete arc starts with [CY22](CY22.md) and ends with the [homeworld return and defense](../../technical/campaign-content.md#homeworld-return-and-defense). Use the private Selden homeworld notes for the saved evidence and allied support example. Read [the enemy requirements](../../design/campaign-rework.md#the-enemy-behind-the-occupations), and review the private faction, pair and final-operation briefs with story and art.

## Decisions made

Build the remaining faction arcs as integrated playable deliveries. Each has its own resolution and a distinct piece of evidence. The final operation pays off the alien objective and uses the crew, planets and combat systems developed in those arcs.

## Do

- Split the remaining arcs and final operation into smaller delivery packets when the first arc establishes the content and asset load. In each arc, author the script, encounters and art for the same release. Expand weapon and expansion families in the missions that need them.
- Give the campaign a readable record of evidence. Validate the reveal and ending gates across supported faction orders, optional scenes and residential outcomes. Author what the alien enemy is trying to achieve and what the player must stop.
- Build and integrate the final operation from the agreed script and encounter brief. Carry allies, prepared resources and station choices into available support. Art must communicate threat identity, the objective's state changes, and the aftermath.
- Balance using campaign encounters and headless runs. Check iPhone pacing, touch targets, performance, save sync, resume and release of the approved private packs. Use the repo's production authorization and delivery rules.

## Done when

The five plots can be played through supported orders. The evidence establishes the final threat and objective in play. The player can reach, complete and recover the final operation across supported relationship and residence choices. The ending shows the consequence for the liberated homes and crew.

## Deliver

Record the final campaign and tuning decisions, validate CI and the iPhone play-through, and ship through the repo workflow. Delete this packet and add its Done row when the delivered campaign meets these checks.
