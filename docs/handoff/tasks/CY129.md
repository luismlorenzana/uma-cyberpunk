## Outcome and baseline

Close the progression gaps in the playable four-act campaign. A fresh player can choose an Uma subclass at Laser
Rank 1, clear her opening alone with issued gear, earn a targeted upgrade through optional play, and progress to
the same ten named members on her duo's first expedition. At the expedition shift the two Umas specialize in Laser.
The other eight supply winged melee, shields, healing and long-range control. Every battle has a preparation target
and every player-facing combat rule has help where the player encounters it.

The current ground reducer supports specialties and runs ordinary battles on rules 6 and ten-member expeditions
on rules 7. The prepared specialist path favors the older authored combat entries. In the audited four-act catalog,
367 ground encounter entries use the ordinary or expedition fitting paths, and Laser specialist practice is optional.
Expedition Medics currently get a rifle and Med unit. The campaign pays 50 XP per eligible participant on first clear,
uses 100 XP per level, and adds Core HP at levels 5, 10 and 15. The Workshop has seven Alloy recipes. Chapter `danger`
is authored and existing Operations gates evaluate story and sortie readiness. CY102 records scripted winning paths
across 367 ground and 186 ship encounters. CY126 station work, base dispatch and the calendar are live on the
four-act profile. [Specialist rules](../../technical/campaign-combat-specialists.md) ·

## A · Deployment and class progression

### Save and transition rules

1. Enable the specialist fitting through the active four-act ground preparation path for every eligible pre-shift
   Uma. Validate a declared exception where a story encounter genuinely needs a mandatory mount, fixed action or
   fitting. Store exceptional restrictions with the encounter and expose them in its gear step. Keep authored
   spawns, factions, objectives, rescue and Talk outcomes, obstacles, named opponents and boss cues.
2. At Rank 1 offer **Heal**, **Shield**, **Melee**, **Long Range**, **Support** and **None**. Recommend Heal on
   the first solo deploy and preselect it for a new first fitting, with an explicit choice before deployment. Keep
   an explicit None distinct from a crew member who has yet to choose. A pre-shift Uma can change her selection
   for free in gear preparation before the battle input is frozen. Ranks 1–5 advance the Laser track. The subclass
   is a single secondary discipline with a Rank 5 ceiling, using its published reduced-strength kit throughout.
   Earlier choices stay available while she is pre-shift.
3. Advance one Laser rank per eligible first-clear ground victory that froze the deployed Uma as a Laser specialist.
   Award that rank in the same result commit as XP and Story. Early training can demonstrate each effect without
   spending campaign supplies. A training win, salvage contract, retry, replay, bench slot and a ship victory give
   zero Laser rank. For old saves that already earned training ranks, preserve the recorded rank. Simulate the
   50 solo arcs to find ranks accessible by each first capture. If an authored route needs an effect sooner, use
   an explicit personal milestone floor or fix the encounter, then compare all routes for parity. Rank 5 is a cap,
   with Area earned by continued pre-shift play or included at the shift.
4. The story shift occurs per Uma when her own duo first successfully launches its ten-person expedition. The
   launch confirmation previews both Umas' Laser-only fittings and which subclass actions are retired. Commit their
   phase with the legal launch receipt, using their saved identities. Promote both to at least Rank 6. The phase
   survives returning, parking, resuming, reinstalling and opening an earlier optional chapter. Other duos remain
   on their own paths. A rejected or canceled launch spends no rank, changes no fitting and retires no subclass.
   Retain the former subclass for migration/history, with no action or fitting derived from it after the shift.
5. Advance to Rank 10 on first clears of eligible post-shift ground encounters using that same settlement rule.
   Show Rank, unlocked modes and the next unlock in Roster and gear preparation. Verify progression to Rank 10 on a
   normal expedition and finale path, using a documented floor only where the catalog has insufficient eligible
   fights. Expedition and finale readiness continue to follow Story, crew, vessel and supply requirements.

### Two-handed Laser and early subclass fittings

Keep the Laser at mid range. Use its modes for damage types and tactical effects, with a single two-grip weapon
silhouette and no late off-hand weapon. The first-pass values reuse the existing specialty implementation.

| Available | Shot | Range | Base tuning and decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Focused | 1–3 | Three Pierce pulses at 6 power per pulse. R4 raises this to 7 and R8 raises it to 8. Base accuracy is 75% at R1, 78% at R3 and 80% at R9. Use for sustained single-target damage. |
| R2 | Thermal | 1–3 | Two Fire pulses at 6 power early and 7 after the shift. Base accuracy 80%. Use for Fire matchups and ignition of eligible tiles and props. |
| R3 | Impact | 1–3 | Two Impact pulses at 6 power early and 7 after the shift. Base accuracy 84%. On hit, drain one Charge from the actual active recipient with Charge. |
| R5 | Area | 2–3 | One Pierce strike at 12 power early and 14 after the shift. Half damage to eligible orthogonal enemy neighbors. Use the legal target as the center. |

Focused uses the ordinary attack cost. Each alternative adds 1 Charge. Use the existing support for Aim on Thermal
and Impact. Keep counters and Crossfire on the frozen fitted profile and show the actual cover recipient, secondary
targets and Charge spend before commit. Verify ignition, Cover, broken weapon arms, no-shot cases and target safety
in an actual rescue encounter. Area Pierce damage and Impact's Charge removal follow the same legal action,
forecast and committed resolution. Proc placement uses actual recipients after Cover and resolution.

| Early subclass | Starting behavior to carry into real missions | Full class boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melee | Pre-sortie switch to the reduced two-handed blade, range 1, 12 Pierce power, 83% base accuracy. The Laser modes return with the Laser fitted. | Full wing movement and melee damage belong to the two winged allies. |

| Support | Jam and terminal access through the Deck with the available action and resource costs. | Squad-wide reaction control comes from the Sniper. Distinct authored ship duties stay visible as bridge jobs. |

Measure half efficiency against the equivalent full role in matched positions. Aim for roughly half its specialized
output or opportunities across an encounter, using a lower value, range, ammo, uses or action access as appropriate.
For binary effects such as terminal access, compare repeatable capability and costs, leaving required Story actions
reachable for a single lead. A ground mission with a required terminal or rescue supplies the required legal action
even after the subclass is absent. Define it as a mission interaction or issued objective tool and test it against
every legal build. A failed combination explains what needs changing and offers an issued compatible fit.
