# CY61 Development · Character and boss signature attacks with illustrated playback

## Outcome and dependencies

Add selectable character signatures and readable boss signature attacks to real campaign tactics.
Read the [delivery contract](../../design/campaign-art-delivery.md), [CY57 Composition](CY57.md),
[CY62 Attack Art](CY62.md) and [CY63 Story](CY63.md). Use CY48's image/portrait behavior and the
existing battle cut-in controller. Implementation follows the first equipped campaign boarding
checkpoint; rules/brief design may begin while the sprite authoring proceeds.

## Current behavior and first rules brief

Normal weapon attacks already emit cut-in cues; chance skills, Captain Orders and duo techniques
also exist. There is no generic per-character selectable ultimate. Define the first signature as
an explicitly equipped action assigned through the frozen battle input, with a stable action key
and separate character/boss presentation key. Reusable weapon/role families may share mechanics
while keeping actor-specific art and lines. Distinct individual mechanics require their own rows.

Before final art, publish the executable fixture and rules table: eligibility/unlock, Charge cost,
per-battle limit or cooldown, turn cost, weapon/part dependencies, legal targets/range/area, accuracy,
damage/support effects, disabled states and reaction behavior. Boss rows additionally define intent,
warning time, cancellation, recovery and AI use. Record chosen values in tuning and the forecast.

## Implementation

1. Introduce the smallest versioned battle action/input/cue extension supporting the agreed table.
   Preserve every older frozen rules version and replay. No runtime reads from story/art/save
   mutable state inside the reducer. Validate malformed action definitions and illegal uses.
2. Implement selection, eligibility and forecast from the same resolver used for damage/effects.
   Display costs, remaining uses, targeting, part requirements and expected outcomes before commit.
   Respect broken arms, two-handed degradation, ammunition and legal mounts. Define interaction
   with counters, Crossfire, Cover, chance chains, duos, Orders and Rig Break explicitly.
3. Add deterministic AI scoring/use for both sides where supported. Boss telegraphs name the affected
   area/target or understandable threat; the player gets meaningful counterplay. Warned moves cannot
   secretly retarget on reload. Handle cancellation after part break, stun, capture or defeated target.
4. Emit presentation cues keyed to the actual action and actor. Upgrade the existing cut-in to show
   the approved armor splash, matching optional profile, action name and short line. Keep the result
   calculation independent; fast/skip and missing art retain the same outcome. Do not replay every
   ordinary attack as a signature or reward a cosmetic splash with additional damage.
5. Freeze signature definitions and remaining-use state in saved battles/replay input/actions. Test
   resumption before commitment, after warning and during playback. Old pending battles complete on
   their original rules. Record a compatible campaign/content migration for new encounter definitions.
6. Supply draft art and dialogue for one real recruit and her first actual boss, with coordinated
   win/loss/retry paths. Expand eligible crew/boss coverage by tier through the shared registry.
   Later signatures preserve service prerequisites, custody, objective interactions and both entries.

## Verification and completion

Use deterministic tests and golden replays for cost/use limits, invalid targets, part breaks,
forecast accuracy, telegraph/cancel, AI choice, reactions, both sides and old rules. Run the affected
actual campaign encounters through the simulator, compare win rate/turn count/resource preparation
and tune out dominant or unavoidable actions. Play the real Hangar/deploy/battle sequence, both
boarding entries, loss/free retry and resumed boss warning on the same campaign. Check controls,
readability, fast mode and performance at landscape phone size. Run lint, tests and build.

Done when the supported signature families and eligible actor bindings are complete, rules and
forecast agree, boss actions have counterplay, replays remain stable and CY62/CY63 presentation is
wired or explicitly draft. Record approved integration in CY36 and physical-device findings in CY37.
Delete with a Done row.
