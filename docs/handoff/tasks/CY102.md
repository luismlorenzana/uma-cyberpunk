## Initial measurements

In the CY97 exact-catalog run, all 186 ship encounters took four submitted orders along a winning route found by search. The 367 ground encounters took 1 to 33 recorded actions with a median of 2 and a 90th percentile of 22. The 100 ground expedition encounters ranged from 1 to 23 actions with a median of 6. These readings describe the scripted winning paths used in verification. The next pass measures first attempts and pressure margins alongside those routes.
