## Initial measurements

In the CY97 exact-catalog run, all 186 ship encounters took four submitted orders along a winning route found by search. The 367 ground encounters took 1 to 33 recorded actions with a median of 2 and a 90th percentile of 22. The 100 ground expedition encounters ranged from 1 to 23 actions with a median of 6. These readings describe the scripted winning paths used in verification. The 186 ship encounters use five mechanical input profiles at the content layer. Two profiles account for 125 and 57 encounters. Every encounter has a four-threat schedule. A scripted focus-fire policy won 129 of 186 encounters with an unstaffed bridge. A policy that disables emitters first and uses guarded fire afterward won all 186 with rank-2 officers, with a minimum winning escort margin of one. The next pass uses those results to choose early, middle and late encounter families for tuning, then compares human attempts and failure margins within each family.

## Tuning pass

- Early convoy first waves lose one fighter. Liberation assaults gain one power on the final wave. Expedition approaches reduce the first wave’s power and add a fighter to the final wave. The finale adds a fighter to its final wave. Threat forecasts match the frozen command input.
- Ground units tied to saved crew gain one Core HP at each of levels 5, 10 and 15. Checks covered all 50 single-recruit capture openings and both orders of the five-system route through all 25 expeditions and the finale.
- The focused-fire unstaffed policy still won 129 of 186 ship encounters. The staffed objective-first policy won all 186 and the minimum winning transport margin rose from one to two. The scripted winning routes verify reachability and retries. CY91 carries landscape iPhone timing, readability and first-attempt play.
