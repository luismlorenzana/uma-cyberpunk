# CY75 Development · Five fronts, core chapters and call-up

## Outcome

Grow the reviewed pilot into the restructured Tier 1–5 campaign. Covers G4 and G9–G12.
Start with the Tier 1 capture ledger and Core I after CY72–CY74's pilot gates work. Ship each
later tier against a signed-off Story cohort and mapped scenes, encounters and art.

## Implementation

1. Introduce explicit tier membership and a unique capture ledger tied to committed journey
   outcomes. Five distinct captures unlock a tier's signal once, and ten unlock its Core
   chapter. Freeze the qualifying IDs and validate source references. Preserve independent
   recruit order within an open tier and make the next tier's sign-ons depend on the settled
   preceding core. Keep the pilot's provisional one-capture signal out of the retail tier policy.
2. Extend the current single finale contract to per-tier core sequences with real ship and
   ground inputs. Keep existing investigations, readiness/muster, encounter retries and resume.
   Validate every settlement/reward and next-tier transition. Define the Core III
   re-capture and return as authored state transitions on the same crew identity and ensure
   equipment, quarters, bonds and dispatch records reconcile after it.
3. Integrate front locations, civilian supply and home liberation at the authored order.
   Preserve the five-system travel model. Track two new homes per system per tier and the
   capitals/final operation in the last tier. Use approved setting equivalents with named
   Story routes while new locations land. Reuse ship upkeep and project consumers, testing
   benefits, provisioning and launch gates on the revised route.
4. Add scoped call-up for a resettled pair on a named operation/core. Freeze crew reference,
   availability, equipment and support at deployment. Preserve home jobs and support outside
   the call-up and restore them on settlement, loss or cancellation. Distinguish call-up from
   changing residence or assigning a permanent ship station.
5. Finish Core V and its post-operation landing/epilogue. Review every tier as a vertical slice
   covering Story, character art, battle and ship/planet management. Record binder changes and
   the owner's canonical campaign decision through CY36/CY37.

## Acceptance

- Four/five and nine/ten distinct captures, repeat/retry, cross-tier capture, alternate order
  and genuine next-tier gates all behave correctly. Test save and reload mid-core.
- Verify resettled, active, away and temporarily called-up units across deploy, cancel, resume
  and settlement. Confirm no lost home support or duplicate duty assignment. Exercise the Core III
  re-capture and restoration when authored.
- Produce a per-tier release receipt covering the ten journeys, five-signal/ten-core gates,
  routes, real campaign encounters, named art fallbacks, previous-save recovery and iPhone checks.
