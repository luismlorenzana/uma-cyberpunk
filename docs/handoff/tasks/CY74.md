# CY74 Development · Collar encounters and sub-boss Talk

## Outcome

Play the pilot's real ship approaches, J4 Collar rescue and J8 home operation in the campaign.
Covers G5 and G6. Depends on CY72's release and mission routing and uses CY73's Ally for J8.
Ship cinematics consume committed ship results through the existing command reader.

## Implementation

1. Create a per-encounter Story binding table for the pilot's five missions. Bind the three
   approaches to existing compatible command scenarios after Story resolves their objectives.
   Reuse the two existing ground layouts through new frozen inputs. J4 needs a named heroine
   leader, a Collar, a legal way to break it and a `free-leader` victory condition. The
   reference scenario is currently `defeatLeader`. Keep the existing deployed scenario and its
   replays available. Validate unit roles, equipment, legal paths, defeat/retry and reachable
   objectives in the campaign packer.
2. Use the existing Hold and Jack-Out paths. Confirm with Story whether Core destruction should
   also free the controlled leader in the new mode. If it should, implement the explicit policy
   in the new encounter input and rules version. Capture follows the settled win and the actual
   freed leader. Cover idle, skip, missed attack, Core failure and retry with one final capture.
3. Add a versioned `Talk` action for explicitly authored unit pairs. Freeze the actor, target,
   eligibility, player-visible hint and result cue in the encounter. Validate adjacency, active
   status and turn costs through the shared resolver. Support the authored Loyal and Doubter
   contacts and the Rival combat-first requirement. Apply the resolved removal or status change
   to the field and settle the garrison outcome once on the allowed result. Keep sub-bosses
   garrison NPCs unless Story issues a distinct playable recruitment contract.
4. Determine whether the authored pair's combined strike fits the existing Duo action. Wire it
   to the real result if the actor, equipment and gate contracts agree. Any new signature
   mechanic follows CY61/CY62/CY63 with its own declared cost and readback.
