Build on the 25 existing named expedition routes, ten-member formations, isolated cargo and shared hull template.

- Expose the active ship, current stop, route, remaining cargo, future battle costs, next objective and return in Home, Navigation, and Operations. Add authored detour, resupply, withdrawal and return paths as appropriate. Keep the fixed Story crew and one active expedition unless the campaign design explicitly changes that rule.
- Provide scoped station, hull, equipment and preparation controls where they matter to the expedition. Respect role assignments, location, supply transfer, roster availability, interruption and save resume. Keep expedition and Homestretch cargo and costs consistent.
- Story supplies route and withdrawal consequences. General Art supplies ship spaces and route destinations. Character Art and Sprite Art use the exact active actor, published equipment and tactical fittings.

**Exit.** Play a standard and a detour/withdrawal route with named members, observe accurate stock and route updates, resume on the small ship, and return to the correct Homestretch context.
