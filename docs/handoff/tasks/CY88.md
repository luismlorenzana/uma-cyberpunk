# CY88 · Ten-member ground tactics, UI and replay

**M3 · Engineering.** Depends on [CY83](CY83.md), named first expedition inputs from [CY92](CY92.md) and the formation from [CY86](CY86.md). Works with [CY87](CY87.md) and [CY95](CY95.md).

## In progress

The reducer and map parser support ten active friendlies on expedition rules 7 and map schema 2. Existing rules and ordinary squad caps are preserved. The versioned save parser checks expedition counts and rejects reused members or slots. Eighteen-unit regression coverage gives all ten crew a turn and resumes deterministically. The larger squad also fits in the API save envelope with 250 crew records and 1,500 recorded actions. The battle view can inspect all ten crew and scroll through support actions. The first expedition now deploys the ten declared actors with their role fittings through the campaign Hangar. It uses four real ground objectives, including a timed hold and a reach objective that requires the named enemy to remain active. Replay of the frozen roster and battle actions passes. The campaign board frames the full deployment in landscape, staggers the ten starting positions and shows the actual goal. Browser verification deployed all ten through the Hangar and restored them and their inspector after reload. The reviewed preview includes two approved lead sprites and stand-ins for outstanding roles. Full sprite coverage, encounter pacing, touch readability and installed iPhone acceptance remain open.
