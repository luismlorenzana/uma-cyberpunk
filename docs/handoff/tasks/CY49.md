# CY49 Art authoring · Meeting, confession, cabin chat and overnight

## Outcome and dependencies

Author the four relationship splash families for the fifty playable Captain arcs, beginning with the
first actual recruit's complete sequence. Use the [shared contract](../../design/campaign-art-delivery.md),
[CY48 Development](CY48.md) and [CY50 Story](CY50.md). References and drafts may start together;
the first executable CY48 layout and CY50 staging determine final exports. Use the existing approved
Cyberpunk style, adult character baselines and wardrobe. Recheck current approvals before drawing.

## Four compositions

| Family and logical slot | Composition | Clothing and reuse |
|---|---|---|
| `relationship-<outfit>-meeting` | Captain-facing arrival at the shared Homestretch welcome/briefing desk; viewport and restrained service props | Approved pilot suit; reuse an approved standing master when it fits |
| `relationship-<outfit>-confession` | Private cabin at evening, closer reciprocal composition with room for the Captain's choice | Approved Quarters look; share cabin geometry and a compatible seated pose |
| `relationship-<outfit>-night-chat` | Seated at the small table or bed edge, looking toward the Captain; hands and nearby furniture make the sitting read | Approved Quarters look; one reusable conversation composition per character |
| `relationship-<outfit>-overnight` | Character lying in bed in the same cabin; keep face, pillow and body position readable behind dialogue | Established Quarters look; one repeatable overnight composition per character |

The Captain is the viewpoint and remains undrawn. Shared room plates are authored once per required
room variant, with character personalization or props as separate layers where practical. A complete
approved flattened CG is acceptable when composition requires it. Keep the room, lighting and bed
continuity between seated chat, confession, overnight and existing morning. Scene bindings are not
a demand for a separately painted background for every character or every night's dialogue.

## Authoring work

1. Read the exact CY50 scene brief and the private scene inventory. Return any physical reference that
   contradicts the common room before final painting. Select the current approved pilot and Quarters
   bases by outfit ID and hash; retain character likeness, palette, accessories and wardrobe cut.
2. Establish shared meeting and cabin plates and the first recruit's four compositions. Present full
   landscape framing plus the in-game dialogue/choice overlays from CY48. Use the existing native plate
   geometry when suitable; record the actual output dimensions rather than assuming a crop.
3. Bind ordinary dialogue to the approved portrait and reusable face crops. Neutral uses its base;
   existing tags such as happy, worried, flustered, tender and loving follow CY50's cues. Reuse the
   character expression packet. A seated/bed splash is not a new expression library. Review crop
   placement for each portrait base where it will be used.
4. For actual shared Uma/former-commander visits, author paired seated and bed variants from the
   approved residence/outfit brief. Preserve both participants' identity and visible personal space.
   Record these as extensions of the two existing families. Keep rear-view studies retired.
5. Expand by recruitment tier after the first complete campaign sequence is coherent. Track every
   required composition as draft, approved or integrated. Preserve previous approved versions.

## Return to development

Use versioned private studies in the authoring checkout for character poses, and this game's ignored
content for shared campaign plates if appropriate. Return each logical ID, source path/version,
native dimensions, transparent/flattened format, SHA-256, approval evidence and export. Supply layer
anchors/order, approved portrait base, face placements, subject bounds, text-safe composition and a
landscape preview. Use the CY48 fixture for machine-readable placements. Clearly identify a draft.

Campaign publication uses the private pack; reusable character assets also follow their established
album/publication path. Publishing an album alone does not wire a scene. Development records the
pack ID and screenshots against the same art record.

## Done when

All required solo compositions and inventoried paired variants have hash-bound approvals, usable
exports and checked bindings. The first recruit's whole sequence and representative later-tier and
paired scenes read at phone size with text, expressions and both choice outcomes. Remaining device
observations are recorded in CY37. Delete this packet with its Done row after authoring acceptance.
