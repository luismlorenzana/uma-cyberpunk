# CY38 Tier 1 opening and roster integration

## Why

Give the ten authored officers the same playable path from First Watch to sign-on and then to their own operations.

## Context

Open recruitment and the Crew stories cards shipped in PR #17. Use the [recruitment contract](../../technical/campaign-content.md#recruitment-and-personal-stories) and the private Tier 1 recruitment and civilian-routes briefs. The local review packs have ten sign-ons. This packet sets the private release that the remaining packets build on.

## Game implementation

- Bring the scripted First Watch, five civilian berths and ten sign-ons through the campaign checker and packer. Keep every request visible after First Watch and every berth reachable with starting fuel and the civilian routes. Show the next stop on an officer card. Keep the one-fuel direct leg behind the drive refit.
- Put each first objective on its crew card. Make sign-on atomic and idempotent if interrupted or replayed. Enable the campaign Training exercise after recruitment as appropriate for the first officer or her story. Keep an eligible trainee and issued gear available in either opening order.
- Record how the new private cast IDs and the placeholder preview coexist. Keep the old pack available for its saves. Use a fresh scoped profile for the authored review and preserve saves for releases that share established IDs.

## Story and art

Use the authored first watch, requests, props, sign-ons and civilian landing lines. Use the established Pilot or Street looks and expression cues, with simple bridge and berth placeholders in the corresponding slots. Follow [CY22](CY22.md) for the exercise review and [CY23](CY23.md) for landing and supply.

Verify that the optional exercise and first evening work with the first recruit from either system. Resume a sign-on or its completed roster state across a private pack revision. Record the pack, fixture and results for CY39.
