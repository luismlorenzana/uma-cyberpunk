# CY31 Shared relationships and home readiness

## Why

Carry the Captain's independent relationships into shared evenings and make resettlement an earned decision.

## Context

Build on [Captain relationships](../../technical/campaign-content.md#captain-relationships), the [recovery contract](../../technical/campaign-content.md#recovery-and-quarters) and [homeworld return](../../technical/campaign-content.md#homeworld-return-and-defense). Begin with Selden and one later recruit. Then author the same progression for the remaining leads.

## Game implementation

- Demonstrate two concurrent consensual romances with the Captain. Add post-release shared visits for eligible crew, including a friendship version. Give the characters their own choices about joining and sharing quarters. Preserve separate-cabin visits.
- Track the maximum Captain-to-pair milestone through shared time and recovery. Give friendship an equivalent way to reach that milestone. Let the player defer relocation or choose to stay aboard so either bond path can finish its faction case.
- Gate resettlement on the milestone, liberation and an open base. Keep existing completed residence and case states valid. Once a pair settles, update quarters, crew availability, local duties and home visits together. Keep all evidence and finale access available on both residence paths.

## Story and art

Write invitations, first shared evening, morning and follow-up for the participating characters. Include the line in which each agrees to the new arrangement. Show the separate and shared room layouts and how they change after resettlement. Review the readiness scene and local job descriptions against CY32's base projects.

## Done when

Play a Captain's concurrent relationships alongside a recruit pair's own choice. Reach the same home readiness through friendship and romance. Verify a postponed move, a move ashore and a stay-aboard case. Resume a shared evening and confirm the night settles once.
