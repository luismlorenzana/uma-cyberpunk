# CY25 Occupied space boarding and liberation

## Why

The first faction arc shows how a recruit's home goal carries the player from space command to HD-2D tactics.

## Context

Build on [CY22](CY22.md) through [CY24](CY24.md). Use the first pair selected for the opening, the shared quest brief, and [the enemy requirements](../../design/campaign-rework.md#the-enemy-behind-the-occupations).

## Decisions made

The first boss arc spans several quests. A ship encounter can change the boarding fight. Liberation and capture are distinct campaign outcomes. Clues about the hidden enemy can come from the missions the player actually performs.

## Do

- Add planetary offense and ship-to-ship objectives to the command system. Pass an agreed result, such as a disabled shield or a secured entry route, into boarding setup. Carry reward, retry and loss effects through the campaign.
- Integrate a boarding map and a planet-station encounter with authored objectives and the weapons required for those battles. Keep the forecast, AI and replay working through the same mission entry as Training.
- With story, script the first faction's local stakes, escalation, capture and liberation. Provide a playable clue and agree on its location, later meaning and fallback access. With art, deliver the boss, boarding and station assets and a readable clue where it appears in play.

## Done when

The player can enter occupied space, play the ship and tactics encounters, affect at least one boarding condition from the ship battle and reach the authored capture and liberation results. The homeworld state and clue survive retry and resume.

## Deliver

Record the space-to-boarding outcome and clue hooks. Follow the repo PR workflow, delete this packet when complete and add its Done row.
