# CY34 Cinematic bridge in the campaign

## Why

Give the crew a visible presence as they take orders and show the result of each action during ship combat.

## Context

Follow [CY33](CY33.md)'s cue contract and the [ship command draft](CY24.md). Use three real encounters for the first authored pass, covering convoy defense, occupied approach and a ship fight that leads to boarding.

## Game implementation

- Present the speaking officer, fighter or watch officer beside the appropriate order and result. Tie the spoken beat and presentation state to the resolved action. Surface attacked shield section, committed fighters and the state of the protected ship or target.
- Offer a faster playback setting and a compact log. Keep controls readable for either pacing mode. Resuming the display or skipping an animation preserves the saved damage and outcome.
- Give the three encounter types distinct objective, loss and victory cues. Extend the cue mapping to the remaining faction ships and the gate approach.

## Story and art

Author the call-and-response and the cue list together. Each entry names the speaker slot, expression or splash, silhouette or effect, and state before and after. Reuse the established character style and existing placeholder presentation for the first pass. Fit the approved bridge and portrait art around the landscape HUD.

## Done when

Play the three encounter types from their campaign briefings. At phone size the player can tell who spoke, what changed, and what to do next. Fast playback and a mid-encounter reload lead to identical game results.
