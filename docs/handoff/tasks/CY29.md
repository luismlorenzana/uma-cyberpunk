# CY29 The Captain's dating loop

## Why

Let the Captain's relationship with a recruit grow through time together and choices made during the campaign.

## Context

Use the existing [Captain bonds](../../game/captain-and-bonds.md), [cabin visits](../../game/cabins.md) and [campaign evening flow](../../technical/campaign-content.md#quarters-and-morning). Start with the opening Selden recruit. Provide a second eligible recruit route in CY31.

## Game implementation

- Use the existing rapport, bond ranks and Trust or Heart machinery where they fit the campaign save. Show each eligible recruit's relationship and next milestone in the campaign crew and evening views. Record the invited companion and pending visit before playback.
- After Training and sign-on, play a teammate visit, a later trust scene and an explicit mutual romance or friendship choice. Let approved campaign actions and varied visits advance rapport. Choose draft pacing with the writers and give replayed scenes idempotent rewards.
- Keep one evening choice and one nightly upkeep settlement per day. Treat the Captain's bond and each recruit's bond with her counterpart as independent. Map the existing Selden solo scene and any saved pending visit to the new flow. Carry v14 campaign choices, roster and evidence forward.

## Story and art

Draft the opening solo scenes, an evening interruption that responds to campaign progress, and the friendship and romantic branches. Settle the speaker, expression, quarters backdrop and morning illustration IDs as part of the scene brief. Use the opening recruit's established look and the existing portrait and backdrop slots for the initial private pack.

## Done when

A fresh captain can complete Training and progress from teammates to either friendship or romance in ordinary play. A saved captain can resume a visit or morning, earn an outcome once and continue into the same campaign missions on either path. Check landscape pacing and both relationship outcomes.
