# Faction arcs and final operation art

Use the established character, enemy and environment guides. The private campaign draft wires existing placeholders through the release format. Review the corresponding private scenes and encounter brief as a set before producing the final images.

| Assets | Scenes and kit | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| `orla-*` | Co-op, patrol and commander | Seed houses, separate water valves, a cold culture crate and a reopened weigh hall. The commander keeps the same silhouette through capture, custody and release. |
| `morrow-*` | Anchorage, patrol and commander | A ferry berth, civic schedules and a route-key reader. The drydock cover and worker exit should read from the tactics camera. |
| `kestrel-*` | Foundry, patrol and commander | Conductor molds, the workers’ lift and clear height changes on the combat map. Show how the relay work moves to civilian control. |
| `vesper-*` | Tender, patrol and commander | Shelter airlocks, the fuel meter and the public reserve. Make the difference between an open relief lane and a blocked one readable. |
| `gate-dossier` and `gate-envoy-look` | Comparison scene | The five inputs and the third-pulse signal align on the evidence board. Keep the envoy recognizable in the briefing, breach and aftermath. |
| `gate-ring-bridge` | Ship assault | Show the gate ring, a protected survey shuttle and the emitter whose loss opens the service entry. Hold a quiet area behind the command display. |
| `gate-control-room` and `gate-custodian-look` | Breach briefing and ground mission | Distinguish the feedback and output controls and identify the custodian defending them. The tactics scenes show each shutter changing from engaged to isolated. |
| `gate-after-light` | Ending | The transit ring still lights. The control signal drops. Show ordinary civilian traffic returning through one lane. |

Each new port uses suffixes `reopened-base`, `brig-scene`, `patrol-bridge`, `commander-look` and `first-morning-art`. The combat maps have placeholder layouts in `scenarios/campaign/maps`. Check dialogue clearance, expression readability and the two console states at landscape phone size. Replace the placeholder sources under the same asset IDs and run the campaign release checker.
