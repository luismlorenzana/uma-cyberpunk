# Selden quarantine station art

The art-authoring session owns the approved assets and follows the project's character, enemy and environment style guides. The private `baseline-7` release uses temporary images and the existing stand-in tactics kit. Use the [campaign content guide](../../technical/campaign-content.md) to replace the private files under the same asset IDs.

| Asset ID or encounter | Where it appears | What players need to read |
|---|---|---|
| `selden-quarantine-approach` | Bridge call and ship command | The station and forward shield emitter at approach distance. Keep the emitter easy to distinguish from the patrol ship. The command display labels its intact and disabled states. |
| `selden-quarantine-gallery` | Boarding call, custody, manifest briefing and liberation | Two distinct access routes, the closed civilian exit and the console with three indicator pulses. Keep the lower part of the shot clear for landscape dialogue. |
| `selden-station-commander` | Commander's dialogue and temporary battle standee | Clear face and silhouette at phone size. Use the approved commander and combat look. Preserve recognition between the call, boarding and custody scenes. |
| `quarantine-boarding` tactics map | Cargo and maintenance entries | Cargo spawn `[1,6]` looks down the sentry lane. Maintenance spawn `[5,3]` enters past the lane. Readable cover and bulkheads matter to move and forecast. |
| `quarantine-station` tactics map | Station control | The manifest console at `[7,1]` should stand out from cover props. The exit at `[6,0]` changes from closed to open on the successful terminal order. |

Use the private casting and story briefs for identities and scene wording. Read the three-pulse mark on the console alongside the earlier pallet and raider cues so it can recur unchanged in the later reveal. Preview the approved replacement on the saved campaign at the approach, boarding, custody and station clear. Keep source images and screenshots with franchise art in private content.
