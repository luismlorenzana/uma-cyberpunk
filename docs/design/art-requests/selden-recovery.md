# Selden recovery and quarters art

The art-authoring session owns the approved looks and follows the project's character, enemy and environment style guides. The private recovery preview reuses the existing temporary art. Use the [campaign content guide](../../technical/campaign-content.md#recovery-and-quarters) to replace the images under their stable asset IDs.

| Asset ID | Scenes | What the player needs to read |
|---|---|---|
| `selden-brig-backdrop` | First visit, testimony, release | Two seats, a steady lamp and the brig hatch. The lamp is a recurring detail. Leave room for dialogue at the bottom in landscape. |
| `selden-held-portrait` | Recovery and release | Recognize the station commander from boarding in her custody look. The testimony scene needs a readable, guarded expression. |
| `selden-released-portrait` | Bond, room choice and later visits | Keep the same identity through the post-release change. Use the approved aboard or Quarters look for these scenes. |
| `selden-shared-quarters-backdrop` | Shared move-in and visit | The room reads as home to two people. Show two keys, a second cup and space each can use. The layout also serves friends who live together. |
| `selden-separate-cabin-backdrop` | Separate move-in and visit | Show one cabin with an available guest chair and the lamp from the brig. |
| `selden-shared-window-morning` | Shared morning illustration | Two cups catch the morning light. Leave the lower area clear for the dialogue overlay. |
| `selden-separate-window-morning` | Separate morning illustration | The lamp's timer and the single cabin furnish the morning beat. Preserve the same landscape framing. |

Use the private story brief and approved cast to review both room outcomes. Preview the testimony, release, move-in and morning at phone size. The two room scenes use separate IDs and can remain independent of the bond choice. Keep source images and franchise art in the private authoring workspace.
