The Cyberpunk Story Sandbox.

A local page where you read, play and judge Uma Cyberpunk's story, beside uma-quest's and uma-farm's Story Sandboxes. It reads the story with the game's own code, so what it shows is what the game plays, and your verdicts go back to the story session.

How it fits

The story session writes files; the game reads them; the sandbox reads them with the game's own code; you judge them there.

The pages

Story

Totals, the five deliverables (SB9) read off the files, every layer, each world with its chapters.

Worlds and a world

The star map; each world's look, mood, factions, leads and its place map, with locked places and the scenes at each.

Chapters and a chapter

Danger order; each scene's place, trigger, cast, reading time (over three minutes flagged) and script; battles' briefs; the debrief.

Player

A scene one step at a time, below.

Cast

Leads and where each comes aboard; the crew's order; heroines by faction and Hold; the Ranker's Umas by batch.

Log

Each File's lines and what fills each in; every Lead, Anomaly and flag, and where.

Calls

The question, the arguments, the branches, where it's held.

Checks, Format, Notes

pnpm story:check's findings exactly; the files and fifteen step kinds; the private world notes and the world doc.

Sprites D24

ChatGPT's combat sprite sheets as they land: each cut by the request's grid and measured, played on a night tile at the phone's size beside her approved chibi, as silhouettes, and cell by cell.

The player

One step at a time: Back, Next, Auto, Restart and the arrow keys. Choices and a Call's branches are one-tap picks, and Back takes a pick back. Story changes (a Lead, a File line, an Anomaly, a flag, a join, coming aboard, a battle) show as cards. The script sits beside it with the current step marked.

Interjection slots are resolved by a squad you set: the line plays for the first lead in the crew's order who's in the squad and has one (TC51), else the fallback, so you hear who would speak.
