Uma Cyberpunk: the concept

A personal sci-fi tactics RPG on the owner's iPhone: a squad of Umas on HD-2D grid maps, in a neon city on an orbital ring, against heroines pulled in from other mobile RPG worlds. The starting doc, 2026-11-12; sixteen calls, TC1–TC16, adopted until the owner changes one. Source: docs/design/concept.md.

The short answer

Grid tactics TC2

Four Umas plus a guest slot, 10×10 to 14×14 tiles, four heights, facing; about ten minutes a battle.

Speed decides TC3

Each unit's clock fills by Speed; the timeline shows the next eight turns. Move, act, face.

Uma stats matter TC4TC5

The five stats become battle stats; five roles start from the running-style aptitude.

An original setting TC6

A megacity on an orbital ring, the Lanes between worlds, and Gates that bring heroines through.

The crossover TC7

Guests from the platform's seven franchise boards: rivals and bosses first, some join. None in git.

Built on what exists TC10TC11

uma-stage for the maps, the shared packages for the rest, its own cloud in uma-rpg.

The loop TC8TC9

The map→A mission→Loadout→ The battle→Rewards→The Hideout→ The story↺

Acts, chapters, missions

Act 1 is the Ring. Later acts open stations, moons and planets along the Lanes. Contracts repeat for levels and gear.

The Hideout

Squad, roles, gear (weapons and implants), levels and the story log, on uma-kit's shell.

Rewards

Experience, credits and parts; parts build gear. No gacha: Umas join through the story, guests through their chapters.

How it's built TC10TC11TC14

The battle view

An HD-2D diorama on @luismlorenzana/uma-stage (P142), with a grid overlay, height, tile picking and a tactical camera. CY4 proves it; the fallback is a Phaser 4 isometric board.

The stack

uma-rpg's: React, Vite, Tailwind, Zustand and Dexie, NestJS on Cloud Run, Firebase Hosting and Auth, a PWA. Six platform packages.

The cloud

In the uma-rpg project, every piece its own: site uma-cyberpunk, cyber-api, Firestore cyber. CY2, on the owner's go with a cost table.

It stands alone

Its own save, rules and economy. No parcels from uma-farm at the start; they can come later (P89).
