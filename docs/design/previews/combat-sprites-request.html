Combat sprites: baseline art requirements

Current request · 2026-11-20 (D75). Author one helmeted armored character for a visual review in the real campaign. This is the current image brief for CY59, shared with CY58 equipment and CY57 integration. The approved baseline will establish the drawing perspective before animation and roster expansion.

Character and references

Use one real recruit with approved Cyberpunk combat armor and helmet references, preferably the first recruit. Record the exact character/outfit ID, look ID and reference versions in the private handoff. Keep her approved chibi proportions, approximately 2.6–3 heads tall excluding the ears.

Preserve her armor silhouette, helmet and ear housings, tail, emblem, palette and anatomical asymmetry. Recognizability comes from those features even with the face covered. Every direction is drawn independently; one-sided details remain on the same anatomical side.

Follow Hardline Anime: confident dark contours, broad angular cel shadows, clear local colors and restrained highlights. Simplify seams and small markings for a figure about 70 CSS pixels tall. Use consistent top/front-left light and retain readable accents on dark armor.

Viewpoint and four directions

Draw the baseline from slightly above, with visible top planes of the helmet, shoulders and boots. Use approximately 35 degrees downward as the first viewpoint study, matching the fixed board camera's elevation. Keep a consistent elevation and proportions across all four directions. The final painted angle is established by the in-campaign review, rather than by the number alone.

The board has an axis-aligned grid and four logical directions. Draw the whole body facing the listed direction; head, torso, hips, feet and weapon agree. The elevated side drawings can show top surfaces while their facing remains left or right. A three-quarter identity illustration can be supplied as an existing reference; the four gameplay drawings are the required baseline.

The renderer displays painted pixels on upright flat surfaces. Its height compensation preserves their on-screen proportions; it does not add a painted viewpoint. Perspective must therefore be consistent in both the character drawing and fitted equipment. Existing eye-level studies can be returned as comparison references with their original review status.

First delivery: four matched body/fitting pairs

Return eight PNGs: four clean body images and four equipped previews, one pair per direction. Use a stable standing rifle-ready pose with both hands in believable contact positions. Within each pair the body pixels, pose, scale and placement must match; derive the fitting from the same body source/layers. Both feet remain planted and the body weight supports the equipment.

Use CY58's latest approved rifle design where available. Otherwise identify the exact draft used as a fitting placeholder. The rifle has an anatomical right primary grip and left support grip; hands, fingers and forearms overlap it naturally in each direction. Its silhouette is fictional energy equipment, consistent with the current equipment brief. Long weapons remain Back-mounted.

If the working master contains separate rifle, hand/forearm occlusion or mount layers, include those native layers or aligned transparent exports. Record missing separations. The fitting preview is sufficient to judge the baseline but reusable runtime attachment acceptance remains CY57/CY58. An existing Back-pack fitting may accompany the baseline if available; it has its own review status.

Handoff

Save the source images in the existing private Combat Sprites collection or return an exact local folder path. Include a short README.md with the character/outfit and look IDs, source references, file-to-facing mapping, intended viewpoint, canvas size and foot anchor. Identify the equipment source and version, anatomical grip positions, any supplied layers, and known deviations or gaps. Provide exact generation/edit prompts and native masters through the existing authoring workflow. Keep draft, owner-approved source and integrated-game status distinct; development can record hashes and extract precise anchors during intake. Images, named cast and approvals stay in ignored content.

Review and next delivery

Development places the four directions in the actual campaign's Training encounter and next real boarding review. Check at 844 × 390 landscape, near 70-pixel figure height, on light and dark ground and behind cover. Review grounding, front/back recognition, identity, weapon alignment, selection and clearance beside another unit. Compare the original and equipped source at the same scale.

Record the selected painted viewpoint and any source corrections. Then CY57/CY58/CY59 establish the reusable attachment fixture and the first required movement/action samples. Animation, additional loadouts, opponents and roster expansion follow that shared result through the same campaign. CY37 retains the physical iPhone check.
