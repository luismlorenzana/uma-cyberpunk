Model quality and clean cel rendering

Use Hardline Anime: confident contours, designed armor planes, broad controlled cel shadows, clear local colors and restrained highlights. Model the helmet, visor, chest/abdomen, hips, joints and boots to match the approved silhouette. Use appropriate smooth normals on curves and deliberate hard edges on plates. Check stance, limb proportions and believable equipment contacts. Keep the rifle distinct from the arms and torso in the front view.

First review the full-proportion model at a generous inspection size, including a body-only view beside the approved reference and an equipped three-quarter view. These are source-quality views; the four board facings still come from the same fixed camera. A larger image of the earlier blockout does not establish model quality. Preserve prior prototypes as history and reuse rig/exporter work where useful, updating bones, contacts and sockets to the new proportions.

Render smooth, antialiased transparent edges and controlled cel-shaded surfaces. Remove the previous low-resolution pixel cleanup, global palette snapping, binary-alpha threshold and automatic native one-pixel contour from this export path. Contour weight is art-directed at the intended display size. Preserve the exact approved palette while simplifying detail for readability.
