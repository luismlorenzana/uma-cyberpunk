Combat sprites: the image request

Production order superseded by D73 (2026-11-20): use CY59 for helmeted armor first, shared family poses and CY57 attachment exports. The helmet-off-first order and fixed sheet/frame totals below are historical references, not current production quotas.

Next delivery (D64, 2026-11-18): CY59 coordinates new playable sheets with CY57's composition contract and CY58's equipment. Before expanding this earlier empty-hand request, reconcile the actual frame/pose and attachment fixture, including two-handed grips and weapon-family actions. Keep approved existing sheets and the owner's identity/walk review sequence. The next acceptance checkpoint is an equipped squad in its real campaign boarding mission.

For ChatGPT, image generation only (2026-11-13, D24). This page asks for one thing: chibi battle sprites of the two pilot Umas in their combat armor, helmet off and then helmet on, drawn as pose sheets. Cutting the sheets into frames, alignment, the atlas, checks, publishing and how the game plays them come later, and aren't ChatGPT's. It's nameless (Umas are outfit ids); each Uma's identity, her paste-ready prompts and her reference board are private, in content/design/character-looks/ (combat-sprites-prompts.md, sprite-refs/). The design behind it is character-sprites.md. Visual page: previews/combat-sprites-request.html.

The figure: her chibi, in armor

  • Her head and body are her approved chibi's: the platform's traveler sprite set of her outfit, revision 5. That means the same face, head size, eyes, hair, ears, accessory sides, tail and proportions (about 2.6 to 3 heads below the ears). The rendering stays the same too: shaped lids and lashes, distinct whites, gradient irises, dark pupils and their highlights, a clear small mouth, coloured outlines, clean antialiased edges, and restrained light from the top front-left.
  • Only her clothes change, to her approved combat armor look, simplified to chibi scale:
    • Keep: the chest plate with her emblem, the big shoulder pads, the segmented abdomen, the forearm gauntlets

    (each forearm as her look draws it), the thigh, knee and shin plates, the armored boots, the fingerless gloves, her one-sided motif, and her three colours (base, accent, trim) where her look puts them.

    • Drop: thin seams, tiny slots, text and any detail under about four pixels at the final size.
    • Make the plates chunky, so her outline is clearly different from her chibi's dress: wider shoulders, heavier

    boots.

  • Her tail is always out. With the helmet off her ears are bare. With the helmet on they sit in the helmet's armored ear housings.
  • Never mirrored. Every asymmetric detail stays on the side of her body it belongs to, in every facing. Her identity sheet in the private notes names each detail's side.

No frame, character or design is copied from any game. These are references for scale, readability and finish only.

The camera and the facings

  • Drawn at eye level, straight on, like her existing chibi. The board's camera looks down from 35°, but the game stands each sprite upright and undoes the tilt, so the sprites must not be drawn from above.
  • Her back must read as her back at a glance: a hit from behind does +50% in this game. Her hair, her tail and the armor's back plate make it plain.
  • Five views. The profiles are real side views, as the owner asked for her existing chibi sets:

Every sheet

  • Transparent background (true alpha 0), and nothing else in the image: no text, labels, grid lines, floor, shadow, glow, effects, weapons or props.
  • 512 × 512 cells, read row-major (left to right, top to bottom); unused cells stay empty.
  • The figure about 416 px from ear tips to soles, feet on y 480 of the cell, centred on x 256, at the same scale in every cell of every sheet. At least 14 px of padding around ears, hair, tail, hands and boots.
  • One figure a cell, each drawn on its own, never a flipped copy.
  • Empty hands: where a pose needs one, her anatomical right hand is a closed, empty grip (the game adds weapons later).

The poses

ActionFramesEach frame
Walk6Contact, down, passing, contact on the other leg, down, passing. Restrained, with armored weight. The arms swing a little and the head stays level. She walks in place, never travelling across the cell
Act30 ready: weight forward, her right hand a closed grip drawn back at the waist. 1 the strike: that arm thrust the way she faces at chest height, the moment a blow or shot lands (no weapon, no flash). 2 recover toward her stance
Hurt20 a flinch away from the way she faces, eyes squeezed, a small open mouth (with the helmet, the head snaps back). 1 recover
Guard20 her left forearm raised across her chest, knees bent, braced. 1 the brace held. A forearm shield shows as her look draws it, and the game adds its light
Kneel (her rig breaks)30 a stagger. 1 down on one knee. 2 kneeling, head bowed, one hand on the ground: worn out, not wounded. This frame is held, and played backwards she gets up
Victory4, front only0 a relaxed stance. 1 she turns to the viewer and smiles. 2 a fist raised. 3 a confident pose, held

In the back and profile sheets, the act and guard point the way she faces (away, left, right). In the front sheet they come toward the viewer, foreshortened.

Before saving a sheet

  • Her head matches her approved chibi: face, head size, every accessory on its side. Her armor matches her approved look's colours, emblem and one-sided details.
  • Scale: every standing figure is the same height, with the feet on one line.
  • Facings: the profiles are true profiles, never a front body with a turned head. Nothing is flipped.
  • Clean: a transparent background, with no grey board, halo or floor copied from the references.
  • Small: at about 70 px tall she's still recognisable, and her armor's outline differs from her chibi's dress.
