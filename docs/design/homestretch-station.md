# Homestretch station, cutters and campaign calendar

**D94 · 2026-11-26.** This design extends the [four-act campaign](campaign-rework.md) and gives the [implementation roadmap](../handoff/homestretch-station-roadmap.md) its game rules. The CY127 overview draws the station from its saved room states and the same cutter kit follows real voyages. Play and balance establish costs, yields and durations.

## What the player does

After First Watch you see the mobile station at its current anchor. In **Overview**, find a deck, capacity, power and current work. Tap a deck to see compatible expansion sockets and occupied rooms. Tap a room to zoom in, see the people and equipment in it, and act in place. Drag and pinch move the cutaway. Visible zoom and back controls, a room list, and direct links from the Roster, Calendar and Operations provide the same access. The station overview, room details and operational menus all read the same campaign state.

At a berth, board a cutter, preview a route and its fuel and time, and leave for an operation or a meeting. Check Calendar for upcoming shifts, construction, appointments and arrivals. Crew can be assigned to an open shift, a cutter station or an away operation. The planner explains conflicts and shows when people will return. End a watch from the agenda or spend the displayed watches on a committed action.

## Station layout

The core starts with **20 permanent berths**, a reserve of **30 temporary berths** for early recruits, and one reusable secure intake room within the command core. The reserve is an emergency accommodation system. Residents keep their roster identity and a private guest space for eligible cabin scenes while awaiting permanent quarters. The player can assign a preferred cabin or use automatic allocation. The original cast can all join in any order after First Watch. Each Uma's Act I is reachable with only her on the roster.

| Core facility | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Bridge | Recruitment, anchors and destinations, Operations, Calendar and station status. |
| Quarters | Permanent and temporary berths, resident directory, consent-based visits and role-appropriate invitations. |
| Reactor | Power balance, essential service priority, optional load shedding, Engineering watch. |
| Cargo | Station inventory, fuel, provisions, deliveries, transfers, material reserves and relief. |
| Cutter berth | Board, crew, fit, provision, launch, berth and service the shared starting vessel. |

Expansion slots fit one compatible module family shown by the deck plan. Placement can choose from equivalent sockets. The player sees cost, output, build time, dependencies and where the work queues before committing. The ten expansion modules are as follows.

| Module | Primary effect |
| --- | --- |
| Habitat A | Adds 100 permanent berths and their configurable cabin blocks. |
| Habitat B | Adds 160 permanent berths across a second residential wing. |
| Reactor bank | Adds generation and supports powered optional work as the station grows. |
| Fabrication | Makes and repairs rig equipment using existing material and recipe systems. |
| Life support | Scales provisions and essential services for the expanded population. |
| Infirmary | Provides recovery beds and scheduled crew return to duty. |
| Secure ward | Houses captured Generals and enables review and transfers. |
| Rehabilitation suite | Hosts the General, lead Uma and Captain for the authored rehabilitation sequence. |
| Shipyard | Commissions fleet cutters, allocates dock time, and fits or repairs persistent hulls. |
| Jump ring | Expands the station's relocation options. Authored anchors and story events govern when it moves. |

Core and two habitats yield **280 permanent berths**, allowing for up to 250 named potential residents plus headroom. A returned Lieutenant may be based on a planet, assigned to a cutter, or stationed aboard as her story allows. Room, custody, relationship, deployment and ordinary work permissions are separate. Start with secure intake and a guaranteed secure transfer or overflow placement for each valid capture. Core intake supports the first rehabilitation path while the expanded suite adds appointment capacity. A cleared Boss Ally moves to residence, and her story determines relationship availability. D87's Act II limits on Boss Ally assignments apply.

Essential systems use protected capacity. Shortfalls pause lower-priority work and disclose the projected consequence. Unpowering, replacing or reallocating a room with people or committed work requires an explicit safe transfer or a clear eligibility message. Crew output and storage use bounded rolls or deterministic rates, rescue supplies stay reachable, and station costs cannot create a mandatory story dead end.

## Station, cutters and battle

Homestretch is jump-capable and mainly operates from stable anchors. Regular recruitment, local travel, approach missions and playable ship battles use a cutter. The station can support an operation, supply eligible sorties or serve as a protected objective. Its anchor, projects and population are recorded separately from each cutter's position. The Captain and one lead Uma can take a cutter into a mission, and additional crew fill battle stations as they join.

| Act | Ship layer |
| --- | --- |
| I | The shared cutter is available at First Watch. Travel to all five systems to recruit. Invite the Uma aboard during her authored sign-on. The sign-on assigns a home berth and opens her Act I even if the cutter is still at the meeting port. Same-port retry costs zero, and a new stop shows its first-arrival scene before the meeting. |
| II | The active Uma roster continues to fly the shared cutter. Custody, rehabilitation, cleared residence, planetary bases and capitals feed station supply and launch readiness. The yard opens through authored progression and provides an attainable baseline hull path for a ready duo. |
| III | Each of the 25 fixed duos can commission one identifiable cutter. Its cargo, fittings, damage and route persist across sorties. One playable voyage can be underway at a time. Returning or explicitly parking at a safe berth lets another duo launch without losing the earlier route. Launch still requires the five capitals, that duo's six returns and its cleared Umas and Boss Allies. All ten deploy on the ground. Committed escorts, blockades and orbital approaches open the matching landing where authored. Each route frees its named Herald or Echo. |
| IV | Fly assigned cutters in the authored final operation. Use the station as logistical support or a defended objective where the authored encounter calls for it. The Queens, witness gate, supported outcomes, soft endings and post-ending life remain the act's focus. |

Separate station stores from vessel tanks and cargo. A transfer creates one receipt for both sides. Hull fittings affect the existing command snapshots only for the assigned craft. The boarding and ground battle engines continue to consume the same eligible cast and gear. A station emergency and an unrelated personal arc can coexist.

## Calendar, work, travel and relationships

A monotonic campaign clock has **three watches per day** with working labels **Duty, Free time, Night**. Four 30-day months make a 120-day year. **Keel, Signal, Longlight, Farwake** are working names until Story confirms lore. A saved watch resolves into a dated calendar cell and a numbered phase. Every queue and cooldown uses absolute watches or boundary IDs. Local dates and orbital descriptions can add flavor to the common Captain's calendar.

- **Today** shows current crew locations, conflicts and useful actions. **Next 7 days** shows project completions, shift coverage, demand and arrivals. The **month** shows authored observances, scheduled scenes and relationship milestones with links into the game. The same day view provides past receipts.
- **Work** offers recurring rotations, manual assignments and a configurable automatic fill for remaining staffing. Explicit orders come first, with visible gaps for unavailable crew, insufficient power and missing stock. Each shift settles once. Top-line duty output and specialist support scale without asking the Captain to issue hundreds of orders.
- **Relationships** read actual presence, custody and role, player choices, and the authored scene gates. Appointments can be booked, deferred and rescheduled. Arrival, custody and key relationship scenes stay available when their first window passes. Celebrate date-specific moments with a repeat or makeup path. Calendar entries never advance affection or consent on their own.
- **Travel** shows fuel, phase and arrival time before confirming. Use authored route durations and return windows. Arrivals, departures, construction, work and appointments are processed through one clock. Tactical turns, menu browsing, real time and retries introduce no additional elapsed watches. A committed mission may apply its authored elapsed time once.
- **Act gates** follow story flags and mission outcomes. A date can organize a scene or describe a window, with an authored return path for necessary scenes. The player can advance explicitly or confirm a multi-watch trip with a summary of due appointments, arrivals and work.
