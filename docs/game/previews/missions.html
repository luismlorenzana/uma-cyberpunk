The mission flow

Status: 2026-11-12, CY10 (D14). From a mission's briefing to the scene aboard (TC32, TC42), each step held in the save so the app reopens wherever it was. The rules between the screens and the save are the director (apps/web/src/mission/director.ts), pure and tested headless.

The short answer

  • Briefing → deploy → battle → results → aboard. A scene's battle step starts it; winning plays the scene the story triggers after it, then the ship.
  • The battle is the sandbox's screen, unchanged, on the battle the mission names (a scenario in scenarios/) with the squad's Umas in its slots.
  • Every step and every battle action is saved, so after iOS closes the app it reopens at the briefing, the deploy screen, the battle (replayed to the same state), the results, or the scene aboard (from its start).

The save

mission holds the mission in progress: its id, chapter, step, the squad (outfit ids), its battle as CY8's frozen input and the actions taken (checked as JSON; the battle package replays it), and at the results whether it was won. Battle actions save as they're made and sync a few seconds later rather than one by one. A won mission joins worlds.missions.
