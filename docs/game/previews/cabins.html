Cabins, decor and visits

Built behavior. For the planned changes to rehabilitation, shared residence and expedition context, see the four-act campaign guide and roadmap.

Status: 2026-11-13, CY14 (D25). Every Uma has a cabin with six decor slots. Furniture comes in six styles, and her cabin's Comfort helps her shed Strain. She reveals her tastes over time, and the captain's visit (talk, a gift, decorating together, an invitation out) is the main way rapport rises between battles. The design is life-aboard.md §4 (TC60, TC61). Berths and the Quarters module's levels come in CY15; the Store and the Workshop (more items) in CY15 and CY16.

The short answer

  • The Quarters deck has a door for each Uma aboard. Each door shows:
    • her face;
    • her bond rank;
    • her cabin's Comfort as five lights;
    • a badge when her bond scene waits (!) or she's asked for something (?);
    • whether she has a private cabin or a shared berth, by the Quarters module's level (CY15, ship-build.md).

    A door opens her cabin.

  • Her cabin is a landscape room with six slots (Bed, Desk, Wall, Floor, Light, Keepsake) and a window tinted by the world the ship orbits. She stands in it in her Quarters look, the approved Uma Character Art image, and master data's bust stands in until it loads.
  • Decorating is free and never a visit: tap a slot, pick an item from the stock, or empty it. A theme kit furnishes her favourite style from the stock in one tap. Comfort changes on the spot. Decorating never gates a rank.
  • Three visits a watch, shared by the whole crew. A visit is one choice:
    • Talk about one of three topics;
    • give a gift;
    • decorate together: she picks a piece from the stock;
    • invite her out, from bond rank 4.

    Each ends on a card with what she said, the rapport, anything she revealed, and sometimes a request.

  • Her tastes (a favourite style, a disliked style, a gift kind, two topics) come from the story when it writes them, else are derived from her running style, role and bands. They stay hidden until a visit reveals them.

Comfort (TC61)

Comfort is the sum of her six items' comfort (1 to 3 each), with three adjustments:

  • +2 for each piece in her favourite style;
  • −1 for each in the style she dislikes;
  • +2 for a full set: all five regular slots in one style.

It shows as five lights:

The rapport goes through the bonds' rules:

  • half again at Comfort 4;
  • doubled below the crew's median rank;
  • the story's gates still hold.

Her bond scene, when one is ready, waits at the top of the visit panel and costs no visit.

Requests: about one visit in five ends with her asking for something for a slot (an empty one first). Granting it from the Decorate panel fills the slot and pays +30. It isn't a visit.

Visits reset when the watch moves on: a mission's results closing, or a jump.

Two topicsHer running style (racing, training, the stars, games) and her best band (speed: racing, stamina: nature, power: food, guts: the crew, wit: tech)

An Uma with no known running style derives all four from her id. The same Uma always derives the same tastes.

The story writes them in casting.json as tastes by outfit id: { favourite, disliked, gift, topics: [a, b] }. Her small talk goes in her bond file as talk: { <topic>: [lines] }, up to four lines a topic, 160 characters each.

Items and the stock

  • A new game owns two of each regular slot in Classic and Cozy, and one gift of each kind.
  • A first win of a mission brings one item, chosen by a fixed table over the mission's id, and the results screen shows it. The Store (CY15) and the Workshop (CY16) add more.
  • Placing a piece takes it from the stock; removing it or replacing it puts it back.

Looks in the cabin

The cabin shows her Quarters look (D24, LK10): the approved full-body image from Uma Character Art's Cyberpunk universe. cyber-api serves it to the signed-in owner at /api/content/looks/…:

  • in production, from uma-platform's content bucket, read-only and only art/universes/cyberpunk/ (D25);
  • locally, from a mirror in the ignored content/looks/.

The game matches a look to an Uma by her character's English name from master data. Without one, master data's bust stands in.

Campaign evenings (CY48)

From End day, choose an available officer. A ready relationship milestone plays first; regular visits rotate acquaintance, friend or partner conversations. A completed solo chat earns its authored rapport once. Independent Captain relationships keep their own progress.

An established rank-10 Heart can receive a mutual invitation to stay. Choose to stay, leave, or take more time. The choice is saved before its response. Staying opens the overnight conversation; Leave tonight remains available there. Both outcomes use one evening and one upkeep, followed by the matching morning. The morning adds no second charge. Leaving is followed by a personal call rather than an assumed stay.

Existing shared visits retain their release, posting and residence choices. Their later cabin evenings require everyone to be available at the same actual location, with separate berths and individual consent. An officer ashore is visited at home when docked there. Officers away on operations are unavailable.

Save v17 resumes the reserved conversation and committed outcome, including after a content update. Earlier pending visits and mornings migrate without restarting their costs or relationship decisions. The authoring contract defines the scene bindings.
