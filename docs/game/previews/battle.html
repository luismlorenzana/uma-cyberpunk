The battle

Built behavior. For the planned changes to ten-unit Act III formations and action readability, see the four-act campaign guide and roadmap.

Status: 2026-11-12, CY8 (D12). The rules as @cyber/battle plays them, rules version 2: CY4's core and CY5's roles, Tech, chance skills, items, Stride, the bench call and three more objectives, with CY6's first balance pass (see Balance); how enemies choose is on enemy-ai.md. Terrain, props and terminals are on terrain.md; the Holds and the Steel Muster on factions.md. The board arrives in CY7. Every number comes from packages/battle/src/tuning.ts, and the tables marked live are rendered from it.

The short answer

  • A battle is a frozen input plus a list of actions. The input holds the rules version, the seed, the map, the objective and every unit with her bands and her resolved rig. Replaying the actions on the input gives the same state every time, so the save keeps only those two (an autosave after every action is a few bytes).
  • Each unit acts when her own clock fills, at a rate set by Speed. A turn is move, then one action, then face.
  • The rig takes the damage: four parts (Core, two arms, Legs) with their own HP. A broken arm drops its weapon, broken Legs slow her, a broken Core is a Rig Break: she kneels and extracts, never dies.
  • Charge is one small bar: spend it on your turn or keep it to counter, join an ally's attack (Crossfire) or take a hit for one (Cover) on theirs.
  • The forecast card shows every number before a turn is sent, from the same functions the result uses.
  • Each role has its Back unit (Bulwark, Thruster, Launcher, Deck, Med), and the Deck's Tech hacks heroines, hardware and terminals. Heroines are freed, never killed.

The map

A map is JSON, schema 1. Its heights stay digit rows, exactly uma-stage's room format, so the board builds them with no translation.

FieldRule
width, depth6–14 each
heightsOne digit 0–3 a tile; row 0 is the back (north), x grows east
tiles. floor, # solid (no standing, blocks sight), = grating, c cover; grating and cover play as floor until CY5
spawns.squadExactly five tiles: four Umas and a guest
spawns.enemyOne to eight tiles

No spawn sits on a solid tile or shares one. parseMap refuses anything else and names the path, such as map.heights[3][0]: expected a digit 0–3.

Directions are N, E, S and W; S faces the camera.

The units and their rigs

Each unit brings five bands (Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts and Wit, 1–5), a Charge cap (3, or 4 and 5 with licences later), her link toggle, a leader flag, a spawn, a facing and her resolved loadout: each part's numbers, never a catalogue id. A unit may carry a ref and a label for the app; the rules never read them.

The loadout: eight slots and a Coat.

SlotCarries
CoreLoad; optional Capacity and HP bonus
Left arm, Right armLoad; optional HP bonus
LegsLoad; optional HP bonus and a Move modifier
Back unitLauncher, Bulwark, Thruster, Deck, Med or none; Load and optional Capacity; a Launcher carries a Long weapon and 1–9 ammo
VisorLoad; aim lets her pick a part at −25% hit
Left hand, Right handA weapon or a shield, or nothing
CoatP, I or F: halves that damage type
  • Load against Capacity: every slot's Load added up must fit her Capacity (10 + 2 a Power band, plus the Core's and the Back unit's bonus). Over Capacity, the battle refuses her.
  • A light rig is a fast rig: at 70% of Capacity or less, +1 Move.
  • A two-handed weapon goes in the right hand with the left hand empty. A Long weapon fires only from a Launcher.

What breaks when a part breaks:

PartWhen it breaks
CoreRig Break: she kneels, out of the fight
One armThat hand's weapon or shield is off; a two-handed weapon fires at half accuracy
Both armsNo attack and no guard; she can still move and Wait
LegsMove 1, no level changes, evasion 0
A hit on a broken partGoes to the Core

The timeline and Charge

  • Every clock fills at its Speed band's rate; a turn comes at 1,000. A round is 100 ticks: one turn at Speed band 3. Rounds are what "hold for N rounds" counts.
  • Ties go to the higher Speed band, then to the seeded roll. The timeline shows the next eight turns.
  • Charge starts at 1 and gains 1 at the start of each of her turns, up to her cap.
  • Wait means no action (moving is still allowed): +1 Charge more, and her clock restarts at 250 instead of 0, so her next turn comes sooner. The forecast shows the shift.
  • The link toggle is a free action on her own turn: with it off she never joins Crossfire or gives Cover.

A turn

One action: { kind: "turn", unit, to?, act, face }, where act is an attack (a mount, a target and an optional aimed part), wait or none. The app holds the two-tap move and its undo itself and sends the turn whole.

  1. Move (optional): to any reachable tile.
  2. Act: attack, Wait, or nothing.
  3. Face one of the four ways.

An illegal turn (not her turn, an unreachable tile, a target out of reach, a part already broken) returns an error and changes nothing.

Movement

  • Move points come from her Speed band, +1 for a light rig, plus the Legs' modifier; broken Legs move 1.
  • A step climbs or drops one level at most; broken Legs stay level. Solid tiles can't be entered.
  • Zones of control: entering a tile next to a standing enemy ends the move.
  • A unit passes through her own side, never through the other, and never ends on an occupied tile.

Reach and line of fire

BandRangeHeight reachLine of fire
Melee1Two levels up or downNot needed
ShortThe weapon's, within 1–3Two levels up or downNeeded
LongThe weapon's, within 3–6; +1 from two levels upAnyNeeded

Range is counted in steps (no diagonals). Line of fire runs from her eye (0.8 of a level above her tile) to the target's middle (0.5 above hers); a solid tile, or a tile higher than the line where it crosses, blocks it. Units never block.

Attacks

An attack fires one mount (right hand, left hand, or the Back unit's Launcher) at one standing enemy. The attacker turns to face her target as she fires.

Where it lands: inside the target's front quarter is the front, inside her back quarter the back, anything else, diagonals included, the side.

Each hit rolls on its own (Short weapons fire several):

  1. Hit % = the weapon's accuracy + 3 a Wit band − the target's evasion (2 a Speed band, 0 with broken Legs), − 25 when aimed, clamped to 5–95%.
  2. Crit: the weapon's crit % (5 unless it says) for ×1.4 damage.
  3. The part: aimed, or rolled on the band's weights; a hit on a broken part goes to the Core.
  4. Damage = the weapon's power (+2 a Power band for Melee) × facing (side ×1.25, back ×1.5) × height (+10% a level above the target, −10% a level below) × Coat (×0.5 when it matches) × crit × (100% − the Guard), rounded down, at least 1. The arithmetic is exact integers, so a replay can't drift.
  5. The Guard: a whole shield arm cuts a hit from the front or side (a tower shield 40%, a buckler 20%), never from behind.

A Long weapon uses one ammo a shot, and is never countered and never counters.

Signature attacks

When an encounter equips a signature, select the named action beside the normal attack. The forecast shows its Charge cost and strike. The action is available once per battle. A boss signature marks the target's tile first. The boss can fire after the targeted unit gets a turn. Move off the tile, break the weapon, or stun the boss to interrupt it.

  R -->|No| E

  • Cover: when the target's own shield doesn't guard the attack (she has none, or it comes from behind), the first adjacent ally in timeline order with a whole shield arm, her link on and 1 Charge steps in. She takes the main strike from the front, so her Guard applies. A covered attack draws no counter.
  • Crossfire: up to two of the attacker's allies join with a free basic attack of one round (CY6's balance pass), 1 Charge each, in timeline order, if their link is on and either they stand on the target's far side with a Melee or Short weapon in reach (a pincer), or they have a Short or Long weapon in reach with a line of fire (a link). Enemies do it too.
  • Counters are automatic: the defender answers with a Melee or Short weapon in reach of the attacker, for 1 Charge, when she's still standing, the attack came from her front or side, it wasn't Long, and (rules version 2) she isn't a Steel Muster unit reloading.
  • Rules version 2 adds to the exchange: a defender's First can counter before the attack; Burst adds rounds to the main strike; after it, Double, else Switch, else Stun (one chain at most); an impact hit on the Core can Stun (10%); an F hit sets the target's tile on Fire. Cover takes an aim at a Hold unaimed.
  • Things on the map (v2): barrels, pipes and barriers can be attacked: no evasion, Guard or Coat, and no Cover, Crossfire or counter.

Rig Break and Hold on

  • At Core 0 her rig locks and she kneels: no turns, not a target. She stays rebootable until the end of her next turn: a Medic next to her can Reboot her (rules version 2); when that turn comes first, she extracts.
  • Hold on: Guts at band 4 or more lets her survive one Core-breaking hit a battle at Core 1.

Tech (rules version 2)

The Deck's three hacks, never countered, each one roll of Wit against Guts: 50% + 5% a Wit band − 10% a target's Guts band, clamped 5–95%. The forecast shows the %.

The bench support call (rules version 2)

The input lists the resting Umas' roles. Once a battle, on a squad unit's turn, the squad makes one call as a free action (her turn goes on), with fixed numbers and no rolls:

VanguardA barrier on any open tile
StrikerA strike on one tile: 15 to the Core of whoever stands there

The end of a battle (rules version 2)

The result lists each enemy as freed, broken or standing, with her salvage: her unbroken parts, freed or broken alike. A guest joins only when freed, so breaking her parts against keeping them whole is a real choice.

Ally Auto and the balance

Ally Auto takes a free Order before her turn: Reboot on a kneeling ally, or Sure shot when the attack she means to make is under 40% to hit. Auto never calls Resonance.

The balance pass (pnpm sim balance) plays every scenario with a bare squad and a life-aboard one. Life aboard means:

  • bond ranks 3, 7 and 10;
  • the first two Umas as level-2 partners;
  • the Sensors, the Shield Projector and the Battery powered;
  • two Orders for Auto;
  • a curry.

At 80 seeds a scenario, the bare squad is unchanged (version 2, frozen), the mean gain is +23 points, every scenario gains, and none reaches 100%. The targets are about one battle in five more often and never every battle. At 200 seeds (CY18) the mean gain is +20.6, and cohesion 5 on top of life aboard adds −0.4 points: within noise, inside its target of −2 to +8.

Two findings changed the rules on the way:

  • The Shield Projector's first form, barriers before the spawns, blocked the squad's own path and lost battles. It became the absorbing shield.
  • The Engines' head start decides some maps alone (+35 points on muster-underlevels), whatever its size. So the life-aboard profile leaves them off, and the rule stays as the packet words it.

Cues

The board and the cut-in play cues; they decide nothing. Each action returns its cues in order: turnStarted, charge (with why: turn, wait, counter, crossfire, cover), moved (with the path), faced, waited, linked, attack (main, crossfire or counter, with the facing), cover, crossfire (pincer or link), counter, guard, hit (part, amount, crit, broke), miss, partBroke, holdOn, rigBreak, extracted, objective (progress) and ended.

Determinism and versions

  • Every roll is a seeded hash of seed:turn:unit:purpose:n, keyed by the battle's turn number. The package has no unseeded randomness and no clock; a test scans its sources.
  • Rules versions: 1 is CY4's core; 2 adds CY5's rules. A battle refuses an unknown version, and a version-1 battle plays exactly as CY4's did: its fields are refused in version 1, and the new state appears only in version 2. Version 2's always-on rules are the impact Stun, Fire from F hits, cover tiles and grating; with none of those in play and every new field empty, version 2 plays exactly as version 1 (a test).
  • battleStateHash(state) hashes everything that changes. Golden replays (packages/battle/src/golden/) hold seven version-1 battles on two test maps and nine version-2 battles on the test-works map (Fire across an Oil slick, current across Wet and grating, a lift mid-battle, a heroine freed by her Anchor and one by a Jack-Out, a reboot inside and outside the window, a volley dodged and one that lands): their inputs, actions, every cue and the final hash, and for each version-2 battle the cue that proves its rule. Any change to a rule or a number fails the test and names the battle; UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 rewrites them, and the commit says why.
