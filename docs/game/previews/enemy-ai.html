How enemies choose

Status: 2026-11-12, CY6 (D10). The enemy AI and ally Auto as packages/battle/src/ai.ts plays them: one scorer for both sides. The simulator plays both sides with it; the app may offer ally Auto later.

The short answer

  • On her turn, an enemy scores every sensible turn (where to stand, what to do there, which way to face) with the same numbers the forecast card shows, and takes the best.
  • She never idles (TC30): with nothing worth doing, she closes on the nearest foe or the objective.
  • The same battle always plays the same way: ties go to the seeded roll.
  • A decision takes well under a millisecond on a laptop (the phone's budget is 50 ms; CI holds it to 5 ms).
flowchart LR
  reach[Every tile she can reach] --> prune[Keep the best dozen by place, plus the two best to strike each foe from]
  prune --> acts[Every legal action from each]
  acts --> score[Score: the action + the place]
  score --> best[The best; ties to the seed]
  best --> idle{Only waiting where she stands?}
  idle -->|Yes| advance[Step toward the goal]
  idle -->|No| turn[Her turn]
  advance --> turn

Where she stands

Each tile she could end on is worth:

Worth
The threat thereLess 0.15 × every foe's best expected damage on her, for each foe that could reach the tile next round
A lethal spotLess 12 and more when the foes in reach could break her Core
Being in reachPlus 0.35 × her best expected damage on a foe she could strike from there next turn
CoverPlus 2 (plus 5 for the Steel Muster) on a cover tile
HeightPlus 0.8 a level above the foes' average, for a ranged unit
TerrainLess Fire's damage on a burning tile; less twice Live's on a Live one
The volley's marksLess 20 on a tile the Steel Muster has marked (the squad leaves them)
Her HoldA heroine with an Anchor or Collar keeps out of reach of a Netrunner's Deck
The goalLess 0.7 a step from it: the objective's tile, a terminal to hack, else the nearest foe; a ranged unit keeps near her best range instead

Caution (the threat and the lethal spot) fades to nothing over 20 rounds, so a fight always closes.

What she does

ActionWorth
An attackIts expected damage; plus the chance to break the target's Core × (20 + her threat); plus each limb's break chance × what it carries (a weapon arm 6, Legs 4, a shield arm 4); plus a share for a hurt target (focus fire); plus each ally who'd join; less 0.7 × the counter she'd draw, and more if it could break her
The leader×1.4 on the leader when the objective is to defeat her; when she's to be freed, breaking her is avoided and the squad aims at her Hold
A barrel or pipeBy who stands near: foes count for, allies against, more under fire
TechIts chance × the worth: Stun her threat × 1.5 + 3, Jam 3 if she has Charge, Jack-Out 35
A hackA turret 14, the lights 4 against ranged foes, a lift 2, a door 1.5; plus 25 toward a hack objective
Mend, itemsCore HP back, more the more hurt she is; a limb 5 or 6; Reboot 30
A barrierOnly under fire, and never beside another
Wait0.8 below her Charge cap, 0.2 at it

Charge: an action that would leave her below 1 Charge costs 1.5, to keep a counter, unless it's worth more.

The Steel Muster prizes cover, and when the volley is coming and every other Muster gun is reloading, a Muster unit holds her fire.

Facing: toward the most foes in her front quarter, the nearest breaking a tie.
