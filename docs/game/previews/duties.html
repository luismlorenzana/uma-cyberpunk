Duties, the ship log and jump events

Built behavior. For the planned changes to Uma-only Act I and II operations policy and expedition availability, see the four-act campaign guide and roadmap.

Status: 2026-11-13, CY16 (D27). The crew hold posts in the ship's modules. Each post makes its output once a watch onto one ship-log card, applied at once. Aptitude, running style, mastery and station chiefs shape the output, auto-assign and presets keep it to seconds a stop, and a jump sometimes brings an event card. The design is life-aboard.md §6 (TC67, TC68) and §5.5 (TC66).

The short answer

  • Posts: each running module has posts: a room one a level, a battle system one. An Uma holds one post at a time, until it's changed. The Duties button on the ship opens every post; a module's quick look lists its own.
  • Aptitude, by thresholds: in the post's band (the better of two where it names two), band 2 or less works at half, band 3 does the job, band 4–5 adds the post's extra. A matching role adds the post's perk. It shows as a three-dot mark before she's assigned.
  • Running style as tenure:
    • a Front Runner is full from the first watch;
    • a Pace Chaser is full and never halved when deployed;
    • a Late Surger gains half again from her third watch in a row;
    • an End Closer grows 10% a watch, to half again.

    Mastery stars at 3, 8 and 15 watches at a post add 10% each. A station chief (the casting's chiefs) adds a quarter at her room's post.

  • One ship-log card a watch lists every post's output, already applied. A post held by an Uma deployed in the battle that ends the watch makes half (a Pace Chaser full), and the card says so. Deterministic: no failure rolls, nothing decays, no duty fatigue. An Uma resting at Strain 3 can stand duty.
  • No chores: Auto-assign fills every post with the best fits (aptitude, then a matching role, then mastery, then her liked duty; the post fewest fit at their best goes first) and sends an Uma at Strain 3 to the Med Bay or the Observation Deck. Two presets keep arrangements.
  • A liked duty (the post that asks for her best band) pays +5 rapport a watch. Two Umas on one module's posts build camaraderie (CY17's partners).
  • The cook makes the next battle's meal, which goes into the battle's input as meal (played in CY17).
  • Jump events: one jump in three draws a card, from the world's region pool or the game's generic twelve. Each choice may be checked by the holder of a named post (a pilot's Speed, a navigator's Wit) and pays from the wallet, items or rapport. Skip is always there.

Each module's posts, in order: the Bridge holds a Pilot, then a Navigator, then a second Pilot. Engineering, the Hangar, the Log, the Store, Comms, the Sim Pod, the Lab, the Med Bay, the Galley, the Workshop, the Garden and the Observation Deck hold one more of their post a level. Each battle system holds one: Battery a Gunner, Sensors a Navigator, the Shield Projector an Engineer, the Drone Bay a Mechanic, the Engines a Pilot. The Quarters and the Guest Wing hold none.

Outputs: a numeric output is its amount × the factor, half again at band 4–5 and a quarter more with the role's perk. A discrete one (Strain treated, a piece made, a build sped) lands every watch at full, every other watch at half, and every watch at band 4–5.

The ship-log card

Shown on the ship after a watch: each holder, her post, what it made, "(half: deployed)" where it applies, and the wallet's gain. Everything is already applied; Done closes it. A jump's event card waits until the log is closed.

Jump events (TC66)

A jump draws an event when a fixed hash over the watch and the world says so, one time in three. The world's region names its pool (events/<region>.json); without one, the generic pool. A checked choice passes when the holder of its post has the band (at or more), else pays its fail; no holder fails. Rewards may cost (negative credits, Alloy or Data, never below zero) and rapport goes to the holder. The stand-in's sw1 is region ring, with three events.
