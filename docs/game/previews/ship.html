The ship and the worlds

Status: 2026-11-12, CY10 (D14). The hub every session starts and ends in (TC9, TC49), the Bridge's star map and a world's map (TC50), with their locks and keys (TC34). Played on the stand-in story until the story session's first world lands. The mission flow is on missions.md.

The short answer

  • The ship is the home screen: a side cutaway of its decks, one hotspot a room. Four rooms are open from the start; three open when the lead whose story names them comes aboard.
  • The Bridge shows the star map (the worlds known; a tap, a jump card, travel, no fuel and no clock), then the current world's map: each location locked, available or cleared, with its danger and, when locked, its key.
  • A Continue card on the ship says what's waiting, and the app reopens where the player was.

Which lead opens which room is the story's (casting.json's opens); the list and its order are the game's (packages/domain/src/ship/rooms.ts). A tap on a room opens its quick look: an open room's summary and Enter, or a locked room's key ("Opens when … comes aboard"). The Menu on the ship holds Account and the test benches (the sandbox, the board bench, the story test).

The star map

The worlds the crew knows, and those a finished chapter has opened (a location whose keys are now met), at their place from the world's file. The current world is cyan, known ones green, newly reachable ones amber. A tap shows a jump card with the world's look; Jump travels there at once. A new game knows the first lead's world and stands on it.

A world's map

The current world's locations as nodes, joined by their links:

StateMeansColour
LockedA key in its needs isn't met: a chapter to finish, a mission to win or a Log File to find; a tap names itGrey, with a lock
AvailableA scene or a mission still waits thereCyan
ClearedNothing waits there now. A world never closesGreen

Each node shows its danger in diamonds. A tap on an available node plays the scene due there (a talk scene, then an arrive one), or starts a mission there not yet won.

What's due: a chapter's scenes play in order, so only the first unseen scene of each chapter can play, and only once the chapter's own needs are met. A scene triggered after a mission plays when that mission is won.

The Continue card

On the ship, top right: Continue a mission in progress (and the step it's at), else Next: the place the next scene waits on the current world. When the app opens with a mission in progress, it goes straight back to it.
