The ship as a build

Status: 2026-11-13, CY15 (D26). The ship is built by the player: decks of module slots, rooms and battle systems with three levels each, building over watches, a wallet that missions pay into, the reactor's pips and the battle systems powered before each sortie, and the Lab's research. Duties at the rooms' posts come in CY16; the battle systems' effects in battle in CY17. The design is life-aboard.md §5 (TC62, TC63, TC66).

The short answer

  • The hull is decks of slots: two decks of five at a new game, a third and a fourth from research (Hull I and II) or a story refit, to six decks; every deck widens to six slots with Hull III.
  • Modules are rooms (always running) and battle systems (powered per sortie). Each takes one slot or two and has three levels. A new game has the Bridge, the Hangar, the Quarters, the Log and Engineering placed at level 1.
  • Building: tap an empty slot, pick an unlocked module, pay, and it builds over its watches (one to three). One build at a time, two from Engineering level 3. A module's quick look offers the next level the same way. Level 3 waits on its family's research. No upkeep, ever.
  • Unlocks: the Lab, the Galley, the Battery and the Sensors from the start; a lead's opens (her room is placed at once at level 1 if a slot is free); the rest by research.
  • The wallet: a mission's results pay its credits, its salvage as Alloy, and Data (5 a battle, 10 a win).
  • Power: Engineering's reactor has pips (3, 5, 7 by level, one more for each Reactor project). Before each sortie the deploy screen lists the installed battle systems; the player powers what fits the pips. The choice goes into the battle's input as systems, and rules version 3 (CY17) plays them.
  • The Lab: one project at a time, paid in Data, taking watches; a level 2 Lab takes a watch off longer projects.
  • The map by capability: a world's location can need ship:<capability> (the drive, the storm shielding, the sensors), and its locked node says so in words.

The decks

The ship's screen is the cutaway of the decks. Each module is a panel in its slot, with:

  • its name and its level as one to three bars;
  • a lit edge: cyan for a room, magenta for a battle system;
  • stripes and its watches left while building.

An empty slot is a dashed +. A tap on a module opens its quick look:

  • what its level adds;
  • the next level's cost, watches and what it adds, with Upgrade, or why not;
  • Enter for a room with a page;
  • Research for the Lab.

A tap on an empty slot opens the build sheet: every unlocked module not yet aboard, with its cost, watches and what it adds, and Build, or why not. The header shows the wallet and the reactor's pips.

Older saves (v6 and before) open with their open rooms and Engineering placed on two decks of five in a fixed order, at level 1, and the starter wallet: 500 credits, 20 Alloy, 20 Data.

Modules

Costs are by family. Levels 1 and 2 are always open; level 3 needs the family's project:

  • core: Core Refit;
  • rooms: Room Refit;
  • quarters: Private Cabins;
  • systems: Systems Refit.

Power before a sortie

The deploy screen's Ship systems panel lists the installed battle systems with their pips against the reactor's. A toggle that would go over is refused, and the choice is kept for the next sortie. The powered systems go into the battle's frozen input as systems. CY15 records and shows them; rules version 3 (CY17) plays them.

The map by capability (TC66)

A world's or a location's needs may name ship:drive-1, ship:drive-2, ship:drive-3 (the Engines' levels), ship:storm (the Shield Projector) or ship:sensors (the Sensors). A capability holds while its module runs at that level; it needs no power. The locked node says "Needs the storm shielding". The stand-in's sw2 has such a place, the storm ridge.

The story's refit

A scene's refit step ({ "refit": true }) adds a deck, to six. The stand-in's Lead B chapter ends with one.
