The crew

Status: 2026-11-12, CY11 (D15). Who is aboard and what each Uma is in a fight: her bands from master data's 5★ stats (TC4), her role (TC5), her Stride (TC46), her level, and Strain (TC27). Her rig is on hangar.md. Shown in the Quarters: a deck of cabin doors, her cabin with her profile, and who rests next mission (CY14: cabins.md).

The short answer

  • The crew is the save's: each Uma aboard has a record (her outfit id, XP, role, rig, three saved loadouts, Strain), in join order. The story brings her aboard (join, aboard).
  • Her numbers come from master data at run time, through one adapter: her 5★ stats become five bands, her best running style her Stride and, untagged, her role. An Uma master data doesn't hold shows as a stand-in card, at band 3 everywhere with no Stride; nothing breaks.
  • Her role is the story's authored tag, else her running style's job; she changes it aboard.
  • Strain: a mission adds 1 to everyone who fought; at 3 she sits the next one out.

The bands (TC4)

Every outfit's 5★ stats sum to 550, so only the spread differs. Each stat becomes a band from 1 to 5 (70–89, 90–109, 110–129, 130–149, 150 and up; below 70 is still band 1), and each band does one job in combat and one on the rig. The numbers are the battle package's (battle.md's tuning table):

Her profile shows each band as a bar with her own numbers beside it.

Which applies: a role set aboard, else the story's tag (casting.json's roles, by outfit id), else her best running style's (ties to the earlier style: Front, Pace, Late, End), else Striker. Her profile says which.

A change aboard: tap a role in her profile. Her rig keeps every part but the Back unit, which becomes the new role's starter unless she already wears one of its kind; a rig still on "best for her role" simply follows the new role.

Level and XP

A mission pays XP as a plain number (missions.md): the squad takes it in full, the crew aboard half, and a loss keeps its XP (TC25). A newcomer joins at the squad's level (CY12): the crew's average XP, or the XP of the battle she just fought, whichever is higher, so a lead never comes aboard at level 1 after her Chapter 1's fight (the loop). Her level is 1 plus one for each 100 XP, up to 50: a placeholder curve until Phase 5's economy, and levels change no numbers yet.

Resting, she still takes half the XP, and the squad can call one bench support a battle from a resting Uma's role: every Uma aboard and not deployed is the battle's bench (battle.md).

The Quarters

Since CY14 the Quarters are a deck of cabin doors (cabins.md): her face tile with her bond rank and Strain in the corners, dimmed while resting, and her cabin's Comfort lights below it. A door opens her cabin, whose Profile tab holds what the roster's profile did:

  • her bust, level and XP;
  • her role and where it comes from, the role buttons, her Back unit;
  • her Stride, her Strain, the band table;
  • Her rig in the Hangar.

Below the doors: who rests next mission, and everyone's Strain. A rest in a comfortable cabin sheds more Strain (cabins.md).

Approved outfits in the combined campaign

Crew Stories offers Wardrobe after a recruit joins. Its six buttons show Street, Ship Casual, Quarters, Pilot suit, Combat armor and Helmet on. This is a portrait viewer; it costs nothing and does not change the saved rig or battle role. Public ship conversations use Ship Casual, private cabin scenes use Quarters, active stations use Pilot, and equipped missions keep their authored suit. Explicit scene staging takes priority. The original app's Quarters Profile tab offers the same six portrait choices. See the wardrobe contract.
