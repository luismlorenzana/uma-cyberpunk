The captain and bonds

Built behavior. For the planned changes to relationships across all acts and independent clearance, see the four-act campaign guide and roadmap.

Status: 2026-11-13, CY13 (D22). The player as the ship's captain (TC55), the watch every life-aboard budget counts by, and a bond with every Uma from rank 1 to 10 (TC56, TC57): rapport from battles, messages and bond scenes, the story's gates, the catch-up, and the Trust or Heart choice at rank 8. Cabins and visits came with CY14 (cabins.md); the bonds' perks play in battle with rules version 3 since CY17 (battle.md), where the captain calls a rank-10 Uma's name for Resonance, and partners fight as a pair. The design is life-aboard.md.

The short answer

  • A new game names the captain before anything plays: a name, pronouns (they by default) and the Romance setting. The captain is never drawn. Account changes all three later.
  • A watch passes when a mission's results close and when the ship jumps. Never real time.
  • Every Uma aboard has a bond, rank 1 to 10, shown as a ring on her profile and her roster tile. Rapport fills it: fighting, covering, joining a Crossfire, replying to her message after a battle, and the captain's choices in her bond scenes. Below the crew's median rank, rapport counts double.
  • Ranks 4, 6, 8 and 10 can wait on the story (the casting's bondGates); rapport keeps filling, and the rank lands when the key is met.
  • Bond scenes play from her profile once she reaches their rank, replay from the Log, and never go missing. At rank 8 she says what she hopes for, then the player chooses Trust or Heart; under Friends only it's always Trust. Both are equal in battle.

A new game, or a save from before CY13, shows the captain screen once, on the ship, before the opening; Take command saves it. Account's Captain panel changes any of the three.

In scene text the story writes {captain} and {they}, {them}, {their}, {theirs}, {they're} (or capitalised, {They}…), and the scene player fills them from the save. The story check refuses any other placeholder.

The watch

A counter on the save: +1 when a mission's results close (won or lost) and +1 on a jump from the Bridge. Messages are per battle; CY14's visits (three a watch, cabins.md), CY15's builds and CY16's duties count by it. No timers, no real time.

  • The catch-up: an Uma below the crew's median rank earns double, so a late recruit catches up.
  • Gates: rapport keeps filling past a closed gate; the rank lands the moment the key is met (the ship checks on opening). The ring's next segment and the profile say which key it waits on.
  • Never lost: rapport never decays. A replay from the Log changes nothing.
  • Only Umas aboard take rapport; a lead fighting her own Chapter 1 starts at rank 1 when she comes aboard.

Her message: after each battle, every Uma aboard who fought writes one line (a generic pool until the story writes hers), shown as ✉ on the ship. Reply pays +15 and reads it; a new battle replaces the unanswered ones.

Bond scenes (TC57)

A bond scene is a story file's scene at rank 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 (story.md). Once she reaches a written rank, her roster tile shows ! and her profile Her rank N scene; the lowest unplayed one comes first. It plays in the scene player, with two steps of its own:

StepOn screen
captainThe captain's two or three answers, each with its tone (♥ warm, ◆ steady, ~ wry); her last line stays above them
pathAt rank 8: Trust (partners in arms) or Heart (a light romance), her line above, "both are equal in battle" below; under Friends only it doesn't appear

Her path shows on her ring (◆ cyan, ♥ magenta) and names her rank-10 Resonance: Resolve for Trust, Devotion for Heart. Her epilogue card (the bond file's epilogue) appears in the Log once she reaches rank 10.

Her profile and the Log

  • The bond panel on her profile: the ring, the rank and path, the rapport toward the next rank (or the key it waits on), the perks of each rank (lit as reached; played in battle since CY17), her partner and her camaraderie with each Uma aboard, and her ready scene.
  • The roster shows each Uma's rank (♦) and a ! when a scene is ready.
  • The Log's Bonds tab lists the bond scenes each Uma has played, to replay, and her epilogue card at rank 10.
