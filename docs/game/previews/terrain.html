Terrain, props and terminals

Status: 2026-11-12, CY5 (D9). Rules version 2. How the board itself changes in a battle: the five tile states, the props that make them, cover and grating, the terminals, and the terrain card. The numbers are in battle.md's tuning table, rendered from packages/battle/src/tuning.ts.

The short answer

  • A tile can be in one state: Wet, Oil, Fire or Smoke. Live is worked out, never stored: every cable's tile, and current spreading at once across connected Wet tiles and grating.
  • States spread at once: Fire across a connected Oil slick, current across Wet and grating, and current meeting Oil sets it alight. Wet puts Fire out and can't catch.
  • Props make states: a barrel bursts into Oil, a pipe into Wet, a cable is Live. Terminals move the map: doors, turrets, lifts and the lights.
  • A long press on any tile gives its terrain card.

Timers count in rounds (100 ticks) and run out as the clock passes them. Entering a Live tile mid-move hurts once a tile; ending the turn on it hurts again.

Props

Props sit in the map's props list, each with an id. parseMap checks them: unique ids, tiles on the map, barrels, pipes and terminals off spawns and solid tiles, a terminal's door or lift existing, a lift's heights 0–3.

PropOn the boardWhen hit
BarrelTakes its tile: no standing, shots pass overAny hit bursts it: Oil on its tile and its four neighbours; under an F weapon, alight at once
PipeTakes its tile like a barrelAny hit bursts it: Wet on its tile and its four neighbours

TerminalTakes its tile like a barrelHacked, not hit
DoorIts tiles are solid and block sight while it's shut—
LiftIts tiles stand at its low or high height—

Barriers (the Bulwark's) are on the board too: solid, block sight, 40 HP; any attack can hit one.

Terminals

A unit with a Deck within range 1–4 of a terminal hacks it as her action, for 1 Charge, with no roll. Each hack toggles or fires its effect, and a terminal counts once for "hack N terminals" however often it's used.

A doorOpens or shuts its tiles. It won't shut on someonedoor (open, tiles)

A turret is a unit with fixed: true: it never moves, and fights on whichever side it's on.

The terrain card

terrainCard(state, tile) answers the board's long press: its height, its ground (floor, solid, grating or cover), its state and the rounds left on it, whether it's Live, the props on it, what could change it, and what it does to whoever stands there, in words.
