The sandbox

Built behavior. For the planned changes to playable campaign milestones and the use of internal fixtures, see the four-act campaign guide and roadmap.

Status: 2026-11-13, CY8 (D12), tier 1's Umas (D19). The second of the three ways the combat is tested (TC54): any skirmish set up and played with the full battle screen, on the phone, at /sandbox. The battle screen itself is on battle.md.

The short answer

  • Set up a battle from a scenario (CY6's format): the scenarios in git, or ones saved on this phone. Pick the seed (typed or rolled), who plays each side (player or AI), and who fills each squad slot: one of tier 1's Umas (with her role), or the scenario's stand-in and its rig.
  • Tier 1's Umas come from master data at run time: her name and face, her bands from her 5★ stats, her Stride, and the best rig for her role, as the crew fights (CY11). Five of the ten walk the board as their authored chibis; the others stand as cards with their faces. Enemies stay stand-ins until the heroines arrive (Phase 4).
  • Play it on the board with the full battle screen.
  • It autosaves after every action, to a local row, and Resume picks it up after iOS closes the app.
  • The results give the minutes and turns, which feed the simulator's pace.

The squad: tier 1's Umas (D19)

The Ranker's tier 1 (uma-platform's Playable Umas T1, its first art batch) is ten outfits, kept in git by id only (apps/web/src/crew/tier1.ts, as uma-quest keeps its batch 1); everything about them comes from master data's release at run time:

WhatFrom
Her name and faceThe release's outfits and characters, and outfit-faces
Her bands and StrideHer 5★ stats and her best running style, through the crew's adapter (crew.md)
Her roleThe one picked, else her running style's (Front Striker, Pace Vanguard, Late Gunner, End Netrunner)
Her rigBest for her role and bands, never over Capacity (hangar.md)
How she looks on the boardHer sprite set from outfit-sprites when the release has one (the authored chibis: five of tier 1 today), else a card with her face; a stand-in sprite only if the release can't be read
  • The picks are this phone's (kept in its local storage), applied when a battle starts: the scenarios stay stand-in only, so Copy, Paste and Save move the same scenario whatever the squad.
  • The bench: tier 1's Umas not in the squad wait aboard, so the bench support call offers each of their roles once.
  • Loading: the picked squad's chibis and faces start downloading while the setup shows, so Start is quick; a first battle brings about 1.6 MB a chibi, then they're cached on the phone. "Bringing the squad aboard… 2 of 4" shows while any are still coming; whatever hasn't loaded in 25 s plays as a stand-in.
  • On the battle screen, her face is on her timeline chip and her unit card, and her bust on her side of the cut-in.

A re-rank in the Ranker changes tier 1; the list is mirrored by hand. The story session's casting replaces it as the crew when the first world lands.

Autosave and Resume

  • After every committed action, and again when the page hides, the sandbox writes one row: the scenario, the frozen battle input, the actions, the rules version, when it started and when it changed.
  • The row is local, in its own small database (uma-cyberpunk-sandbox), never the synced save: a sandbox battle is a test, not progress, so it bumps no save version, sends no sync traffic, and can't touch account.updatedAt.
  • Opening the sandbox with a row offers Resume (the actions replayed on the stored input, landing on exactly the state they left) and Restart (the same scenario and seed from the start). If the actions no longer replay (the rules changed under them), it says so and offers Restart.
  • CY10 moves mission battles into the save in the same shape.

Results

Won or lost; player turns; the wall-clock minutes since the battle started; parts broken, counters, Crossfires and Covers; how many enemies were beaten and freed. Retry plays the same seed again, New seed a fresh one, Back to setup returns.

The test scenario

The sandbox: the Steel Muster and its Key (sandbox-muster), on underlevels-14: 14 × 14, heights 0–3 with the high ground at the back corners, cover tiles, a grating run, a Wet area beside it, an Oil slick under the enemy line, a pipe beside a live cable, and a door on a terminal.

SideUnits
SquadVanguard A, Striker A (Break Stride), Gunner A, Netrunner A; two repair kits and a smoke charge; a Medic on the bench
EnemyThe Key, the leader, on the high ground; five android troopers; all Steel Muster (cover, reload, the volley, pinning fire)
ObjectiveDefeat the leader

Played AI against AI over 300 seeds it's even (the squad wins 47.7%, in 27 player turns); a player usually does better.
