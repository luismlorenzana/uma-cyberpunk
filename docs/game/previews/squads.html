Squads, operations, standing and readiness

Status: 2026-11-13, CY18 (D31). The crew forms named squads that grow cohesion by fighting together. A squad not deployed runs an operation and comes back after its watches with a deterministic outcome. Each faction has a standing with the ship, and a finale measures the ship's readiness: each unmet need makes its battle harder, never anyone lost. The design is life-aboard.md §7 (TC69, TC70, TC71) and §5.3 (TC64).

The short answer

  • Squads (TC69): up to four, each with a name, a banner, a leader and one to four members. An Uma is in one squad at a time, or none. The Quarters deck shows a row a squad: rename it, move Umas between squads (or to a new one), and star a leader. The deploy screen picks a squad in one tap, or any four by hand.
  • Newcomers: the casting's canonical squads form once all their members are aboard. Anyone else joins the first squad with room, else a new squad while there are fewer than four.
  • Cohesion: +1 for each battle a squad fights with three or more of its members, won or lost. It reaches level 1–5 at 2, 5, 9, 14 and 20 battles. In battle:
    • level 1 gives the leader +1 Charge at the start;
    • level 3 gives every member +1 Crossfire reach;
    • level 5 gives every member one free Cover a battle.

    It counts for more in operations (+3 power a level).

  • Operations (TC70): Comms offers two to four at a time on the worlds visited: the story's own and a generic table's. Send a squad and it's back after the operation's watches. A mission's end or a jump passes a watch. The outcome is deterministic, and Comms shows it before you send. The squad's power (levels, bond ranks, cohesion, partner pairs) is weighed against 12 × danger for full and 8 × danger for partial; less is thin:
    • partial pays 60% and thin 30%, and each costs a point of Strain;
    • standing moves only on a full success;
    • nobody is ever lost.

    Members away can't deploy, and they hold their posts at half. Recall brings a squad back at once with thin rewards. Each return writes a report on the ship-log card.

  • Faction standing (TC71): each faction the story names stands from −2 (Hostile) to +2 (Allied). It starts at the story's value, or −1 (Wary) when the story doesn't give one. It moves three ways:
    • by the story's standing step;
    • by operations that name the faction;
    • by +1 once three of the faction's heroines are freed.

    A standing:<faction>:<level> key opens a location, a chapter or an operation. The Log's Factions tab and the Bridge show every standing.

  • Readiness (TC64): one total on the Bridge's meter:
    • 2 for each level a faction stands above Hostile;
    • 3 for each operation won in full (each counts once);
    • 4 for each battle system built.

    A story mission marked finale names its needs (a system built, a faction's standing, the total), each with the harsh condition its absence adds: fog (lights out for three rounds), hazards (a hull breach: four tiles of Fire across the middle row) or no reinforcements (the bench call is gone). The briefing lists them. Never permadeath.

The deploy screen shows a button for each squad that isn't away. A tap fills the slots with the chapter's leads first, then the squad's members who can deploy (not resting, not away), up to four. The default squad starts the same way: the leads, their squads' members, then the rest of the crew.

A battle counts for a squad when three or more of its members are deployed, won or lost. The battle input carries cohesion: { level, leader, members } for the squad with the most members deployed. When its leader stays aboard, its first member deployed leads.

The simulator (pnpm sim balance, 200 seeds a scenario) plays life aboard with cohesion 5 against life aboard without it: −0.4 points on average, within noise and inside the target (−2 to +8). Cohesion's weight is in operations.

Operations

What's on offer: the worlds visited, the current one first. Each world gives:

  • the story's operations in its file whose needs are met and that haven't been won in full;
  • then the generic table, two to four by a hash of the world's id.

The first four not already running are on offer. Generic operations can be run again, but each counts once for readiness. A story operation won in full is done.

Danger is the world's lowest location danger (the raid one more).

Power: each member adds her level + 2 × her bond rank. The squad adds 3 × its cohesion level, and 4 for each partner pair inside it.

Outcome: full at 12 × danger or more, partial at 8 × danger, else thin. Comms shows what the chosen squad would bring back before you send it.

Sending: a squad can't go while it's away already, while a member is resting at Strain 3, or while a member is in the mission under way. While it's away:

  • its members can't deploy (the deploy screen says "away until watch N: recall her squad from Comms");
  • a chapter's lead who's away holds the mission until she's back or recalled;
  • they hold their posts at half, as a deployed Uma does; the ship-log card says "(half: away)".

The return: at the watch it's due, the squad comes back. The pay goes into the wallet (and into the card's wallet line), and Strain lands on each member (+1 for partial or thin, never past 3). A full success moves the faction's standing and counts the operation. A report joins that watch's ship-log card ("Vanguard is back from patrol the ring…: a full success. +80 credits, +5 alloy, +5 data, dock-union +1.").

Recall: at once, thin rewards, with its report on the current card.

There's no free "wait" button. Watches pass at a mission's end and at a jump, as before (D31).

The factions are every faction named by a world's factions and the heroines' faction. A faction's standing now is its start (the casting's standing, else −1) moved by every step and operation, plus 1 once three of its heroines are freed. That bonus is worked out from the missions won, whose boss pool holds her board. It always stays within −2 to +2.

The save keeps a faction's value only once it has moved; the start stays the story's. Standing keys (standing:<faction>:<level>) open what they guard when the faction is at that level or higher. Their lock reads "Needs dock-union at Friendly".

Readiness and finales

The total: 2 × (standing + 2) for every faction, + 3 × operations won in full, + 4 × battle systems built (level 1 or more).

The meter, on the Bridge's star map and world map, folds to one chip ("Readiness 6 · 2 unmet"). Open, it lists:

  • the formula;
  • the next finale (the first story mission marked finale not won yet), with each need ✓ or ✗;
  • every faction's standing as a five-step bar.

A finale's needs: { system, else } is met when that battle system is built. { standing, at, else } is met when the faction is at that level or higher, and { total, else } when readiness reaches the number. The briefing lists them; at deploy, each unmet need adds its else to the battle input's conditions:

The stand-in

The invented stand-in story shows every piece:

  • The Vanguard: a canonical squad of Lead A, Crew C and Crew D, formed once all three are aboard.
  • A second faction: sw1 adds dock-union, starting Neutral, beside steel-muster, which starts Hostile.
  • Two story operations on sw1:
    • "Patrol the ring for the dock union": danger 1, 2 watches, dock-union +1;
    • "Cut the muster's supply lines": danger 2, 3 watches, steel-muster −1, after Chapter 1.
  • A standing step: Chapter 1's debrief moves dock-union +1.
  • A finale, Chapter 2 on sw2's storm ridge: it needs the storm shielding to reach. It has two readiness needs: the Sensors built (else fog) and dock-union at Friendly (else hazards).

The check flags:

  • squad members not in the cast;
  • unknown factions (in standing, operations, steps and readiness);
  • unknown systems;
  • operation ids used twice, and standing moved without a faction;
  • readiness on a mission that isn't a finale.
