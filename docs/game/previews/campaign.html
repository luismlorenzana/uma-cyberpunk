Playing the four-act campaign

Open Campaign and play First Watch on the Homestretch. On a campaign with station operations, Home opens the four-deck cutaway. Choose a deck or tap a room. Use pinch or the zoom buttons to inspect it. A room shows whether it is operational, being built, or paused for power. Start from 20 permanent cabins and 30 reserve rooms. Habitat A raises permanent capacity to 120 and Habitat B raises it to 280.

Use Recruit crew to find all 50 sign-ons across the five home systems. The starter cutter shows the fuel and watches for the trip before you depart. Finish the meeting to invite the recruit, or leave the offer open and return later. The Roster opens her Act I once she joins. Dock at the station to transfer supplies between its reserves and the cutter. Local port stock remains available in Navigation and Cargo.

Use the date button for Captain’s Calendar. Duty shifts pay out when the Duty watch ends. Staff the Reactor to recover alloy and data, check the week ahead for construction and shift output, and reserve a Free watch for an eligible conversation. Advancing a watch can complete projects or move the station. Check Quarters to find a resident, change their cabin, or open their profile. An eligible returned Lieutenant can take a vacant permanent cabin from her Roster entry.

Use Navigation to jump or load supplies, Ship systems to fund projects, and Hangar to refit or craft. During an expedition, Home shows the active cutter and its separate cargo. Navigation lists the current stop, the remaining route, the Gate and the return. Ship systems shows the cutter’s officers and escort fit.

Open Roster to find the 50 recruitment candidates, the 50 Generals and the 150 Lieutenants. Select a character to see her chapters, her current status and port, and relevant wardrobe or relationship options. Sign on at the listed port and continue from the same character's chapter list. Use Duo operations for shared bases, evidence, returns and expeditions. Personal interactions lists Captain rapport, completed milestones and the next visit when eligible. Returned Lieutenants can reopen their return conversation and join authored watches if assigned to the active expedition.

Open Operations for story actions and ship or ground encounters. Filter by act, type or status and search by operation or port. The ready list uses the current location. The completed list shows settled progress. Ship rows show their supply cost. Ground rows show the tactical objective. Expedition Readiness shows each duo’s returns and clearances.

A first settled ship or ground victory gives 50 XP to each eligible participating crew member. Check the character’s level and XP in Roster. Ship credit follows the assigned bridge officers and required personal lead. Ground credit follows the deployed squad. Levels 5, 10 and 15 each add 1 Core HP to new ground deployments. The Hangar compares part slots, Load, Move, armor and weapons. Check Effective sortie stats in the mission’s equipment step for the exact rig and stats. Lieutenants keep their assigned expedition kits.

The 50 sign-ons span the five home systems. A recruited Uma receives her quarters, and her Crew Stories card opens her origin and personal missions through ship combat, ground capture and custody. Training is optional. Captain visits use the existing evening flow.

Five capital operations are playable on one save. Open Expedition Readiness to see your capital tally, each duo’s lieutenant returns and both General clearances. A duo can depart once its own six lieutenants have returned, both Generals are cleared and all five capitals are liberated. With station operations, build and power the Shipyard, then commission the duo's cutter while docked at Homestretch. Muster its crew at the launch berth with the listed supplies.

After a duo establishes its local base, open Ship systems at its home port. Fund the base project with Alloy, then use the normal watch to advance construction. The base report follows the actual funding, completion, or supply state. Once operating, the project produces at its port. Navigation shows the next jump cost, port transfers, the net output per watch, and provision upkeep. You can advance a supply watch at the dock. Loading fuel or provisions transfers up to eight available units while protecting the port’s reserve.

Each of the 25 expeditions takes two Umas, two Generals and six lieutenants. Any ready duo can be the first to cross the Gate. The game records that crossing for later voyages. Its bridge operations use the small ship’s cargo. After the first approach, the crew can go off duty during a safe pause. A supply report appears when the next ship operation needs more than the voyage has aboard. Between sorties, withdraw to the launch berth for one fuel. There you can resupply, reinforce the hull or repair damage, then resume the same route. A cutter at a safe berth can be parked so you can return to it later. Before a ship sortie, choose among the ten expedition members for the four bridge stations. Assigning a specialist to a matching station gives a second drill rank. Choose Deploy all ten to take the full crew into tactics. Follow the objective shown in each battle. Keep the named Herald or Echo alive in the final ground operation to unlock the release scene. Finish the return to restore access to the Homestretch and unload any remaining supplies.

The finale and return to play

The five liberated capitals and five recorded witness releases open the finale. At Halcyon, choose an eligible ten-person formation. Secure the civilian nodes, commit the common cut, and hear your recorded witnesses. The outer Queen operations can be played in any order. Follow the objective and keep the named Seraine alive.

The last approach uses the bridge assignments and the chosen formation's cutter in a station campaign. Choose who will speak at the berth before deploying the formation. The ground battle requires separating the witness channels before breaking the Collar. Both named Seraine must survive. The briefing and objective list track which actions are available. Withdraw and retry from the crew report if the attempt fails.

Both approaches to Lark lead to the shared berth. Supper and the folded request reopen travel. The ordinary ending is available there. Recorded mutual relationship choices can open the True Ending. A private reunion or shared visit has its own choices and can be revisited if deferred. Civic events appear where the relevant officers are available. At a repair site, survey first, then complete the two work stages. The project remembers its stage if you leave and return.

Training is optional after signing on. Gear, replays, and other established game systems are described in the manual.
