The Hangar

Status: 2026-11-12, CY11 (D15). Where each Uma's rig is built (TC17, TC18): eight slots and a Coat, the Capacity bar against the total Load, every part's numbers before a swap, three saved loadouts, and "best for her role". The rules are the battle package's; the Hangar reads Load, Capacity and Move from it and never recomputes them.

The short answer

The Laser rifle uses both hands and fires three pulses at 1–3 tiles. It keeps the hand-rifle part ID and pierce damage; Long weapons remain Back-mounted. Older worn/preset rifle combinations stow incompatible off-hand gear. Inventory and campaign progress remain, and an in-progress battle finishes with its original frozen loadout (D73).

  • Eight slots and a Coat: Core, Left arm, Right arm, Legs, Back unit, Visor, Left hand, Right hand, and the Coat.
  • Capacity is 10 + 2 a Power band, plus the Core's and the Back unit's bonus. Over it, she can't deploy, and the deploy screen says why. At 70% load or less, she gets +1 Move.
  • Before a swap, every part shows what she'd have wearing it: part HP, Move, Capacity left, and hit and damage for a weapon.
  • "Best for her role" in one tap: the best rig of the parts owned for her role and bands, never over Capacity, always with her role's Back unit.
  • Three saved loadouts per Uma, named by the player.
  • The battle wears her rig: deploy puts her bands, her rig as a loadout and her Stride into the mission's battle.

Hit is her weapon's base accuracy plus 3% a Wit band, before her target's evasion and Cover; damage is a hit's, with +2 a Power band for Melee, before facing, height and Coats (battle.md).

Hands: a two-handed weapon goes in the right hand and empties the left; putting something in the left hand takes a two-handed weapon out of the right. A Long weapon only fires from a Launcher Back unit; Tech comes from the Deck.

Best for her role

Every rig of the parts owned with her role's Back unit (the Gunner has two) is scored for her role and bands, and the best that fits her Capacity is worn; ties go to the first in the Hangar's order, so it's the same every time. The score weighs part HP, Move (a light rig's +1 included), Guard, and her weapons' expected damage (hit × damage × hits), each role leaning its own way:

RoleLeans toward
VanguardHP, a shield, Short weapons
StrikerMove and Melee
GunnerShort and Long weapons, an Aim visor
Netrunner, MedicMove, then a light weapon

Something always fits: Capacity is at least 12, and the lightest rig with any role's Back unit and a hand weapon weighs 12 at most. Each role's default Coat is the Weave coat (P) for Vanguards and Strikers, the Heat coat (F) for Gunners, the Padded coat (I) for Netrunners and Medics; swap it for what a mission's enemies deal.

Later campaign kits

The faction missions can issue an Arc axe or Pulse lance for two-handed melee, a two-handed Marksman rifle for a single Short shot, and a three-shot Sniper rack for ranged precision. Each mission lists its main weapon and Back unit choices at deployment.

Deploy

The deploy screen (missions.md) shows each Uma's role, Load and Capacity, and refuses one who can't go, with the reason: over Capacity or a two-handed weapon with a full left hand ("fix her rig in the Hangar"), or resting at Strain 3. Hangar opens it with the mission waiting. Deploying puts into each squad slot her bands, her rig as the battle package's loadout and her Stride; the Umas aboard and not deployed are the battle's bench, by role.

The save

A rig names a starter part id for each slot; the Back unit, the hands and the Coat may be empty (null). The save refuses a part that isn't of its slot's kind. A loadout is { name, rig }, the name 24 characters at most.
