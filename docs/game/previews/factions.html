The Holds and the factions

Built behavior. For the planned changes to Palinode, returned lieutenants and Seraine, see the four-act campaign guide and roadmap.

Status: 2026-11-12, CY5 (D9). Rules version 2. How heroines are freed, never killed (TC26, WD9), and the first faction's mechanic, the Steel Muster's (WD11). Each world brings at most two new mechanics; the others arrive with their worlds. The world and the cast are the story session's (the story brief); this page holds the rules only.

The short answer

  • A heroine is never killed. She carries a Hold: an Anchor, a Collar, or none.
  • Freeing her takes her off the field and counts as beating her. Break her Anchor or Collar, or use Jack-Out.
  • Rig Break beats her. In a Collar rescue that calls for it, breaking the leader's rig also breaks her Collar.
  • The Steel Muster fights from cover, reloads after it fires, builds a shared volley it signals a round ahead, and pins arms.

The Holds

A heroine unit has hold: "anchor" | "collar" | "none" in her input.

Anchor or CollarNone
On her rigAn extra part with its own HP (30 unless she says), hit only when aimed at or by TechNothing extra
Freed byBreaking it, or a Jack-OutA Jack-Out
  • Aiming at the Hold needs her to be able to aim (a Visor with aim, or a Launcher), at −25% hit. Every hit of an aimed strike goes to the Hold. A covering ally takes the strike unaimed: there's no Hold on her to hit.
  • A landed Stun or Jam also does 10 damage to the Hold.
  • Jack-Out (2 Charge, adjacent, on a heroine below 30% Core): Wit against Guts.
  • Freed: she leaves the field (freed cue, so the board swaps her palette back, TC41), counts as beaten for every objective, and all her unbroken parts are salvage.
  • Broken (Rig Break): beaten, not freed; only her unbroken parts are salvage, and she won't join as a guest.

The battle's result lists each enemy as freed, broken or standing, with her salvage, so the app can open guests and Files later. Free the leader needs a freed leader to win. The encounter states whether Rig Break frees her.

Talk

On encounters with Talk, inspect a target for the contact hint. Move the named ally to an adjacent tile and spend her turn on Talk. Some targets only listen after a hit from that ally. A successful Talk takes the target off the field. Breaking the target's rig can lead to a different outcome. Both carry over after the battle is won and debriefed.

The Steel Muster

On when the input lists factions: ["steel-muster"]; its units carry faction: "steel-muster".

MechanicRule
CoverIts units favour cover tiles (the AI's part, CY6); the rules give cover its −20% for everyone
ReloadAfter a Muster unit fires a Short or Long weapon, she can't counter until her next turn
The volley meterShared: +1 for every Muster attack (main, Crossfire or counter), full at 6
The signalWhen it fills, the standing squad units' tiles are marked, to be hit one round later; the forecast shows the marks and when
The volleyWhen that time comes, every standing Muster unit with a Short or Long weapon and a line of fire hits each marked tile once: the weapon's power, on a part by its band's weights. Whoever left the mark dodges it. The meter then empties
Pinning fireA Muster hit on an arm costs that unit −10% hit until her next turn (her counters and Crossfire in between)

Without the flag, none of it applies, even to units marked steel-muster.
