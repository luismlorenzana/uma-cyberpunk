# Playing the four-act campaign

Open **Campaign** and play **First Watch** on the Homestretch. On a campaign with station operations, **Home** opens the four-deck cutaway. Choose a deck or tap a room. Use pinch or the zoom buttons to inspect it. The build forecast shows the completion watch, projected Alloy, power and permanent berths. A room may open with power paused. Start from 20 permanent cabins and 30 reserve rooms. Habitat A raises permanent capacity to 120 and Habitat B raises it to 280.

Use **Recruit crew** to find all 50 sign-ons across the five home systems. The starter cutter shows the fuel, arrival watch and overlapping appointments before you depart. Finish the meeting to invite the recruit, or leave the offer open and return later. The Roster opens her Act I once she joins. Dock at the station to transfer supplies between its reserves and the cutter. From an active liberation base, send surplus fuel or provisions to Homestretch. Calendar shows expected deliveries.

Use the date button for **Captain’s Calendar**. Duty starts at 0600, Free watch at 1400, and Night at 2200. Travel and committed ship and ground sorties advance the same clock. Duty output, construction, deliveries and relocation settle as watches pass. Rehabilitation appointments use Free watch. Book a visit at Night and check the listed venue and cast before leaving.

At 2200, **Quarters** gives the Captain two visits. Take two short visits or spend the remaining time on a consented overnight. Completed visits with morning scenes queue for dawn. The same person can be visited once per night, including shared visits. The evening directory lists the last visit and established bonds. A pause in visits leaves relationship progress intact.

Released Generals become eligible when their authored quarters and relationship requirements are met. Returned Lieutenants who take station quarters can join evening crew check-ins. Their return scenes and expedition duties remain in Roster. A visit helps the person recover strain at dawn and can improve her station output on the next Duty watch. A resident Lieutenant who checked in adds one provision to station reserves on that watch.

The station issues two provisions plus one per 20 residents at dawn. Staff Life Support to supply two provisions plus one per 40 residents. Homestretch can draw ration shortages from its port’s surplus. The cutter has a separate bill while it is away. **Calendar** shows the next dawn and a seven-day ration forecast. **Cargo** shows the next dawn’s remaining reserves or shortfall. Forecasts use the current assignments, queued construction and incoming deliveries. A station shortfall pauses optional fabrication and shipyard duty output until the next funded dawn.

Use **Navigation** to jump or load supplies, **Ship systems** to fund projects, and **Hangar** to refit or craft. Before departure, **Review launch budget** shows the loading requirement, planned battle costs, expected cargo on return and earliest return watch. It includes the Gate when your campaign still needs to cross it. During an expedition, Home shows the active cutter and its separate cargo. Navigation lists the current stop, the remaining route, the Gate and return. An advance at the launch berth spends one watch. Ports replenish at dawn. At a shared berth you can turn off Homestretch’s draw on port surplus to leave incoming provisions available for cutter loading. Check the station’s next dawn forecast before doing so.

Open **Roster** to find the 50 recruitment candidates, the 50 Generals and the 150 Lieutenants. Select a character to see her chapters, her current status and port, and relevant wardrobe or relationship options. Sign on at the listed port and continue from the same character's chapter list. Use **Duo operations** for shared bases, evidence, returns and expeditions. **Personal interactions** lists Captain rapport, completed milestones and the next visit when eligible. Returned Lieutenants can reopen their return conversation and join authored watches if assigned to the active expedition.

Open **Operations** for story actions and ship or ground encounters. Filter by act, type or status and search by operation or port. The ready list uses the current location. The completed list shows settled progress. Ship rows show their supply cost. Ground rows show the tactical objective. Expedition Readiness shows each duo’s returns and clearances.

A first settled ship or ground victory gives 50 XP to each eligible participating crew member. Check the character’s level and XP in **Roster**. Ship credit follows the assigned bridge officers and required personal lead. Ground credit follows the deployed squad. Levels 5, 10 and 15 each add 1 Core HP to new ground deployments. The Hangar compares part slots, Load, Move, armor and weapons. Check **Effective sortie stats** in the mission’s equipment step for the exact rig and stats. Lieutenants keep their assigned expedition kits.

The 50 sign-ons span the five home systems. A recruited Uma receives her quarters, and her Crew Stories card opens her origin and personal missions through ship combat, ground capture and custody. Training is optional. Captain visits open as each relationship becomes eligible.

Five capital operations are playable on one save. Open Expedition Readiness to see your capital tally, each duo’s lieutenant returns and both General clearances. A duo can depart once its own six lieutenants have returned, both Generals are cleared and all five capitals are liberated. With station operations, build and power the Shipyard, then queue the duo's cutter while docked at Homestretch. Each cutter takes two powered watches in one of two berths. Choose the standard hull or spend 3 Alloy to start with a reinforced escort. Muster its crew at the launch berth with the listed supplies.

After a duo establishes its local base, open **Ship systems** at its home port. Fund the base project with Alloy, then use the normal watch to advance construction. The base report follows the actual funding, completion, or supply state. Once operating, the project produces at its port. Navigation shows the next jump cost, port transfers, the net output per watch, and provision upkeep. You can advance a supply watch at the dock. Load a standard shipment or take a bulk transfer up to cutter capacity while protecting the port’s reserve.

Each of the 25 expeditions takes two Umas, two Generals and six lieutenants. Any ready duo can be the first to cross the Gate. The game records that crossing for later voyages. Its bridge operations use the small ship’s cargo. After the first approach, the crew can go off duty during a safe pause. A supply report appears when the next ship operation needs more than the voyage has aboard. Between sorties, withdraw to the launch berth for one fuel. There you can resupply, reinforce the hull or repair damage, then resume the same route. A cutter at a safe berth can be parked so you can return to it later. Before a ship sortie, choose among the ten expedition members for the four bridge stations. Assigning a specialist to a matching station gives a second drill rank. Choose **Deploy all ten** to take the full crew into tactics. Follow the objective shown in each battle. Keep the named Herald or Echo alive in the final ground operation to unlock the release scene. Finish the return to restore access to the Homestretch and unload any remaining supplies.

| Screen or entry | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Opening and roster | Meet the Captain's crew, choose from all 50 recruitment leads and see their next personal chapter. A single Uma can complete her own conflict. Pick additional crew at any time to support the next mission. |
| Homestretch | Assign eligible crew to duty, repairs, engineering, mining or resource operations, crafting and ship stations. Check cargo and the next heading. Use quarters and independent relationships alongside these activities. |
| Personal arc and rehabilitation | Run the Uma's ship and ground encounters, capture her General and attend rehabilitation with both actors. Move her Boss Ally into the shared quarters and open her own relationship scenes when appropriate. |
| Local bases and capitals | See which same-system Uma is her fixed partner, investigate together, see who the lieutenants are and what remains for their return. Restore services and liberate five capitals. |
| Locating the next operation | A readiness view shows the five capital outcomes and each duo's six individual lieutenant statuses. A ready duo can embark while other duo stories remain open. |
| Expedition ship | Allocate supplies and take two Umas, two cleared Boss Allies and six lieutenants on their own small ship. Play its actual bridge missions. All ten deploy together in Act III battles against the Seraine. See the target's liberation, outcome report and return. |
| Finale and return | Free a witness from each Seraine court and return to the main ship. Follow the readiness list to prepare the finale. After reaching Lark, reopen travel for civic visits, relationship scenes and repairs. |

## The finale and return to play

The five liberated capitals and five recorded witness releases open the finale. At Halcyon, choose an eligible ten-person formation. Secure the civilian nodes, commit the common cut, and hear your recorded witnesses. The outer Queen operations can be played in any order. Follow the objective and keep the named Seraine alive.

The last approach uses the bridge assignments and the chosen formation's cutter in a station campaign. Choose who will speak at the berth before deploying the formation. The ground battle requires separating the witness channels before breaking the Collar. Both named Seraine must survive. The briefing and objective list track which actions are available. Withdraw and retry from the crew report if the attempt fails.

Both approaches to Lark lead to the shared berth. Supper and the folded request reopen travel. The ordinary ending is available there. Recorded mutual relationship choices can open the True Ending. A private reunion or shared visit has its own choices and can be revisited if deferred. Civic events appear where the relevant officers are available. At a repair site, survey first, then complete the two work stages. The project remembers its stage if you leave and return.

Training is optional after signing on. Gear, replays, and other established game systems are described in the [manual](README.md).
